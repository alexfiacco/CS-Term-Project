Blend · 0 of 6 · the rename

A taste match between two people is now a Blend

The owner's call: the person-to-person match is called a Blend everywhere (Instagram uses the word too, so it's familiar). Films keep "match": "89% match" on a film stays. Checking someone's Blend stays a free, instant lookup, and this page is that lookup, renamed and otherwise unchanged. What follows in frames 1 to 6 is the opt-in part: you keep a Blend, and it becomes a living list for the two of you. Marked strings: red is today's wording, blue is the new one.
