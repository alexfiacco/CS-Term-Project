Why lines: templates, not the model

Zero AI cost. Each line is one template filled from the two people's nine strands (tasteDnaV2.strands), the film's labels (film_labels: tension, violence, propulsion…), the watchlists and the services. The voice is the match screen's own shapesMeet: "Both of you lean…", "You split on…". The most specific template that applies wins.

SHARED: both rank strand S in their top 3, and the film is high on S
"Both of you lean slow burn, and this one takes its time to tighten."
strand rank (both) + film's label for S over 70
BRIDGE: your top strand A, their top strand B, film high on both
"Your complexity, Jo's intensity: it has plenty of both."
each person's #1 strand + film labels for A and B
SOFTEN: they rank S far higher; the film is high on S's gentle labels, low on its harsh ones
"Jo's strongest pull is intensity; this keeps it to dread, never gore."
tension and scariness high, violence low
SPLIT: shared strand, but the film's pace sits on one side
"You both go for visual craft; Jo will want more pace than this gives."
propulsion vs. the person's slow-burn rank
SAVED / NEW: savedBy both, or released in the last 60 days
"On both your watchlists since spring. Tonight's the night."
the movie night's savedBy (rule 880), the release date

Each template has 3 or 4 phrasings, chosen by a hash of the film and the pair, so twelve rows never repeat a sentence shape, and a pair always gets the same line for the same film. It lives beside lib/pick-reason.ts (groupPickReason is already templated this way) and is checked by a test over every strand pair.

Honest notes

  • Templated lines will feel samey by the third week. Variants help, but they're no match for a written line. The fallback, if people notice, is to cache one model line per film per pair, which costs money only for kept pairs.
  • "You 91 · Jo 88" shows Jo's predicted score to you. It's already shown on today's page (rule 179); keeping a Blend doesn't widen it.

Recommendation

Make the kept Blend the same page, list first, rather than a new screen. One place per pair, and the free lookup and the kept list can never disagree.
