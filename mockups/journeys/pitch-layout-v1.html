Pitch ask: options first, and the ideas explained

Three changes to the Pitch ask. The option chips move above the box, inside the panel, so the panel reads top to bottom as "narrow it, describe it, Action!". "From the writers' room" gets one line saying what the cards are and what a tap does. The first-time line takes the subtitle's place on the first open, instead of sitting under the panel, where it is read after you've already started typing. The tick under "Set the scene" stays. Drawn as a signed-out first open on iPhone 390 (no poster wall, no Previous takes, no Saved cuts). Blue dashed: what changed.

Now · first open, build 28

Recut.swift ask: the box first, the kinds row under it, Action!, the free line; then the first-time line under the panel, which repeats the subtitle above it. The writers' room heading has nothing to say what the cards are.

Now · two options picked

The picked chips sit between the box and the kinds row, so the box and the row it opens from are apart, and every pick pushes the kinds row and Action! down (as in built-pitch-options-ask).

Proposed · first open

Kinds row first, then the box with its mic, Action! and the free line, 12 apart. The first-time line replaces the subtitle (once); the writers' room line sits 4 under its heading, the cards 12 under that.

Proposed · two options picked

Picks land under the kinds row they came from, above the box: the row never moves, and the box reads as "plus anything you want to add". Later opens show the usual subtitle.

Recommendation

Options above the box. Putting them first tells you that you don't have to write a sentence: a guest can tap Genre, Mood and Action! without typing. The kinds row stays in one place, and picks build up between it and the box, like a filter bar above a search. The row still scrolls sideways past the panel's edge (Decade, Length, Language off screen), as today.

Writers' room line: Ideas to start from. Tap one to try it. Sans 13 muted, 4 under the heading. It's true to what the code does: a tap runs the pitch straight away (model.run(s)). For a guest that uses their one free pitch, so try it is the honest verb. Tap one to pitch it. is the alternative if "try" sounds too casual.

The first-time line

Today two lines say the same thing: the subtitle (Describe a mood or a film, and we'll find the films.) and, under the panel, the first-time line (Pitch is where you describe the film you want, in your own words, and get a short list that fits.). Proposed: on the first open the first-time line takes the subtitle's place, in the subtitle's style (sans 15 muted, two lines), and from then on the subtitle is back. You read it before the panel, never twice. The page grows by one line, once.

If rule 1335's term lines must stay in their own small style under what they explain, the second best spot is directly under the subtitle (sans 13, 8 under it), still above the panel.
