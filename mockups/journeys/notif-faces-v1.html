Notifications with the sender's face

iOS Communication Notifications, as iMessage draws them: a message, a group line, a movie night invite, a pitch or a cut, and a Watched with tag lead with the person's photo instead of the app icon. Drawn in the approved notif-ask-v4 look (the owner's reference beside it): newest first, iOS's times ("now", "2m ago", "9:41 AM"), one stack per conversation, the bold title and the time on line 1, no app-name line, liquid glass. The circle is iOS's, not ours: a communication avatar is always drawn as a circle with the app icon at its lower right corner; that is system UI we cannot restyle, so the rounded-square faces stay inside the app. Emma and Sam's faces are drawn stand-ins for their photos.

A circle, the app icon at its corner

On a Communication Notification iOS crops the sender's image to a circle and pins the app icon to its lower right. We hand iOS a photo; it decides the shape, the size, the badge and the stack count. Our rounded squares (12pt corners) cannot be kept here.

What changes in the words

  • iOS writes the sender's name on line 1 of a one-to-one notification, so today's titles that start with the name ("Sam sent you a pitch") would read "Sam / Sam sent you a pitch". The words after the name move to line 2, starting with the verb: "Sent you a pitch", "Sent you their cut", "Invited you to a movie night", "Watched Past Lives with you".
  • A plain message keeps today's copy exactly: "Emma" / the text; in a group, the group's name / "Sam: the text".
  • Watched with does not push today; it is drawn here as a new push in the People switch.

Stacks, by conversation

  • Every message gets the conversation's thread id, so iOS stacks a chat's messages under its newest, as iMessage does, with the count on the face.
  • That replaces today's collapse for chats ("Friday crew · 3 new messages" updated in place): with faces, the newest message and who wrote it is the better line. The daily limit and quiet hours are unchanged.
  • Pushes with no person (How was it, a release, the films are up) keep the full app icon.

To build

  • A Notification Service Extension and the Communication Notifications capability; the extension builds an INSendMessageIntent with the sender (an INPerson with their image) and, in a group, the group's name and members, donates it, and updates the content.
  • The server adds mutable-content, the sender's id, photo URL, tint and the thread id to the push.
  • No photo or a slow download: the extension draws the initial in their tint so the face is never blank. Older iOS shows the app icon as today.
