Your year in film: the share card, three better ones

The owner: "Year in film looks solid but share card ugly." The share card is the last of the year cards ("That was 2026", lib/year-card.tsx case "share"): a sentence, the archetype, five small posters, "Blend with me" and a URL pill, all small type floating on black with the red spine. It is the one card people post, so it needs to read at thumbnail size in a story tray. Three directions, each as a story (9:16, 1080×1920) and a post (1:1, 1080×1080), all in the Montage palette: ink, cream, blue; red only as one small mark. Posters are the year's real logs and run edge to edge.

Open for you

Pick a direction: A Poster wall (most striking in a story tray, and unique per person), B Ticket stub (the most designed, and "a ticket to your year" is a strong share line), C The 2026 issue (calmest, cream stands out in a feed of dark cards, shows the most information). My pick: A for the story and C for the post, or A for both if you want one look; B as the alternative if you'd like something more playful. "Blend with me": dropped from the card face; the link (montagefilm.app/m/alexfiacco) still opens your profile with the compare button, and the share sheet's text carries the invite. Which posters on the wall: every film you logged this year, newest first (shown), or your highest-rated first. Few films (under 12): A falls back to C's layout so the wall never repeats posters.
