b114 · App icon: the tick as film

Concepts for the app icon only, nothing in the app changes. Every concept keeps the accent tick's two flat colours (#c24c56 red | #5a86bd blue, hard split at the middle, round ends) on the app's ground #0b0b0d. Cream in 4, 5, 7 is today's middle card (#c4c0b8). Each shown at 1024 in the iOS rounded-square mask, then 180, 120 and 60 (CSS px, rendered 2x). Note: the brand tick in code (AccentTick, .rdu::after) is a straight two-tone bar, 24 × 3, not a check mark, so "two strokes" here means its red half and blue half.
