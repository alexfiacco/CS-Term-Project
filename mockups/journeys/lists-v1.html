Lists, the basic version

Films you group yourself, in your own order: a mood, a season, a dare, a friend. Every part is one the app already has: the hold drawer gains "Add to list…", a drawer of your lists toggles the film in or out, a list is a page with the profile's poster grid or the Canon's ranked rows, and the profile gets a Lists tab. Someone else's list shows your match on every poster and how many you've seen; it can be sent, saved to your lists or reported.
