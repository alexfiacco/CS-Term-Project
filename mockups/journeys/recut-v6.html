Recut v6: one short word you'd say out loud

The owner on v5: the industry names are "too try hard"; wanted, a catchy word that makes people go "oh, that's cool, good name". The rules: one word, ideally 4 to 6 letters, easy to say, works as a verb and a noun, not jargon, and not a word the app already uses (Montage, Blend, Tonight, Cast, Take, Double Feature, Picks, Mood). Every candidate is said aloud three ways, then the best six go on the Browse button.

Recommendation

Final pick: Pitch. It is the only word that passes all three tests without a nudge. "Pitch me a film" is ordinary English, and it is exactly what the feature does: you give a one-line pitch ("Alien, but funny"), it pitches films back. "3 free pitches left" counts cleanly, and "@alex's pitch" is the best share line on the board, because the thing being shared really is a pitch. A movie word everyone already knows, with no jargon and no glitter. Five letters, fits beside search on the smallest phone.

Runner-up: Cue. Three letters and very cool on the button, and "Cue up something tense" is natural. As a counted noun ("3 free cues left") it goes a little abstract.

Third: Hunch. The warmest and most human ("Got a hunch?" in the empty field). Noun only, so the button reads "Hunch" but you can't "hunch me a film".

Honest about Recut: it has the best family resemblance to Montage, but it means re-editing something that exists, so "Recut me a film" sounds like it will change a film rather than find one. It passes as a noun and fails as the button verb, which is the one place everyone sees it.

Ruled out quickly: Reel (Instagram owns it), Spin and Wish (casino and genie energy next to "free"), Spark and Vibe (read as AI in 2026), Cut (Montage and Recut already own it), Lens and Blink (brands), Mood (already the Browse chips and Movie Night).
