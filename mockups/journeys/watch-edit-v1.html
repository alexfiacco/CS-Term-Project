Your watches: edit, delete, and a take for each watch

The owner's iPhone shows "Your watches 2" with two rows (Nov 5 2026 with a friend, Seen; Jun 25 2024 on your own, Seen) and no way to change or remove one. Today the web can remove a watch with a ✕, but only in See all, with no confirm, and it leaves the film's date and any friend tag behind; the iPhone can't remove one at all. This mock adds: tap a watch to edit it (date, who you were with, its take) or delete it; a plain confirm that keeps your rating and Seen; the take prompt after + Log a watch (the bug: it never asks today); and one take per watch, folded in Discussion. Drawn at 390pt from FilmScreen / WatchedWhen.swift and FilmActions / WatchedWith.tsx on origin/main (e05beca4), with Theme values. People are anonymised (@sam_r, @mika, @kofi).

The one red

The edit drawer's red is already the dash before its kicker (drawers-v1 rule), so Delete this watch is a plain text button. The confirm is its own plain drawer (no kicker, no dash, as drawers-v1 says for confirms), so there the Delete watch button is the one red. The film page under it is dimmed.

The take prompt bug (owner: "I wasn't prompted to create a take")

By design, but the design has a hole. Both apps show the take only after the first log: web FilmActions.logWhen sets takeFor only when ask === "first" (and the who-step's done only when w.first); iPhone WatchedWhenSheet does guard first else { onCancel() }. take-prompt B said "Log a watch stays step 1 only". It never checks whether you have a take, so if you skipped it on the first log (or the film came from Letterboxd, where there is no first log), + Log a watch never asks again. Fix: after + Log a watch (and after Watched with, if shown), always go on to the take step for this watch, with Skip. Headline: "What made it land?" family as today when you have no take; "Anything different this time?" with your last take shown small above the box when you do.

Data plan

A watch is a row in user_library_events with watch set (today / earlier / rewatch), its watched_on date (null for "A while ago": 387 of 456 rows) and the rating at the time. The film's own row, user_ratings, holds the rating, Seen, and one date (the newest, plus watch, is_rewatch). A friend tag is an activity_feed row (watched_with / _added / _declined / _joined) joined to the watch by date in ref_name. Letterboxd watches are one profile_films row per film (a count and the last date), no row per watch.

One take per watch migration: watch-takes-v1.sql
Linkfilm_takes.watch_id bigint, FK to user_library_events(id) on delete set null, plus watched_on date copied at post (the label survives an unlink). Drop UNIQUE (film_slug, author_id); add a partial unique on watch_id (one take per watch). No unique on the unlinked ones: a set-null delete must never fail. Backfill links a take to its author's newest watch on or before it; on today's data that is none of the 7 (all on Letterboxd or undated films), so they stay unlinked, film-level takes, shown with their post date.

Edge cases

  • One watch, no list. "Watched once · Jun 25, 2024" becomes tappable and opens the same drawer.
  • "A while ago" watches (no date): the drawer opens on "A while ago"; picking a date dates it. Two undated watches show as "Earlier" rows, each editable.
  • Date in the future or before 1900: refused by cleanWatchDate; the picker stops at today.
  • Two watches on one day: allowed; tags join by date, so both rows show the same friends. Changing one row's date asks whether @sam_r was at this one.
  • Before an "unseen" reset: those events already don't show; never editable.
  • A tag you accepted from a friend (their night): you can delete your watch; their tag and watch stay. Changing its date unlinks you from their night, said in the drawer.
  • Friend no longer a friend or blocked: already hidden from the row; removing them is still allowed, adding is not.
  • Imported plus logged on the same day: one row (the logged one wins), as the year review counts it.
  • Deleting the newest watch moves "Watched once · date", the profile diary and the goal to the next newest.
  • Undo: the toast "Watch deleted · Undo" puts the same row back (same id) with its tags, within the toast's life; the confirm stays because tags tell other people.
  • Take on a deleted watch: stays in Discussion with its date; it can be relinked by editing it from Discussion (pick a watch).
  • Offline or failed save: the row goes back, toast "Couldn't save. Try again".
  • Guest (no account): no tags and no takes; date and delete still work on their owner_key.
  • Spam of takes: one per watch, and watches need a date in the past, so it is bounded by honest logs.
