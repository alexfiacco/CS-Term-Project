Claw and shutter, less glitchy: three tuned cuts

The owner picked v2's variant 3, Claw and shutter, and asked for it a little less glitchy. It is on the left as it was, so you can replay it beside the three new cuts. All four keep the character: the film is pulled down one frame at a time by the claw, then held still in the gate. What changes is how hard each pull is, how much the card dims while it moves, how fast it runs and how it stops.

What the glitch was, measured: v2 pulled each frame in 0.12s on a cubic curve, so at the middle of a pull the film moved at 25 frames a second, then stopped dead; the card dimmed 13 percent for each pull, 2.4 times a second, which read as flicker; and the run ended by slamming into its last frame. What all three new cuts do: a sine ease in and out over 0.18 to 0.22s (peak speed 7 to 9 frames a second, a third of v2's); a dim of 3 percent or less that follows the film's speed, so it swells and fades with each pull instead of blinking; a top speed of 1.6 to 2 pulls a second; the first pulls come slower and a little softer; and on the picks' arrival a pull in progress hands over to a damped settle onto its frame. The sprocket holes and frame lines still move as one strip, and the side cards never move.

Unchanged from v2: the fast path (picks ready by the time the fan opens; no film), the film starting at 1.6s, the light fading out on the stop so the resting card is the FanGlyph exactly, and the 0.2s pause after the picks arrive (5.0s here) before the fan flies to the header.

The traces under each phone are the slow path drawn out: the top row is the film's position in frames from 1.4s to 6.0s (each step is one pull; the softer the corners, the softer the pull), the lower row is the shutter dim on one shared scale, so v2's 13 percent blinks sit next to the new cuts' 2 and 3 percent swells. Hover a trace to read it. Reduce Motion, in every cut: no rise, no spring, no film, no light, no dim. The lockup is still, fades in over 250ms and out over 300ms when Picks is ready (0.6s at the earliest), exactly as live today. Use the toggle above.

The recommended cut as the app's loaders

Cut 2, Soft claw, as MontageLoader (iPhone Design/Loading.swift, web MontageLoader.tsx), in place of today's coin turn: the fan's centre card pulls its film on the same curve, the same dim and the same spin up as the launch, and loops at the launch's top speed for as long as the wait lasts. The streak strip, for rows and gaps, takes the same claw sideways: each pull moves it one frame to the left in 0.2s, then it holds; its holes and frames move as one. Pitch gets a loader for the first time: today on the web, after "Pitch it", the cream button says "Pitching…" and nothing else moves for 3 to 6 seconds. Two placements are below, (a) and (b).

Pitch: (a) is the one. The wait is 3 to 6 seconds, long enough that people wonder whether anything is happening. (a) puts the loader where the answer will appear, so the eye is already in the right place when the results land, and the caption says what the work is in plain words. (b) is tidy and keeps Or try in reach, but in a disabled button a 30pt strip reads as a small busy badge, the word "Pitching" is gone, and the space under the button stays dead for the whole wait. (b) is a good fit for short waits inside a button elsewhere; for Pitch, use (a).

At real size, looping

Reduce Motion for the in-app loaders: the film does not pull and the card does not dim; the fan or strip holds still and breathes in opacity (0.55 to 1, 2.6s), as MontageLoader does today, and the words swap without the fade.
