Letterboxd lists and reviews, brought in

Today an import reads ratings, watched, diary, likes and the watchlist, and throws the rest of the export away: lists/*.csv is never opened and reviews.csv goes up with its text cut out. This keeps both. A list becomes a Montage list (lists-v1/v2), Friends only until you say otherwise. A review becomes your take on that film, seen by nobody but you until you say otherwise. One import summary says what came in and holds the two switches; Settings gets a row for people who imported before this existed. @alexfiacco and @noorh are stand-in accounts; list names, notes and review text are invented.
