Coverage (films table, read-only)

Backdrops: 68,488 of 73,001 films (94%); all of the 1,000 most-watched, 97.6% of the top 10,000. 4,294 films have a poster but no backdrop: they fall back to Now's poster face.

The table stores w780 backdrops. Cropped to a phone, about 225 source pixels stretch across 780 screen pixels (3.5x): blurry. w1280 is 2.1x, original 0.7x. Building C/D means asking TMDB for original (or w1280 at least) on Picks.
