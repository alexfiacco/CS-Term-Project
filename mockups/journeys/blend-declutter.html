Honest notes

  • The title bug isn't the Drawer. .fit and DrawerScroll are fine; the Blend page opens as a system sheet (PageStack, ProfileScreen, MontageApp) with its header inside the scroll. The fix is local to TasteMatchScreen.
  • Dropping the switch loses something: seeing Sam's shape alone when the two overlap heavily. That shape is one tap away on Sam's profile, and on a Blend the overlap is the answer. If owners miss it, tapping a legend name to fade the other shape is a cheap way back, without a control.
  • Keep the middle ring, unlabelled. Each shape is centred on its own middle, so without the ring "further out" has no reference. Faint, and explained once in the drawer, not in the legend.
  • The takeaway needs a fallback when nothing is shared: "You split on Intensity: Sam's strongest, your #6" (blend-start already writes it that way).

Recommendation

Ship all of it together; every piece is S and none needs new data. Order, not joined: pinned header · hero (faces, %, band) · Invite Sam to Blend + Share icon · Your Film DNA, together (takeaway, radar, two-name legend, Read each strand) · Films you both love · picks each way · the folds. Joined: pinned header with the hero row · Start a movie night + bell · the list · DNA and films you both love as folds · the rest. That takes the DNA block from nine elements and 27 label fragments to five elements and nine words, and the page reads top-down as: who, how close, what to do, why.
