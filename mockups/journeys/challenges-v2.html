Weekly taste quests

Second pass on challenges. One card a week instead of three rows: a featured quest and two small ones. Each quest says what it sharpens and shows it as a before and after bar, starts with one tap, and counts progress in dots. The reward is the change you can see in your own profile.

What the first draft got wrong

  • It read as a to-do list. Three equal rows with icons and buttons, all the same weight. Nothing stood out.
  • The titles named the task, not the payoff. "Rate 3 films from the 1970s" is a chore. Why it mattered was in grey text underneath.
  • The reward came after, as text. Nothing showed it before you started.
  • Too much text: about 60 words of instructions in a drawer that's already long.

What v2 does

  • One hero quest, named like a programme ("Three slow burners"), with two small ones beside it.
  • The payoff is the subtitle and a bar. Cream is what we know now. Hatched blue is what this quest adds.
  • One button, Start, which opens the existing rate deck on that quest's films.
  • About 20 words on the whole card. No XP, no badges, no emoji.

Where it lives

  • The streak drawer is home. Quests are weekly, the drawer is the weekly screen, and the open-week line already asks you to act by Sunday.
  • A slim Picks row is the doorway, because the drawer only opens from a run of 2 or more. It only appears when no other banner is due.
  • No Profile card. The profile was just decluttered, and it already opens the drawer from the streak line. The payoff shows up in Film DNA without one.

Should a quest count the week?

  • Yes, any finished quest counts, and the slot shows it with a blue ring. Each quest is at least 3 ratings or 1 logged watch, about the effort of logging a film.
  • It keeps busy weeks alive. A week with no time for a film can still be a taste week, so the streak breaks less often for people who are still engaged.
  • Guardrail: a quest counts only the week it was done in, never a past week, and rating the same film again counts for nothing. What counts gets one more line: "Finishing a weekly quest".
