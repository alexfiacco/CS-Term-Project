The owner: "ours looks solid, but maybe can use some inspiration." Three upgrades that stay Montage (no map, no postmark, no borrowed fonts or badges), each usable on every card in part 2. Shown before and after on the archetype card and the Blend.
Three upgrades

1 · One hero, set huge

Every card leads with one fact in Bodoni as big as it fits: the archetype's name (already), the Blend's 94% at about 40% of the card's width, a night's film title, a list's name. One thing to read from across a feed; everything else steps back. Only true numbers, never ranks.

2 · The ticket stub

Montage's own mark at the foot: an outlined ticket stub with the fan, the card's question on the left and, past a perforation, the @handle, the date and the address. It reuses the movie night card's stub (rule 1914) and the app's ticket cards, so it reads as ours. Outlined in the ink at 55%, never cream, so a card has no second "button".

3 · Their film as the backdrop

The dim four-column poster wall becomes one full-bleed still from their #1 film (a TMDB backdrop, the house grade baked in), fading to the ink by the middle. Richer and personal: your Inception, not a grid of anything. The five posters stay as the proof. (Poster crops stand in for backdrops in this mock.)
