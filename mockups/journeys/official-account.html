Changes and effort

  • In all, about S to build (3 or 4 days)

Honest notes

  • Suggestions depend on there being public profiles worth following. At today's size the top matches may be in the 60s, or the same few people for everyone. Show only those above about 65%, and fewer rows rather than weak ones.
  • An official account that goes quiet makes the app look abandoned. Only launch it with a month of posts queued.
  • Don't auto-follow. Following the team account by default would inflate its count and read as spam.

Recommendation

Build it first. It's the cheapest item here and it fixes a real hole: a new user's Social is empty. The suggestion rows already exist one tap away. Moving them into the empty state is a few days' work. Make the official account a separate yes or no, depending on whether someone will post every week.
