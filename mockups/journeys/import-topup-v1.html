The deck

Logged films first, then popular

  • From your Letterboxd on every logged-but-unrated card, with the log date from watched.csv ("Logged March 2021"). The only answers are the stars and "Don't remember it": they have seen it, so no "Haven't seen it".
  • Order: most recently logged first (fresh memory is the fastest tap), widely seen films first among a day's logs. Speed is the goal here, not informativeness: under 15 rule 853 keeps taste out of the deck, so no "why this one" line.
  • Then popular: after 3 "Don't remember it" in a row, or when logged films run out, the taste test's own deck follows, with its usual buttons. Most of the 175 here never get reached.
  • The deck is the taste test's (web TasteTest, iPhone TasteDeckScreen), seeded from the import's importSeed as today; the seed's seen list gains the log date.
