Confirms are plain drawers (no dash), so the destructive button is the one red: Delete this watch with other watches left; Delete watch and rating on the only one, where Delete watch only is the cream button.

Only watch: which is the default

Delete watch only (keep 4★) is the cream, top button. Why: (1) it is the smaller loss and the one you can't regret: the 4★ stays and a new watch can be logged any time, while removing the rating clears the prediction it fed, its place in your year and streak, and Seen; (2) the usual reason to delete a lone watch is a wrong or duplicate log, not "I never saw it"; (3) one red per screen puts red on the destructive one, and a red primary on top would make the bigger loss the easy tap. Delete watch and rating is right under it, full width, named plainly, one tap.

The take prompt bug (unchanged from v1)

The take step shows only after the first log (web FilmActions.logWhen, iPhone WatchedWhenSheet), so a skipped or imported first watch never asks again. Fix: after + Log a watch, always go on to the take step for this watch, with Skip.

Data plan

A watch is a user_library_events row with watch set, its watched_on and the rating at the time. The film's own row, user_ratings, holds the rating, Seen and the newest date. Friend tags are activity_feed rows joined by date. Letterboxd watches are one profile_films row per film. Only the rows marked v2 changed since v1.

Edge cases

  • One watch, no list. "Watched once · Jun 25, 2024" ends in the same pencil (drawn under frame 1).
  • Only watch, with a take: either choice keeps the take in Discussion, unlinked, under its date. The confirm says so.
  • Only watch, Letterboxd count too: deleting the in-app watch can't remove the film (the import still says Seen); the confirm shows only Delete this watch.
  • Only watch, tagged by a friend: both choices leave their watch; the body names them.
  • Delete watch and rating, then Undo: the toast's Undo puts the watch and the rating back (re-rate with the same stars, prediction rebuilt), within the toast's life.
  • "A while ago" watches (no date): the pencil opens on "A while ago"; picking a date dates it.
  • Two watches on one day: allowed; each row its own pencil and take.
  • Two takes, one person, in Top: the soft cap; in New they sit where their dates put them, often far apart.
  • Take on a deleted watch: labelled with its copied watched_on; edit from Discussion to relink.
  • Offline or failed save: the row goes back, toast "Couldn't save. Try again".
  • Guest: no tags, no takes; date and delete work on their owner_key.
