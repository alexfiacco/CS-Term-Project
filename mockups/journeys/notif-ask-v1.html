The owner asked "shouldn't we have the notifications at the end of the tour?". Yes, as a soft ask: our own card says what Montage will send, and only Turn on shows iOS's alert. Maybe later spends nothing, so the Tonight drawer that 0a18a707 (rule 1142) added on the first Picks visit stays as the second chance a minute later. Whichever Turn on or Remind me comes first shows the real prompt; it never shows twice. 390pt, the app's tokens, Bodoni roman, cream on ink, one red accent per screen (the C1 dash under the label, rule 1127). Remind me just confirms. The drawer has no small line (allowed: tonightNote is nil). The tap schedules the 10:00 reminder, closes the drawer, and a toast says "We'll ask tomorrow at 10:00." No prompt.

3c · Don't Allow at iOS

Email instead, never a nag. The ask becomes the morning-after email the server already sends ("Did you end up watching Prisoners?"), and Remind me reads Email me. "Turn on in Settings" is a quiet link to iOS Settings. It shows only in this drawer: no alert, no banner, no second ask.

5 · What changes against 0a18a707
iPhone app

The card back, the drawer told what iOS knows

  • WelcomeTour: the .notify card back as card 6, only while authorizationStatus == .notDetermined (the old undecided()). Last card: Maybe later / Turn on; See my picks stays on card 5 when it is left out. TOUR_VERSION stays 8, so people who finished the tour are not shown it again; the drawer is their ask.
  • Art: notifyArt with three banners (invite, pitch, the morning ask with their first pick and its %, without the % under 15 rated).

  • ThreeForTonight: allowed, the toast "We'll ask tomorrow at 10:00." (today the drawer just closes). Denied, or Your films off: the Bodoni line goes to the email wording, the button to Email me, and the note to "Notifications are off for Montage. Turn on in Settings", opening openNotificationSettingsURLString. Today it says "Picks will ask instead" and still reads Remind me.
Server

One ask per morning, by the right channel

  • Double ask today: on an allowed iPhone, Remind me schedules the 10:00 push and also writes tonight_shown, so the 16:00 UTC email (/api/cron/tonight-followup) asks again if the push was not answered.
  • Change: POST /api/tonight/shown takes remind: "push" | "email", stored on tonight_shown (a small migration). The cron skips push rows; 3c sends email.
  • 3c only when email can go: the drawer reads emailPrefs followup (and an address). Off: Email me is left out, and the drawer is the film and Not now, as Tonight.
  • Copy, optional: a pitch push reads "Sam" / "Sam sent a pitch" (pushForGroupMessage, rule 1140). The card shows "Sam sent you a pitch" / "The Wailing", so naming the film needs the pitch title passed in.
  • No new push kinds. Invites, pitches and the morning ask all exist.
