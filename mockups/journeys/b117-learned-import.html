Why the drawer over Screen DNA

The summary says "Lately". Its proof is a handful of recent films, not the strands of an all-time profile, so Screen DNA answers a different question. The drawer shows exactly what the sentence was read from, and someone who disagrees can see which rating pulled it ("oh, Baby Driver").

Needs

GET /api/learned already builds the window to write the summary (lib/learned.ts). Returning its slugs, stars, heart and date with page is database work only, no model call. One drawer on web (BottomDrawer) and iPhone (.drawer, height .fraction(1)).

The simpler alternative

Keep the link and just open Screen DNA, as rule 1780 already says, fixed on iPhone (dismiss the menu, then open the profile on its Taste tab once it is gone, or push the profile inside the menu's stack). Cheaper, but it lands on an all-time read that may not mention adventure or action at all.

Either way
