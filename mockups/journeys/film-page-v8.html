Recommendation

1. Where to watch: option b, list rows on the text line. The logo starts at the page's 16pt gutter, under the section label, and the name and the kind read as one block (name, then "Stream · one of yours" or "Rent or buy" in grey under it). Nothing is pushed to the right edge and there is no boxed panel or hairlines, so it lines up with the stars, the cards' text and Discussion. It keeps what the tiles lose: which service is which (HBO Max and its Amazon Channel are the same logo) and whether it's free with your plan or costs money. Option a is nicer to look at and saves 60pt, but a row of logos only works once you know every logo. It could suit a small space later, like the Tonight card. The "Streaming on Max, one of yours" line goes: the tick and "one of yours" on the row say the same thing.
2. Watch with, inside People you follow. Same data and same action as today's Watch together card: the top friend you follow who hasn't seen it and has a 70%+ Blend with you (lib/film-people.ts watchWith). On iPhone, Movie night starts the room with them seated and the film as the first pick (POST /api/theater "Movie Night", seedSlug). On the web, with more than one such friend it opens the "Who's watching The Dark Knight with you?" picker. The row sits on a slightly lighter strip, not under a line. If nobody you follow has seen it but there's someone to watch with, the card shows only this row. If there's no one to watch with, the card ends at "@jules loved it".
3. Removed: "You and @jules: 3 nights together · 81% Blend" under Your watches. Your watches ends at the list and "See all". Kept as live: the rating block, the People you follow card, the Discussion card (top take, Friends / Everyone in the thread, Add your take).
