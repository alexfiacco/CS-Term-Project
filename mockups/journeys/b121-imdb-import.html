Build note for the owner

The file. IMDb's ratings export is one CSV. Columns used: Const (the tt id), Your Rating (1 to 10), Date Rated (YYYY-MM-DD), Title, Year, Title Type. Others (URL, IMDb Rating, Genres, Directors, Num Votes) are ignored. The watchlist export has the same layout without Your Rating. Tell the files apart by their header row, not their names, and check the header spelling against a fresh export before building (IMDb changed the export in 2024; not checked here).

Matching. Match Const to films.imdb_id. Measured today: imdb_id is filled on 52,767 of 73,600 films, but on only 9,855 of 29,141 films with 1,000+ Letterboxd watches (34%), and 2,943 of 3,829 with 100,000+ (77%). tmdb_id is on 29,015 of those 29,141. So imdb_id alone would miss most of a normal library. Before launch, backfill imdb_id from TMDB's external ids (free, no model call; one request per film), and at import time fall back to TMDB /find/{tt} then films.tmdb_id for any id still missing. Why imdb_id is so thin was not investigated.

Skip TV for now. Keep Title Type Movie, TV Movie, Short and Video; leave out TV Series, TV Mini Series, TV Episode, TV Special, Video Game and Podcast Episode, and say how many were left out (3b).

Stars. Halve: 10 → 5★, 9 → 4.5★, 8 → 4★ ... 1 → 0.5★. It lands exactly on Letterboxd's half-star scale, so nothing is rounded. No likes or hearts come in (IMDb has none). Not measured: IMDb raters may use the scale differently (a 7 there may mean "liked"), and likeWeight counts only over 3★.

Dates. Date Rated is a rating date, not a watch date. Store it as such with source "imdb". A first-day rating spree (hundreds of films on one day) must not count as recent watching: leave days with more than about 20 ratings out of latelyWindow, streaks and "busiest year". Import facts on the wait say "busiest year for rating", never "watching".

Both imports. Same film in both: the Letterboxd rating wins, silently, and the done screen says how many (5d). IMDb only adds films Letterboxd doesn't have. I recommend this over asking per film: a list of 200 conflicts is a chore, and Letterboxd has the diary and the person's own half stars. Montage's own in-app ratings keep today's rule (the newer rating wins).

Unmatched. Keep the unmatched rows (title, year, tt id, rating) on the import job so "See them" can list them; nothing else is stored from the file.

Rules to check. An IMDb import must count as an import for publishing (invariants 1, 2, 33) but not as a "Letterboxd library" (invariant 4, rule 168): no daily sync, no Letterboxd re-import path. iPhone and web in the same task (iOS parity).
