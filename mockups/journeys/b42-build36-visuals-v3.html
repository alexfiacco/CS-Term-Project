How the read is written. Per person and strand: their favorites (top third of their own ratings) strong on the strand, against the strand's typical films (the strong films in the candidate pool), compared on film_labels (warmth, humor, heaviness, tension, wonder, formula and the rest) and films.character_traits (Ensemble cast, Found family, Underdog story...). The one to three that differ most, each with enough favorites behind it, go to the fast model with nothing else; it writes one plain sentence from only those, and the sentence is checked (no film names, no stars, no em dashes, nothing outside the traits given). If it fails, a fixed sentence from the top trait stands in; if support is too thin, there is no read. Cached per person and strand for the week, with the values. For a strand you lean away from, the same comparison runs on your favorites low on it. The two reads above are hand-written to this rule for the mock, not generated from an account.
