Friday: one push, only if something changed

Lists change all week, quietly, when something real happens. Friday is when people plan movie night, so that's the one day a kept Blend speaks up: one push at most, bundled across every Blend you keep, and only for Blends that gained picks since last Friday. Nothing new means no push. In the app, the refreshed Blends sit on Social's People tab, beside your people and the movie night ticket, where the plan gets made.

Honest notes

  • A capped push competes with this week's pick and release reminders for the 3 a day. On a busy Friday, Blend can lose. That's acceptable: it's the one most likely to be timely, so send it first in the day's order.
  • Fridays only works for people whose planning day is Friday. Weekend-only households may never see it at the right time. Watch opens by weekday before adding a setting.
