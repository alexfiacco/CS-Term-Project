The pattern: a plain label, then "What's this?"

Nothing is deleted. Montage's named concepts keep their names, get a plain label beside them the first time they appear, and an ⓘ that opens one short drawer. Every drawer reads from one glossary, so "Blend" is explained in the same words on Social, profile, Pitch and movie night. Today's TermLine (one sentence, once, then gone) stays as the first-time line; the ⓘ is what's left after it goes.
