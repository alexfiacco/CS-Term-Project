Movie night Live Activity, as built today

Every presentation the widget extension draws, read from origin/main: the lock screen view (also the banner on an iPhone without the Island), and the Dynamic Island's compact, minimal and expanded, for each stage: voting (leader, votes in, faces, End), decided (the cream ticket), and stale (no update for 15 minutes, rule 802). A room called Friday with the crew, four seats (Emma, Sam, Jo, Alex), three votes in. Posters are plain title blocks. Amber circles mark the issues listed at the foot.

1 · Lock screen
MovieNightLockView on activityBackgroundTint(Theme.panel 0.92), or the cream when decided. 370pt wide, 14 padding, the system's 22 corner. The full phone first, then each state on the wallpaper.
2 · Every lock screen state
Same view, state by state. The room line shows only for a room with its own name (MovieNightWords.room hides "Friday Night Feature" and "Movie Night"). Titles wrap to two lines, then scale to 60%; never cut.
3 · Dynamic Island: compact and minimal
Compact: MovieNightThumb 16 wide (radius 3) and "Movie night" (or "Tonight" once decided) on the left, "3/4 voted" in the data blue (or "Decided", or "Not live" in faint) on the right. Minimal, when another app's activity shares the Island: the poster alone. .keylineTint(Theme.red) on all of them.
4 · Dynamic Island: expanded (long press, or an update)
Leading region beside the camera: the kicker with the dot; trailing: the faces; bottom: the room line, the leader (poster 38, Bodoni 17) and End. Decided: the film, "Decided · Friday with the crew · 4 of you", no End. The camera's place is dashed. Leading and trailing get about 104pt each: the kicker is drawn at its real width there.
5 · What the state carries but nothing draws
ContentState.standings (up to three films and their votes, "for the bars under the leader") and startsAt are filled by RoomModel and MovieNightActivity.content on every update, and no view reads them. Left: what is sent today. Right: what drawing them could look like (a sketch for the owner's call, not a build).
