Spec · one badge, two places

What the bell does today

  • iOS: a count, not a dot. ActivityBell: a Theme.red (#c8233a) capsule, min 16x16, 4 side padding, white 11 bold, "9+" past nine, at the glyph's top-right. It counts every Activity row newer than your last open (follows, reactions, invites), minus invites put away with Not tonight.
  • Web: also a count, but a different badge: .topicon-badge is 17 tall, --accent blue with dark text, 12 corners, no cap. The two platforms disagree; this mock settles both on the iOS red pill at 11 semibold.
  • Social today: the 7px --accent-2 dot with a 2px ring (owner, 2026-10-29: "Dot", rule 567), for unread one-to-one chats only. Inside Social, the plane carries a blue count and group rows a blue dot.

Recommendation: B, and only for things addressed to you

  • Count for direct, nothing for passive. Your instinct was count for direct, dot for passive. Count for direct is right: a chat waiting or an invite is something to answer, and "3" tells you whether it is worth leaving Picks for. But passive activity (friends rating, Lately posts) should not mark the tab at all, not even a dot. Your people post most days, so a passive dot would be lit almost all the time and stop meaning anything. Two marks on one icon (a dot sometimes, a number other times) also have to be learned. Passive things already have a place: Lately when you open Social, and the bell for follows and reactions.
  • What counts: each chat with unread messages (one-to-one and group chats, one per chat, not per message), plus each movie-night invite you have not answered, plus a night you are in that is now voting. Reading the chat, joining, or Not tonight takes it off. Cap at 9+.
  • One badge everywhere. The same 16 tall --red pill (white Inter/SF 11 semibold, tabular figures, 2px ring in the ground it sits on) on the bell, the Social tab and the chats plane; it moves the plane's count from blue to red. Blue stays for selection and for the per-row unread dot inside lists (group rows, chat rows), as in Messages.
  • Red, not oxblood. --shadow oxblood (#7a1220) is too dark to read as a mark on --panel at 8 to 16px. --red is the one already on the bell, and the closest in the app to the system badge on the icon. This is a second job for a red the tokens reserve for the ticket; the bell already does it, so this makes it official rather than new.
  • App icon: the system badge shows the Social count (not Social plus Activity), so a follow never buzzes the home screen. The Activity count stays on the bell.
  • Why not C: marking the place inside is good, but it is already there (the plane's count, the invite card). C only adds dots; it does not need its own rule.
