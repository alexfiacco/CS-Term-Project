Building on v1's pick, (a): the goal chip sits beside the 4-week streak. The owner liked the idea and the drawer, but not three things. The progress line looked sideways and broken, the face wasn't his photo, and the chip looked wrong both before a goal is set and while one is in progress. This round uses his real header at the live app's proportions with his own photo, and keeps both chips on one row, both 28pt tall and centered on the same line. Recommendation: (A), the quiet outline and the ring

With no goal, the chip is a plain outline reading "＋ 2026 goal" in grey. It's the same shape and height as the streak, so the row looks finished, but it doesn't call for attention the way v1's blue "Set a 2026 goal" did. Once a goal is set, it reads "42/100 films" with a 16pt ring in front. The ring sits where the streak's film-strip icon sits, so the two chips are built the same way: icon first, then the fact. There's no line hanging off the chip's floor any more. When the goal is met, the ring closes into a blue check and the chip turns blue. On someone else's profile it's the same chip, and you can't tap it. (B) has the most character, because the fill shows progress, but at 42% the tint stops partway through the words and can look like a loading bar. Its text-button empty state is also the quietest, so quiet it could be missed. (C) is the most literal: its 30pt bar is easy to read, but it brings back a "line" in the header, which is what bothered the owner.
