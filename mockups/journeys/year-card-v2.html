Your year in film

Second pass on the 9:16 Stories card. One number leads, the archetype is who you are, and five posters show what you watched. The top strand and the friend stay small, with the mark and the link at the foot. Drawn at a third of its real size (1080 × 1920), using only the fonts and layout the server card renderer already supports.

What the first draft got wrong

  • It was a data dump. Eight facts (year, handle, films, hours, archetype, strand, friend, best film), each with the same caps label and serif value. It read like a form.
  • No hero. "2026" was the biggest thing on the card, and everyone's year is 2026.
  • One poster, at the bottom, labelled "Best film". The films are what people want to show.
  • The strand and the friend were text, and the friend was an @handle, which reads as private on a public Story.

What v2 does

  • One hero number (hours logged), with a line under it saying what it is.
  • The archetype as the identity line: "A year as a / Midnight Cultist", set larger than everything but the number.
  • A strip of your top five posters, with the first one framed the way the app frames a hero poster.
  • The strand as a small radar and the friend as a face with a %. The fan mark, wordmark and link sit at the foot.

More
  • STwo chips under the preview instead of two switch rows. Tap one to hide it from the image, and the preview redraws. Shown here with Sam off.
  • SSam only appears if they're a mutual who shares activity. On the card it's always a first name or initial and a face, never an @handle.
  • SHours starts off when fewer than half of the year's films have a watch date (a Letterboxd import with no diary). The card then leads with the film count.
How the card is built
  • Mlib/year-card.tsx, an ImageResponse at 1080 × 1920, built like pickCardBody. It reuses OgSpine and OG_GROUND (the oxblood spine and the warm glow), FanMarkOG for the mark, and loadPickCardFonts: Bodoni Moda 600 and Newsreader 400/600, with no italics (only the upright Bodoni is loaded).
  • SHours = the sum of films.runtime_minutes over this year's dated watches, rewatches included, rounded down. The label always says "logged".
  • STop five: the highest ratings this year, latest watch first on ties, posters through posterDataUri at w185. With fewer than 5 rated, the strip shows what there is. Under 3, the card falls back to the profile card.
  • SThe radar is inline SVG (Satori draws it): nine strands in STRANDS_9 order, with the top one drawn in cream to its point. No percentages.
  • SThe friend is the highest taste match among mutuals who share activity: their avatar as a data URI, or an initial on blue.
  • SThe link is montagefilm.app (siteHost). The card itself carries no handle, so a screenshot doesn't expose the account.

Honest notes

  • The hero is only as good as the logs. That's why it falls back to the film count, and why the label says "logged", not "watched".
  • A tiny radar won't be read as data, and it isn't meant to be. It's a mark that says "this card is about taste". The name under it does the explaining.
  • B is the better poster but the weaker share. Keep it as a second style only if people ask.

Recommendation

Ship A for Dec 1. It has one clear message (a number, who you are, five films), it still reads as a thumbnail, and it holds up when either toggle is off. The entry point is the profile row with the drawer's two chips, so there's no new screen.
