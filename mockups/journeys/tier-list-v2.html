Tiers v2: Montage tiers it by match, you argue

Owner feedback on v1, applied: the Nº1–Nº5 numbering is gone, and each tier has a muted colour (a dark tint on the label block, a thin rule in the tier's colour, cream type). And one change of rule: Montage now places every film by its match %, seen or unseen, never by your stars. Your stars sit on the poster beside the match, so "Montage says I have Tenet too low" is a real argument with the app (it had Tenet at 62%, Background; you put it in Skip), not with your own rating.
