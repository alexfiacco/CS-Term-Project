  • Line 1 is the event, never wrapping. When it doesn’t fit, only the film title or the names give way with “…”, so “is in theaters” always reads. A long title stays whole in the film sheet one tap away.
  • Line 2 is the meta and the time together, in the feed’s short time (“6h”, the web’s shortAgo), not “6h ago” on a third line. The Blend % leads it in blue, as option A put it.
  • 40 lead (a 27 × 40 poster or a 40 face, was 42 × 62), so the poster no longer sets the row’s height. Rows are 58 tall.
  • Option A’s pill, compact: 84 × 28, one column, vertically centred; Follow back is still the one cream pill. Grouped rows keep the poster thumb in that same column. The unread dot stays centred at the far right.
  • Grouped likes move the film to line 2 (“on Heat”); grouped replies give line 2 to the reply preview, and the thumb says which film.
