Cream selection across the app

The owner: "those tags when you select them are blue. Should we make them all cream like we did with Pitch and do this across the app where applicable?" Yes. One rule, taken from Pitch's chosen chip (rule 1133): a chosen thing is cream, blue is only data (a match %, a Blend score, "91% for the room"). Most of it is three shared pieces (ChipButtonStyle, chipLook, SegmentPill on the iPhone; .mood-chip.on, .chip.on, .mood-seg.on on the web), so the build is small; the hand-made controls are listed below with file and line so it can follow them one by one.

Chips: the cream tintInk 12% fill, ink 38% outline, ink semibold text, exactly Pitch's. Many chips can be on at once (moods, genres, decades), so a quiet tint keeps the drawer calm and leaves the solid cream to the one ticket.
Segments: solid cream, dark textA segment always has exactly one chosen, so it never gets loud, and it has to read as "this one" at a glance on a dark track. The tint on the panel track was too close to the unchosen segments (shown in the grid, B).
Faces, ticks, tabs, switchesThe chosen face gets a cream ring and a cream check; tick circles fill cream with a dark tick; tab underlines go ink; switches take the cream paper tone (#C4BAA8) so the white knob still shows.
Blue stays for dataMatch %, Blend %, "91% for the room", star ratings, progress bars. Three things are questions for you, not changes: the blue sent bubble in chats, unread badges, and the liked heart.
