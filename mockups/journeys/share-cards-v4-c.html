Share cards v4 (c): the pick ticket, from scratch

The owner hated v3's ticket. Three genuinely different directions for the image a single film pick shares as, each drawn as the link preview (1.91:1) and the Story. Same facts as v3 (Heat, Friday's movie night, 91% for the room, four people) and v3's 17 words on every preview. No barcode, no heavy shadow, no ghost archetype names, Bodoni roman throughout, and each option carries one red accent.

Option A

The admission ticket

/pick/<slug>/image · OG and Story
  • A real ticket, made properly: warm cream card stock with a fine paper grain, a printed double rule, both short ends perforated (punched half holes), a column of punched holes at the tear, the stub turned on its side.
  • ADMIT ONE in spaced caps, led by the red dash (its one red). One ticket per person, so the room of four each get their own.
  • Heat big in Bodoni; year, director, date and runtime in Newsreader.
  • 91% is the stamp: a round rubber stamp in blue ink, a little worn, tilted, struck over the print like a box office would.
  • Sits at a slight angle on ink, no shadow at all.

Option C

Poster first, a slim stub

/pick/<slug>/image · OG and Story
  • The poster leads, with the subtle oxblood shadow (its one red), title big beside it, exactly the film share card's grammar.
  • A slim ticket stub along the bottom in the same card stock as A: notched ends, a dotted tear after Admit 4, the night and runtime in spaced caps, and 91% as a small blue ink stamp.
  • Recommended. In a chat the image is about 300px wide: the poster is the one thing that reads at that size, and you know the film before you read a word. The stub says this is a night out without turning the whole card into a costume, and the card stays dark like every other Montage card.
