Did it exist, and was it removed?

  • It exists today, on both apps. iPhone: OwnFilmsTab in ios/Montage/Sources/Features/Profile/ProfileLibrary.swift draws "\(model.unrated.grouped) unrated · Rate them" in the count row, seenDeck = true opens RateSeenDeck. Web: .lib-count-link in app/_components/IdentityView.tsx, "N unrated · Rate them", opening the web RateSeenDeck. Both read GET /api/rate-seen (unrated = watched minus rated minus Can't recall).
  • History: the web line came with rule 112 (2026-09-29, "a quiet 'N unrated · Rate them ›' beside the Films tab's count"); the iPhone made it a button in 67c81f1b (2026-10-29, "iOS parity: profile extras … Rate them"), which turned the plain "N unrated" into:
Text("\(model.unrated.grouped) unrated · Rate them ›").font(Typo.ui(12, .medium)).foregroundStyle(Theme.accent2)
  • What changed since is its look, not its presence: ee9e2db7 and 3fe36010 (2026-10-31, "one button system and one type scale", rule 598) took the › off and, on the iPhone, moved it from the blue to muted meta semibold. Since then it reads as part of the faint count, which is likely why it feels gone. Rule 615 (2026-11-02) removed only the after-import "Your profile is ready · Rate 20 films" sheet: "rating what you've seen stays in the Films tab".
  • The filter exists too: "Seen, unrated" is the last option of Filters › Your rating on both apps (RATING_BANDS in IdentityView.tsx, FilterOptions.ratingBands in BrowseFilters.swift), shown as an active chip under the sort bar when on.

Recommendation: keep it, and make it a row

  • Yes, worth having, and it is cheap: nothing new to build, the count, the API and the deck all exist. The change is placement: a row of its own at the top of your own Films, so it is read as an action, not as part of the count. Remove the count row's link at the same time (one door).
  • Why now more than in September: the archetype reads unrated watches too (watchWeights, "ratio" mode: each one a fraction of an average favorite, more the less of a library is rated), but an unrated watch only says "seen", never "loved" or "didn't land". Rating them turns a guess into a real signal: a 2★ takes a film out of your type, a 5★ makes it count in full and can make it one of your defining films (only rated films can be).
  • Rule 112 to amend: it says "never a link at the top of the profile". The Films tab's top is not the profile's top (the archetype and figures stay clean), but the rule should say so.
  • Copy: "182 films without a rating" over "182 unrated": it says what the number is. "Rate them" stays the action.
  • The chip: rename "Seen, unrated" to "Unrated" on Films (every film on Films is seen). I would not add a standing chip to the sort bar for it; the row already gets you rating, and the filter is one tap in Filters.
  • Keep it quiet: no progress bar, no streak, no badge (rule 112: no milestone moment). Hidden on others' profiles and at 0.
