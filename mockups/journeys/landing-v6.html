Recommendation

Go with A, three moments. It keeps exactly what made v5 A work, showing instead of describing, and drops everything that made it busy: no UI chrome, no labels, no extra posters, no second sentence. Three pictures a visitor gets in a glance (a name, a 92% on a poster, an 87% between two people) and sixteen words in all, down from 58.

Archetype and Film DNA stay first, as the name in big serif with a quiet DNA strip under it.

Desktop needs no extra design: the same three sit side by side.

B is the most compact, but cards 2 and 3 hide behind a swipe most visitors won't make. C is the most editorial and the only one that names groups; take its "Agree on one film" row into A as a fourth moment if the owner wants groups said out loud.
