The four question cards come out of the deck

Today a question card sits after every 4th film (slides 5, 10, 15, 20: QUESTION_EVERY = 4, the same on iPhone). Two of the four are about what you like and two are about what you'd rather not. Only the two "rather not" ones have somewhere to go on a skip page.

Now · slide 10, question 2 of 4"Quick one" replaces the eyebrow; the card takes the film's place. Multi-select, so a Next film button. Its answers move the read a little and lean the picks toward films that end that way.
Now · slide 15, question 3 of 4Single-select: a tap picks and moves on. I'd rather not on its own saves "Languages: English" in Settings: a hard filter, not a lean.

2 · After "Where do you watch?"

Anything you'd rather skip?

One screen, chips only, in the chip-select style (select fill, edge, tick), every chip 44 tall. A: its own screen straight after the services screen (firstrun-v1 item 9). B: both questions on one screen.

A · 1 of 2 · being built (firstrun-v1 item 9)Unchanged. Continue goes to the new screen instead of the walkthrough.
A · 2 of 2 · the new screenThree groups, all on one screen. Skip and Continue side by side; Continue works with nothing picked. Then the walkthrough.
B · one combined screenServices as smaller tiles, then the three groups. It doesn't fit: the page scrolls past the fold, the bar has to stick, and the two halves mean different things (what you have, what you'd skip).

Recommendation: A, two short screens

  • Each one fits and answers one question. B runs to about 1,100pt and hides Subtitles below the fold, so most people would never see it.
  • The cost is one more tap, and both have Skip. Four question cards come out of the deck, so the first run is still three steps shorter.
  • Chips map to what exists: the genres are Not my thing's keys plus three new ones (War, Sci-fi, Romance). Gore and Jump scares are already in Things I'd rather avoid. Graphic violence, Sad endings and Slow burns are new, read from the film labels (violence, ending, propulsion). The eras and subtitles are new soft keys.
  • All warnings (a 44pt link on the Themes line) opens the rest of Things I'd rather avoid (sexual violence, self-harm, animal death...) in a drawer, so the screen stays short.
  • Never "Not my thing" wording here. These are softer than that setting is today (sheet 3).
4 + 5 · Later

Change it in Settings; the films you'd still love on Browse

The chips live on in Settings → Recommendation preferences as Show less of, which takes over the hard "Not my thing" row. A skipped kind ranks lower but keeps its real %, so a Western that matches you at 91% can still have its own row.

Proposed · Settings, Show less ofThe same groups and chips as the first-run screen, in the usual Settings drawer. Things I'd rather avoid stays as it is: a hard filter for content warnings.
Proposed · Browse, under Made for youOne row for one skipped kind: the one with the most unseen films at 88%+, shown only when at least 4 qualify. The title names the kind. Never built from content warnings.

How it plugs into Picks

  • Today Not my thing is hard: those films are left out of every suggestion (fitsViewingPrefs), and anywhere else their % stops at 55 (PREF_CAP). If skips worked that way, no 88% row could exist.
  • Proposed: a skip costs a film rank points, never its %. Add it to demotionOf() in lib/taste/pick-demotions.ts, which every pick surface already subtracts beside Less of: 10 points for one skipped kind, 16 for two or more, not faded by ratings, because you said it yourself. An 88% Western ranks like a 78%: still in the running, behind most of your list. Rooms keep their own room penalty.
  • Remove notMine from fitsViewingPrefs and prefCapReason. The why drawer says "Western · you'd rather skip these" beside the true %, and Show Westerns again stays.
  • Hard stays hard: Things I'd rather avoid, Languages, Oldest films and Age ratings keep filtering. They are explicit filters, and a content warning must never be a stretch row.

Data: no table, no SQL

  • Everything sits in public_profiles.result_json.viewingPrefs and saves through POST /api/viewing-prefs, the same call the services screen makes. notMine keeps its name and its stored values (NOT_MINE is append-only; add war, scifi, romance, pre1960, 6070, subtitled, sad, slowburn). avoid gets "graphic violence" appended to CONTENT_WARNINGS. prefsKey already keys both, so cached picks rebuild.
  • The deck stops sending answers; the old answers on existing profiles keep fading as they do now. viewingPrefsFromAnswers goes, so new runs no longer get English only or 1980 on by default.
  • Browse row: a new lane in lib/taste-mixes.ts, filtered to one skipped kind at 88+, not seen, not hidden. iPhone reads the same /api/taste-mixes.
