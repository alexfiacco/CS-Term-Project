Recommendation: build it on Social, but don't over-trust it. It is cheap and the owner's competitor review shows acquisition is the open question. Social after day 1 is the better place: it is quiet for new accounts and keeps Picks' top slot for things that improve recommendations. The cost is reach: some people rarely open Social, so expect fewer answers than a Picks card would get. If answers are too few after two weeks, fall back to the Picks banner slot on day 3, only when no other banner is due.

Honest weak points: self-reported source is noisy (people say "App Store" for anything they installed from it, and "TikTok" when a friend sent a TikTok), so treat it as a rough signal next to real referrers (the invite links' ?from=, web UTM tags, App Store campaign links). Eight chips is the upper limit for one glance; drop Google into "Somewhere else" if the card feels busy.
