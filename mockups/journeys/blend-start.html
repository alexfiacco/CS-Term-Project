"Keep this Blend" turns the lookup into a living list

Looking at a Blend stays free and instant. Keeping one is the opt-in part: you ask, they say yes, and from then on a list of films you'd both love stays fresh for the two of you. Why "Keep this Blend": the page already is your Blend, so "Start a Blend" would say it doesn't exist yet, and "Save" means a watchlist bookmark everywhere else in the app. "Keep" says the one new thing, that it lasts and keeps up, and it reads the same on the other phone ("Sam wants to keep your Blend") and in the menu later ("Stop keeping"). "Living Blend" was the runner-up, but it's a term you'd have to explain.
