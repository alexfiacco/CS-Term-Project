One way in: what are we watching?

Today the moon opens Tonight, which picks one film from moods. Pitch (describe it in words) lives on Browse beside the search, and is only reached from Tonight by a faint line under the moods. Movie night starts from a ticket on Social, or from a line that appears only after you choose Friends or Family in Tonight. Three ways to find a film, three places, three names. Proposed: the moon asks one question, "What are we watching?", and offers the three ways (and Surprise me) as plain rows; each way opens under a header with its own name and a back to the question. iPhone 390. 3 · Option B: a segmented control at the top of one sheet

The same three ways as a 36 SegmentPill (44 target) under the question. No chooser step; the last segment is remembered and opens.

B1 · Just me

Two segmented controls stacked: the ways, then Who's watching. Just me is in both.

B2 · Describe

The box under the control. The segment words have to be shorter than the ways' names, and nothing says what each does.

Recommendation: A

A explains, B only labels. The problem is that people do not know the three ways exist or how they differ. A's one plain line per row is the explanation; B's three words (Just me · Describe · Friends) are not enough, and there is no room for more.

B stacks two segmented controls on Just me (the ways, then Who's watching with its own Just me), which reads as one control set twice. Tonight's choices would need re-laying-out to avoid it.

B implies the three are the same thing, filtered. They are three different tasks: tap chips, type a sentence, gather people. A sheet that changes completely under a fixed control feels like tabs inside a tab bar.

What A costs: one more tap before Tonight's choices. Surprise me (one tap to a film, as today's Just pick for me) and the quiet Last time hint keep that small. Measure moon to first pick before and after.

Build order if approved: the chooser and headers (web TonightHost + Tonight.tsx; iOS TonightSheet + MontageApp TabBar), then point Browse's Pitch button and Social's ticket at the sheet, then the With friends screen (people before a room exists).
