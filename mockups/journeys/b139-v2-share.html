When. The first time you open your own profile after saving, even if Picks showed a first-run thing earlier that launch. Once: ×, a swipe down or any send closes it for good. Later, Profile › Share profile opens the same screen.

Send in Montage. One tap sends to that chat, friend or group; the face reads "Sent". Search opens today's friend picker. A film, a list and a pitch go as the card the chat already shows; the archetype, a Blend and a movie night go as a link to the page (chats don't carry images yet).

Elsewhere. Instagram Story: one tap, the card as the Story's background. Instagram post: saves the 4:5 card and opens Instagram (a toast says so). Messages: the image attached. TikTok: saves and opens TikTok until TikTok's Share Kit is approved. More: the phone's share sheet. Computer: Save Story size, Save post size, Copy link, More.

The words, as the reveal says them. The name, "with a Nihilist streak", then the tag and the streak's sentence ("And it lands better when the ending doesn't let you off.").
