Montage brand / logo system v6

The strip, away from three dots

S1 read too close to Letterboxd: three coloured shapes in a row on dark. Four variants keep the strip and the fan animation but change the count, the orientation or the shape of the frames, so the silhouette says film, not dots.

Construction rules
  • 100 grid. Strip body cream #efe7d6, radius 5. Sprocket holes 6 × 4 (4 × 6 when vertical), radius 1.2, dropped at 32 and below.
  • Frames radius 1.5, separated by a 3-unit frame line. Red #c24c56 always before blue #5a86bd (reading order of the tick).
  • Small cut (≤ 32): body grows to fill 84% of the tile, holes go, frame lines thicken to 5.
  • Drawn M from v5 (S3c) is the companion mark in every lockup.
