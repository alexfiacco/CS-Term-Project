Owner, 2026-11-06: "Takes on profile. What tab is this under?" The profile's tabs are Taste, Films, Watchlist (yours only), History. History lists every watch by month as a 3-across grid, and every take is already tied to the watch it was written about, so a take belongs with its watch. Each option is drawn at 390pt on Emma's History as Sam (a friend) sees it, in October with five watches, two of them with takes. Only takes the viewer is allowed to see appear (a Friends take only to friends, nothing across a block). No red is added. Names are anonymised.
