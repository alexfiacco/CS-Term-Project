Recommendation

Hero: the owner's line, "Know what you'll like before you press play, solo or with your people." Keep it whole; the tighter cut saves two words and loses "your people".

"Once you're in": A, show don't list. It is the only option that stops describing the app and simply shows it: an archetype with its Film DNA, a poster stamped 92%, an 87% Blend, the film a room picked. Each caption is one plain sentence, so there is nothing for the "AI" pattern to hide in. It also carries Where to watch as a Browse filter, which is what it really is, and it lays out four across on desktop with no extra design.

Runner-up: B, end credits, if you want the landing to have a wink. It is the most on brand and the most screenshot-able, but it is a list in costume. A good home for B is the end of the reveal or the share card, where the joke lands harder.

C is the safe one: clean and clear, closest to today in length.

Dropped from every option: Where to watch as its own row, and "no app needed".
