What changes

  • No "Browse" title. The search row sits 16 under the top bar's hairline; Made for you starts higher than today, and more of Every film shows above the tab bar.
  • Pitch. The same 44 cream-outline button at the 12 radius, now "Pitch" in sans 15/600 beside the Lucide quote icon (18), so it reads as a control next to the field, not a heading.
  • Rules. Every section rule runs the full content width, 16 to 16. Today Made for you's rule stops where the title's column ends (the (i) and "All 10" sit beside it, outside the rule); Browse by's and Every film's already run full.
  • A. "Made for you" stays the one Bodoni 22 head: it is the personal part of the page. "Browse by" and "Every film" become 11 caps labels (600, tracked .14em, muted), as utility sections. One clear hierarchy.
  • B. All three Bodoni 20: one rhythm, quieter than today's 22 but still three equal display heads on one screen.

Pick

A. One Bodoni head per screen is calmer and says what matters (your mixes); the caps labels make Browse by and Every film read as tools, which is what they are. B is a smaller step from today if you want all three to keep the serif.
