Decide

  1. A, the credits line, left-aligned, on every profile. It is compact (the tabs stay in the first screen), it keeps the face-beside-name layout you already have, and Bodoni plus the archetype in italic does the branding. One layout serves your page and a friend's.
  2. B, the centred billing block, if the profile should open like a poster. It is more distinctive at the top, but it pushes the archetype hero below the fold on a 390 phone.
  3. The name is Bodoni Title 28 on every profile: yours, a friend's and the public web page. No more sans on your own page.
  4. "since 2024" uses the account's created date, and the archetype in the credits links to its page. If you would rather not show the year, the line becomes "1,260 films · Nihilist".
  5. The streak and the 76/110 goal ring move into the archetype hero as one line of UI meta. Today's chips with emoji and a ring leave the header.
  6. On someone else's profile: Follow is cream and Following is quiet, Share is an icon (A) or a link (B), the add-person circle goes, and the Blend card keeps its blue 94%.
