  2. Pickers: Person searches our people (GlobalSearch's people); Genre, Decade, Length and Language are chip lists; Service lists yours first, then the rest; Mood is today's strands with ↑ ↓, moved into its picker.
  3. Rename Steer it to “Narrow it: older, shorter, no subtitles”.
  4. Server: op: "run" takes a sentence and the picked chips as a query and merges them (steer's merge, lib/recut.ts:1161); chips alone run without a sentence. op: "rank"'s add takes any chip, not only a dial.
  5. Leave Ending, Setting, Occasion, Seen and the extras to the sentence; the row stays one line.
