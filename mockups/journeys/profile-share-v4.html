Profile share card v4: the Criterion cover in black, red and blue

The owner on v3: "I like this Criterion look, but let's style it more like using black, red, and blue. Because that would be cool." So B2 keeps everything it had (the No. 1260 spine and collection number, the title in Bodoni, "Starring Alex Morgan as Nihilist" with no article, the tagline and numbers as one sentence, the poster row on the Story and the film sentence on the Post, What's our Blend? and the link) and drops the per-archetype colour fields for one house palette: black #0b0b0d, oxblood #7a1220, Montage blue #5b8fc4 / #8fb3d9, cream type #efe8da. Three arrangements, each for a Nihilist (Alex Morgan) and a Lovestruck (Emma Hart) at Story 9:16 and Post 4:5, shown scaled, and at chat-thumbnail size. With one palette for everyone, the archetype is told apart by its still, its title and one accent swap: Nihilist's small accents (the number and the foot rule) are blue, Lovestruck's are red. Share-card art is allowed more than one red or accent (house-rule exception); each card here uses at most one red surface and one blue. Mock limitation: Prisoners is the only sharp still on disk (703 wide); the Lovestruck still and the poster row are 240-wide poster files, so they are soft. Built, the still is the #1 film's TMDB backdrop at w1280.
