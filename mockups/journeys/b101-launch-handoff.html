b101 · Cold launch: the logo hands off into the screen that opens
How a cold launch ends (iPhone; rules 611, 2010). The flight to a tab header is already built for Picks (origin/main LaunchOverlay.land, TopBar(landing: true), LaunchGate.glyphFrame); this board shows it frame by frame from the code's own timeline, then what changes: every tab header with the logo reports one anchor, the overlay lands on the header of whichever tab is on screen, and a launch that opens a page with no header logo (a film, a profile, a chat) only fades. Screens laid out from the current SwiftUI (TopBar 59 + 4 + 44; tags 2 + 44; full-bleed card), not device captures. Logo: FanGlyph (fan.svg) and Wordmark (wordmark.svg), the real assets. Yellow dashed boxes are annotations only.

Feasibility

Picks: built. The overlay already eases the fan's frame and the wordmark's frame onto the global frames TopBar measures (a GeometryReader reporter, not anchor preferences: the overlay sits outside Picks' view tree), fades its ground from h+400 to h+750 ms and is removed at h+800. Once landed, the overlay's marks sit pixel-for-pixel on the header's own, so removing the overlay is invisible. One gap: the ground starts fading before the flight ends, so the header logo ghosts for ~150 ms (A4); hiding the header mark until done() (your suggestion) fixes it.

What to build

One anchor per tab, keyed: TopBar always reports launchLanding into LaunchGate.anchors[AppTab] (Picks, Browse, Social; Tonight and Profile have no logo) instead of Picks' one landing flag, since mounted tabs stay alive and would all report. At land time the overlay picks anchors[router.tab]; no anchor, a page up, or Reduce Motion: fade. MainTabView sets LaunchGate.routed when a cold-launch route opens a page, and that counts as ready so the fade doesn't wait for picks.

Risk · effort · files

The real risk: pages are UIKit full-screen covers (PageStack, screenCover, filmCover), drawn above RootView's .overlay. Today a cold launch from a notification opens its page about 0.35 s after the tabs appear, so the page likely covers the animation mid-play (read from the code, not seen on a device). Fix: host LaunchOverlay in its own UIWindow above the app for the launch (as MenuPopover does), or hold the route until done(). Safe area and the Dynamic Island are already handled (global frames; the bar sits under the 59pt inset). A header not laid out yet: no frame, it fades (built).
