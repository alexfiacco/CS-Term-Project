The DM film card, compact Print

The owner picked A, Print from dm-card-archetype-v1.html: "A but make it more compact with the buttons." Three compact versions of A, each drawn in the same thread with Sam as v1, beside today's card and v1's A. All three share a tightened Print: 60 by 90 poster (was 80 by 120), 10pt padding (was 12), Bodoni 16.5 on 1.1 (was 18 on 1.12), the note 15 on 1.28, 8pt above it. The line is still the reader's own: unseen, the blue "91% for you" stamp; seen, "Rated 5★". The oxblood offset sits only on the newest card (Portrait, at the bottom). People are made up. Heights are measured live from this page and listed at the bottom.

What changed from v1's A

  • The 30pt Save pill had its own row under the stamp. All three variants drop that row; the save lives on the stamp's line or on the card's corner.
  • Card is 280 wide (was 292); the poster sets the card's height, and the text column now fits beside it, so a card with no note is the poster plus 20.
  • A tap anywhere but the save opens the film. Hold reacts, swipe replies, as before.

Save vs Rewatch, without a label

  • The film page's Rewatch button uses the same bookmark as Watchlist (FilmScreen.swift:431 bookmark / bookmark.fill; web BookmarkIcon); only the word changes. So the icon-only variants (a, c) draw the bookmark for both, outlined off, filled on.
  • What tells them apart is the stamp right beside it: a blue % means unseen, "Rated 5★" means seen. The toast and VoiceOver say "rewatch list".

Recommendation: b, the inline chip

  • b keeps the word (Save, Saved, Rewatch) the owner asked for on seen films, on the stamp's line, at the height of a.
  • a is the cleanest look but asks the reader to read the stamp to know what the bookmark does. Take a if words are not needed.
  • c is the smallest by a lot, but titles clip at two lines and the 44pt poster reads more like a link preview than a print.

Reading the numbers

  • "Thread" is the whole message list (two text bubbles, four cards, the receipt), so it says how much of a screen four shares take.

  • The dashed rings on Portrait in a and b mark the 44pt tap area around the save; the rest of the card opens the film.
