A film goal for the year, and a free year card in December

Two quiet additions to the profile. A yearly goal: you pick a number in January ("52 films in 2026"), and a slim bar on your profile shows how far you are ("41 so far"). It's private unless you choose to show it, and there's no penalty for missing it. The year Montage, free for everyone: the year recap is already built (lib/taste/lately.ts, recapFor: hours, top films, most-watched director, best streak, deep cuts) but sits behind Lately and Pro. From December 1 it becomes a share card for everyone, since sharing it is the growth.
