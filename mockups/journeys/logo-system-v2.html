Montage brand / logo system v2

One system: the ticket and the tick

Construction rules: stub 48×68 on a 100 grid, corner radius = notch radius = 5, tear at two-thirds height, five holes on a 7 pitch. The tick is always 8:1 at −6°; at 32 and below it thickens to 4:1 and the perforation becomes a single cut. The M is Bodoni Moda 800 at optical size 18 for sturdier hairlines. The ticket stub is the primary mark and app icon. The Bodoni M over the tilted tick is the monogram. “Montage” in Bodoni Moda is the wordmark. All marks are flat SVG in red #c24c56, blue #5a86bd and cream #efe7d6 on #0b0b0d.

Why these two

Rationale

Ticket = admission to film

A stub is the oldest everyday object of going to the cinema, and your profile is already a ticket with a member Nº.

Tick = our mark of taste

The red/blue bar is the tick we put on films we think you’ll love. It isn’t a film symbol on its own; it gets that meaning from being on the ticket.

Together

The film you’ll love, ticketed for you. The ticket says what Montage is about, and the tick says it is yours.

Where each piece lives

In use (shown with ticket variant B)

Pick

Ticket variant B: cream stub, tick printed on it

B reads as a ticket first: a cream paper stub is what a ticket looks like, while the all-colour stub in A reads as a two-tone badge. The tick is printed on it the way we mark a film for you, so the two motifs are literally one object. On dark home screens and browser tabs the cream silhouette has the strongest contrast, so it stays clear at 32 and 16, where the perforation drops and the tick thickens. C adds an M and is the busiest; keep the M for the monogram instead.
