Bug report v1

Reel's pattern for Montage: take a screenshot in the iPhone app and a toast offers to report it, with the picture already attached. It opens a short "Report a problem" drawer: the screenshot (Mark up with iOS's own editor), what went wrong, an optional 15-second recording of Montage, and the device and version sent along quietly. On in TestFlight and internal builds; in App Store builds only after a Settings switch. Settings also gets two Support rows so it can be found without a screenshot.

Now
As built: no screenshot path. A bug goes through Menu › Send feedback (AccountScreens.swift FeedbackView): Idea, Bug or Other, a text field, Send, into /api/user-feedback (the user_feedback table, then a mail to FEEDBACK_NOTIFY_EMAIL). Settings has no Support section; the Menu's About group ends in Help and support (the /support page). A screenshot today only does something on a profile: "Share your card instead?" (rule 1219).
Now · Menu › Send feedback
Text only. No screenshot, no version, no device, so "the poster is doubled" arrives with nothing to look at.
Proposed · screenshot, report, sent
Alex screenshots Picks because The Prestige shows twice. The toast is at the top, not over the tab bar where the one toast usually sits: iOS puts its own screenshot thumbnail at the bottom left for the same five seconds, and the two would collide.
1 · The toast, over Picks
The one toast's surface (--panel-raised, 12 corners), 16 in from each side, 4 under the status bar. A 32×69 thumbnail of the shot, "Spotted a bug?" 15 semibold, "Tap to report" 13 muted, × in a 44 square. The whole card opens the drawer. Goes after 5 seconds, with a swipe up, or with ×. Once per screen visit; never on a profile, where the card prompt answers screenshots.
2 · Report a problem
The app's drawer, large. The screenshot is attached (× removes it). Mark up opens iOS's own Markup on the image (QuickLook editing), so there's nothing to build. Record 15s is the second tile. The device line is faint, there so you know what gets sent. Send is the Large 50 cream button; it needs words or a recording.
3 · Record 15s of the screen
The drawer steps aside and the pill sits where the one toast does, above the tab bar. ReplayKit records Montage only (never other apps or the keyboard's text). iOS asks first and shows its own red pill; ours counts to 15 and stops itself. Stop returns to the drawer with the clip in the second tile.
4 · Sent
The drawer shrinks to fit: a Bodoni title with the tick, one sentence of what happens next, a receipt of what was sent, Done. If the upload fails it says so with Try again; it never thanks for something that didn't arrive (today's feedback thanks either way).
Proposed · Settings
Two places: the switch under On this iPhone, next to Shake to re-roll, and a Support section above Letterboxd so a report can start without a screenshot. Dashed outlines mark what's new.
Settings · the switch and Support
"Screenshot to report a problem", not "Shake or screenshot": a shake already re-rolls Tonight, and one gesture shouldn't do two things. On by default in TestFlight and internal builds, off in App Store builds.
From Support · no screenshot yet
Report a problem opens the same drawer with an empty first tile (Add screenshot, from Photos). "Record what happened" opens it straight on the recording, so you can show the bug happening.

Not: a GitHub issueScreenshots carry other people's names, faces and chat. Put them in a repo's issues and they're one visibility change from public, and the server would hold a write token. File one by hand from the mail when it's a real bug.
  • Reuse what's there. /api/user-feedback already has the rate limit, the session-only sender, the table and the mail with its daily cap. A sibling route (or the same one taking attachments) adds signed uploads; the 8 KB body cap stays because files never pass through the route.
  • TestFlight already has one. Apple's screenshot feedback (Share Beta Feedback) goes to App Store Connect, without the account, archetype or screen. Ours adds those and lands in the inbox, so testers should use ours; keep Apple's as the fallback.
  • What's sent, said plainly: the device line in the drawer is the whole list (model, iOS, version and build, the screen, your handle). Nothing else is read; the screenshot is only the one you just took.
  • Cheap to build: userDidTakeScreenshotNotification (already observed on Profile), the newest image from the photo library with limited access (or a re-render of the key window, which needs no permission: recommended), QLPreviewController editing for Mark up, RPScreenRecorder.startRecording for the clip.
