What changes

  • A: Popular this week. One caps label (as Browse by and Every film) with "See all" on its line, the full-width rule 8 under, then 12 to a row of eight posters, 104 wide, 12 apart, the 5/5 oxblood offset, so three show whole and the fourth peeks. Each poster carries the new badge (solid #0b0b0d chip, radius 2, Bodoni 18 blue %) and its title in 13. See all opens a door page like Most popular's (its kicker, the pills with the sprocket rail, the grid). Made for you follows 32 under; it starts lower than today.
  • Source. TMDB's /trending/movie/week, which the server already calls: trendingThisWeek() in app/api/start-films/route.ts (Rate films you love's "Trending this week" row), matched to the catalog by tmdb_id, refreshed daily. It moves to lib/ and Browse reads it; the order is TMDB's, the badge is yours. Posters here are today's cached TMDB trending releases (ai_cache trending-releases, 2026-11-07); no TMDB key in this checkout to call trending/week itself.
  • B: your friends are watching. A second caps row, 32 under, of films the people you follow rated, logged or watchlisted in the last 7 days, most friends first, none already in Popular this week. Under each title a stack of up to three 20 faces (their photos; initials here), the first friend on top; a long press or the film page names them. Shown only with 6 or more films from 2 or more friends that week; otherwise the row is gone with no gap or placeholder (thin frame). Made for you then starts lower than today, below the first screen.
  • Most popular beside Highest rated. In the Collections drawer the first row becomes Most popular · Highest rated (the two "what everyone rates" doors together), then Hidden gems · Newest, Streaming · Under 90 minutes. Its page stays all-time; Popular this week is the "now" door.

Pick

A. It answers the question on the first screen with a source that is full every day, costs one row, and keeps Made for you's first tiles in view. B's friends row is the better idea once there are more members, but today two thirds of followers would see one friend's week dressed as "friends", and Made for you drops below the fold for everyone who gets it. Ship A and the Most popular move now; add B's row behind the 6-films / 2-friends floor when Social grows.
