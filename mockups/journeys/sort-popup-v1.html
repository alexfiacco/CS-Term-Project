  • Our panel (panel-2, the line edge, a 14 corner, a little pointer to the chip), the app’s UI type at 15, and the chosen check: a dark tick on a solid blue circle (the one chosen state, rule 1172).
  • Direction moves inside as a two-way segment that names what it means (“Highest first / Lowest first”, “A to Z / Z to A”, “Newest first / Oldest first”, the web’s own words). That removes the separate ↓ chip, which most people never decode. Best for you hides the segment.
  • “Sort by” is a caps label, not a Bodoni title, so no tick. A popover isn’t a page or a drawer.

  • Matches the Filters drawer next to it, gets the tick (it’s a drawer title), and is easy to reach one-handed. But it covers the whole grid for a single tap, and it’s the heavier gesture for something you change often.

Decide

  1. A: the small custom popover, direction inside it, blue check, no tick Rec
    One tap to open and one to choose, and the grid stays in view. Same component on web (replaces the native select) and iPhone (replaces the system Menu), and the same for Watchlist and Browse sorts.
  2. B: a bottom drawer titled “Sort” with the tick, matching Filters.
  3. A, but keep the separate ↓ chip for direction, and only the menu changes.
