# b132 v2: words that say what the button does

The owner approved the b132 before/after and the widget, and asked "what does Play mean?". The button opens the service's app at the film, using the pick's TonightWhere url. It never plays anything inside Montage.

| Words | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Play | Doesn't say where you go. The play triangle is also the Trailer icon. |
| Play on Netflix | Tonight's current words. Names the place, but promises playback; Netflix opens on the title page. |
| **Watch on Netflix** | **Recommended.** Says what you're doing and where, the way people say it, and promises only to open Netflix at the film. |
| Open in Netflix ↗ | Literal, but reads like a link; the app doesn't use the arrow. |

**Recommended:**
- **"Watch on Netflix"** on Picks' card and on reopened Tonight, until the film would have ended. On the card, "On Netflix" moves out of the line under the title (which now just says "Picked at 8:10 pm") and into the button.
- Keep "Rent on …" and "Buy on …" when that's the only way to watch (they signal it costs money), and "Showtimes" for a cinema.
- **"Where to watch"** when there's nothing; it opens the options drawer.
- For one vocabulary, switch Tonight's result drawer and the movie night ticket from "Play on Max" to "Watch on Max" too. That is `TonightWhere.play` on iOS and `playLine` on the web.

The widget is unchanged: its "On Netflix" is a label, not a button.
