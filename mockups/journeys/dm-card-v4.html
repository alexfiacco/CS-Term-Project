The owner on v3: "I like backdrop but make it more on theme, and open is never a button name for us?" So direction 1 stays, now built from Montage's own parts: the card's 8 corner and 1px rule grey outline (every other card), a darker, desaturated, grained backdrop instead of a glossy blur, the title in Bodoni roman on cream ink, year and director as the film page's muted caps, the straight blue stamp from the posters and the film page, and one red per screen: the oxblood offset on the newest card's poster, or (B) the C1 dash under its kicker instead. The second pill is Watch on Hulu when the film is on your service, else Details, the word the chat card, the decks and the film page tabs already use. Round 3's band (with Open swapped out) is drawn first for comparison; each card's height in pt is printed beside it.

All three keep

  • The frame: radius 8, 1px #3A3A3E, #141417, as Montage's cards and tiles (--radius-card, --line-2). No glossy drop under the card, no white.
  • The backdrop: grayscale 35%, saturation 62%, brightness 50%, a fine film grain over it, fading to the card. A real backdrop_path still in the build; the mock crops the poster.
  • Your line: the blue stamp "91% for you", straight, as on posters and Our read's Spot on; seen, "Rated 5★".

The pills

  • Save, or Rewatch on a film you rated: cream, with the bookmark. Once saved: blue Saved with the bookmark filled, a second tap takes it off.
  • Watch on Hulu when it streams on one of your services (where-chip's "Watch on Netflix"), else Details, which goes to the film page. Never Open.
  • 34pt pills with a 44pt tap area; the rest of the card opens the film too.

The one red

  • A and C: the newest card's poster on the oxblood offset (5 on this 64pt poster; 6 on the film page's 108).
  • B: the C1 dash (rule 1127: 24 by 3, 3 under the label) under "You sent" on the newest card, and no offset.
