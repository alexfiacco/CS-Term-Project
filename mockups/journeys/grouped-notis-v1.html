How it groups

  • One row per take or post. The newest person leads; their face sits on top of the next one.
  • Replies get their own row with the newest reply as a grey preview, separate from likes, so a conversation never gets lost under a pile of likes.
  • Likes and reactions share one row: “Jake and 11 others liked your take.” A single reaction shows the emoji.
  • A new like or reply moves the row back to the top and marks it unread (blue dot).
  • Likes and reactions never push. Replies push at most once per take per hour.
