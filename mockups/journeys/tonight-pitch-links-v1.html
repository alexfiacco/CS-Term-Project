Tonight and Pitch · iPhone 390 · mock

Two quiet links between Tonight and Pitch

Tonight decides for you: a few taps, one film. Pitch is for when you know what you want: a typed sentence, a sorted shortlist. The owner asked for a link each way, "if you can make it subtle". Each link here is one line of 13px text: grey words with the action in blue. No new buttons, no cards, no red. Built from what is live on main: TonightSheet.swift (setup and result) and Recut.swift (drawn as Pitch, since the rename is landing).

1 · In Tonight: "Know exactly what you want? Pitch it ›"

Opens Pitch with the box focused. If moods are chosen, they seed the sentence ("Something tense, "), so nothing typed so far is lost.

Today · setup

Live. Who's watching, Streaming on, In the mood for, then More filters to scroll. The cream ticket is pinned at the bottom. No way across to Pitch.

A · Under the moods Recommended

One grey line under the mood chips. It sits where you are choosing how to describe the night, so it reads as the other way to answer, not a second action. It scrolls with the choices, so it never sits beside the pinned ticket.

Today · result

Live. The ticket, the stub (Trailer, Watchlist, Re-roll) and Watching this tonight, sized to fit above the fold, then Or one of these.

B · Under the result

One centred line under Watching this tonight. It catches the "not this one" moment after a Re-roll, but it pushes Or one of these down and it is only seen by people who already got a pick.

2 · In Pitch results: "Just pick one for me ›"

Takes this pitch's shortlist, already sorted by your match, and shows the top fit as one Tonight-style ticket. Re-roll steps to the next film on the same list. No new search and no Pitch used from the monthly free ones.

Today · results

Live. The sentence, Understood as, Steer it, then Sorted for you with the shortlist. New pitch sits at the very end.

A · On the Sorted for you line Recommended

Right-aligned on the heading row, in the space the row already leaves empty (it has a Spacer today). Seen the moment the list loads, takes no height, and reads as a way to act on this list.

B · Beside New pitch, after the list

Under the last film, scrolled. Quietest of all, and natural once you have looked through everything, but with 24 films it is far down and most people will never see it.

After the tap · one pick from the shortlist

Tonight's ticket, fed by the pitch. The caps line says where it came from. Re-roll reads "Next fit" and moves down the list. Back returns to the shortlist as it was.

Tonight: put it under the moods (A)

Why A. The fork between "decide for me" and "I know what I want" happens while you are choosing moods, so that is where the line earns its place. It stays inside the scroll and well away from the pinned ticket, so it never competes with Just pick for me. The result screen was sized so the ticket and Watching this tonight fit above the fold; B would cost that space for a link most people only reach after a miss.

Build. One Button with QuietButtonStyle(size: Typo.metaSize) under the mood FlowLayout in TonightSheet.inputs, opening the Pitch page (the existing RecutLink) with the moods as a starting sentence. Web: the same line under the mood chips in Discover.tsx, as a .linkbtn.

Pitch: on the Sorted for you line (A), one pick from the shortlist

Why A. It is visible as soon as the shortlist arrives, adds no height, and sits on the heading of the very list it acts on. B hides it under 24 films.

Simpler behaviour: one pick from the shortlist. The shortlist is already ranked by match, so the top fit is films[0]; show it in the ticket layout that exists (TicketPoster, TicketStub, Watching this tonight) and let Re-roll step through the list. No server call, no Pitch spent. Handing off to Tonight is harder and worse: the chips ("Like Prisoners", "Stranger") mostly do not map to Tonight's moods and filters, Tonight runs a different ranker, and it can come back with a film that was never on your shortlist.
