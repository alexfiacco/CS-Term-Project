When. The first time you open your own profile after saving, even if Picks showed a first-run thing earlier that launch. Shown once: ×, a swipe down or any send closes it for good. Later, Profile › Share profile opens the same screen.

Send in Montage. One tap sends to that chat, friend or group and the face reads "Sent". Search opens today's friend picker. A film, a list and a pitch go as the card the chat already shows; the archetype, a Blend and a movie night go as a link to the page (chats don't carry images yet).

Elsewhere. Instagram Story: one tap, the card as the Story's background. Instagram post: saves the 4:5 card to Photos and opens Instagram (a toast says so). Messages: the image attached. TikTok: saves and opens TikTok until TikTok's Share Kit is approved. More: the phone's share sheet. On a computer the row is Save Story size, Save post size, Copy link, More.

The card. The poster wall becomes one still from your #1 film in the house grade, fading to black by the middle. The stub replaces the two-line foot. The mix bar goes (the streak line says it in words).
