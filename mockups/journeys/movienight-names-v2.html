How the name works

1. It comes from the room's taste, using the words we already have. As soon as two people are seated, the room gets a name from their archetypes blended together, with lib/group-names.ts's owner-approved words (groupTasteName): "The [second archetype's adjective] [top archetype's crowd]", or "The [crowd]" when one archetype is over 60%. It updates as people join, and stops changing once the host touches it or voting opens. Guests count too (their quick quiz picks); a room with only the host keeps "Friday Night Feature" until someone joins. 2. Shuffle draws the next combination from the same lists (a new seed, the same mix), so it always fits the room. Here the mix is Midnight Cultist 64%, Nihilist 22%, Auteurist 14%. Two new patterns need your OK, because the words are yours: "The {its own adjective} {crowd}" for a room led by one archetype ("The Midnight Cultists", "The After-Hours Night Crawlers") and "{Weekday} {crowd}" ("Friday Cultists", "Friday Night Crawlers"). Without them, this room would only get "The Cultists", "The Night Crawlers", "The Late Shift" and "The Midnight Crowd". 3. Rename: the host only, 28 characters at most, run through the same word filter as group names. Members and guests see the name read-only: under the back row, in the invite ("@hugo invited you to The Midnight Cultists") and on the card. 4. Keep this group is v1 as it is, now offering the room's name. 5. The card swaps "Together we're [archetype]" for the room's own name next to the initials, so the archetype is said once. It drops the director, "5 people · 4 minutes", "4 of 5 voted for it", "86% for the room", the prediction line, where to watch, and "Anyone can join…". It keeps the film, the year, the room's name, the initials, the room's rating once two have rated, and the /mn link with "Start your own". That's 51 words down to 13 before anyone rates it, and 59 down to 17 after (counted on the cards below). The share text that goes with the image stays as it is. No QR, no room IDs, initials only.
