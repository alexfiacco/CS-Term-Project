Montage brand / logo system v5

Frames, tickets and a drawn M

Back to what Montage already owns: the fan of three posters from the marketing edit, and the three-frame film strip used for streaks. Each laid flat is the other. Frames are frames on any screen, so the strip covers film and TV. The M is no longer a font glyph: it is drawn from strokes on a 100 grid.

Construction rules
  • Strip 72 × 48 (3:2) on a 100 grid, corner radius 5, cream #efe7d6.
  • Sprocket holes 6 × 4, radius 1.2, five per edge on a 14.4 pitch, 4 in from the edge; dropped at 32 and below.
  • Three frames 19.33 × 26, gutters 3, radius 1.5: red, blue, red (the streak order).
  • Drawn M: cap height 52; hairlines 3, thick strokes 12 (1 : 4, Bodoni contrast); hairline serifs 1.6.
  • Tick 8 : 1, red then blue. Flat colour only, no effects.
What was cut

The two-fanned-cards M (S3b) was cut: the V read as a gap between two cards, not as a letter. The single-ticket-with-sprockets option for S2 was drawn and cut; it looked like a generic “cinema” clip-art icon. S2 uses the torn strip instead.
