Honest notes

  • Hours are only as good as the logs. Someone who imported Letterboxd without diary dates will see low hours, so hide the number when under about half of their films are dated.
  • "Best film" is the top rating. With ties, pick the latest watch and don't pretend there's more to it.
  • The full Montages recap (mockups/montages/) is a bigger job. This card is the cheap, shareable slice of it, and it doesn't replace it.

Recommendation

Build the year card now, for December. It's seasonal, so it pays off only if it ships by about Dec 1. Its free share also advertises the match link. Add the month card in January, once you can see whether people share the year one. Leave "any range, any time" for Pro.
