Recommendation: (C)

(C) text with a dot is the lightest that still says "switch". It has no fill, no colour and no underline, so it carries the same weight as the row under it, and the two lines read as one quiet block.

Why not the others

  • (A) looks almost as light, but it repeats the profile tabs' underline idiom 56pt lower. Two underline rows is exactly the "second tab bar" the brief wants to avoid; the ink colour only softens it.
  • (B) is the safest read as a toggle, but it is still a box, the heaviest of the three new ones, and its selected half only shows with a hairline added (panel-2 on panel is near invisible).
  • (D) doesn't fit: it needs 87pt more than the row has, still 38pt over with Sort shortened.

Honest risks of (C)

  • Muted words on a line can read as a caption rather than a button. Mitigated by the two numbers and by today's habit (people already know the switch is here); not tested with anyone.
  • Rule 632 says one segmented style everywhere (SegmentPill). (C) is a Watchlist-only control, so it needs the owner's yes as an exception, written into INVARIANTS with its check. (B) would instead restyle SegmentPill everywhere (Tonight, movie night), a wider change.
