Recommendation and what I found
Recommendation

Yes to C, with a solo sample pitch

Why C. Pitch is the newest thing in the app and the only part of Browse that needs teaching: what to type, and that it comes back ranked for you. Today the tour never names it, yet card 6 already shows "Sam sent you a pitch", so a new member meets the word before they know what it is. C fixes that without adding a card (B would make it seven), and the % on card 1 already teaches "scored for you", so card 3 loses the least by changing.

The tweak. Draw C's sample as one person ("Like Arrival but scarier", Sorted for you, one match each) rather than "for me and @Emma". A brand-new account has no friends yet, the drawer's @friend example only shows once there is a friend to pick, and the two-person idea is the very next card (Blend). The solo sample is also the drawer's own first example, so what they see in the tour is what they can do on day one. Both versions are drawn in row two.

Ship it with the card guarded. Card 3 shows Pitch only while Pitch is on (recut.enabled), else today's Browse art. Free accounts get 3 pitches a month, so the art stays an example and never runs one. Closing the tour sets pitchTipSeen, so Browse's "New:" tip does not teach it twice. Keep TOUR_VERSION at 8 (new accounts get it, existing ones already have the tip).

Who sees which card

  • Everyone whose account has not closed version 8 (tour_v below TOUR_VERSION 8), after the reveal, the account and the handle. It opens again from the profile menu, "How Montage works", with a close button.
  • Cards 1 to 5 on both apps. No Skip, Back or Next; card 1 says "Swipe up", the middle cards a faint chevron.
  • Card 6 on the iPhone only, and only while iOS reports notifications as not determined. Then the tour ends on Maybe later / Turn on and has no "See my picks".
  • Under 15 rated every stamp and match reads "?%", with "unlocks at 15 rated · N to go" and Rate N more (cards 1 and 4), and card 6's first note becomes "Did you watch Parasite?".
  • Their own data where it exists: card 1's nine picks and Because you loved, card 3's Every film, card 2's services (saved on tap), card 1's Anything to avoid? (saved).
