b138 · Share your archetype, full screen
Today (build 41, rule 2071) the profile shows a one-time "Share your archetype" ticket card that opens the share drawer (iPhone ShareCardSheet, web TypeShareSheet: the card in a sheet, Story / Post / Profile, then a row of small circles). The owner wants a full-screen pop-up instead: the 9:16 card edge to edge, × at top left, a light bar of round one-tap targets at the foot that scrolls sideways. The card is Montage's own, exactly as /api/share-card?fmt=story draws it (ported from lib/type-card.tsx: the poster wall, "My archetype", the Bodoni name, its tag, the red-and-blue tick, the streak line, the mix bar, five defining films, "What's your archetype?", the address and handle). 1 · The pop-up
A · First profile visit after saving (recommended)
Rises over the profile the first time you open your own profile after the save, before anything else on it. It doesn't wait for the launch's first-run slot (today FirstRunSlot.take makes it wait behind Picks' coach marks, often until the next launch). The pill says what this is; the card says the rest. × or a swipe down closes it for good (settleShare, on every device); so does any send. Shown once.
B · Later, from Share profile (same screen)
The profile's share icon opens this same screen instead of the drawer. The Story / Post switch goes: Instagram post sends the 4:5 Post by itself, every other target the Story. The Criterion profile cover (rule 1238) is page 2: swipe the card sideways (two dots); its Red / Blue flip sits on that page as a small pill. "What's our Blend?" moves to Social (it's already there).
C · With true stats (later)
Up to three lines under the tag, only when each is true and worth saying: films rated (from 100), weekly streak (from 4, WeeklyStreak), top director (from 5 rated by one director). Later, not now: most new accounts have 15 to 60 ratings, which reads small on a card. No percentile ranks ("Top 5%"): at today's user count a percentile rests on too few people to mean anything, and it would move week to week.
2 · Which targets are truly one tap
iPhone (tags: what one tap really does)
1 tap Instagram Story, Messages, Copy link, More. 2 steps Instagram post (no API: save, then open). TikTok is 1 tap with TikTok's Share Kit, 2 steps without it (today).
Phone browser (Safari, Chrome; same screen)
A web page can't open Instagram's or TikTok's composer with an image. Every brand circle opens the phone's share sheet with the card attached (navigator.share({ files })), where Instagram, TikTok and Messages are one more tap. Copy link is one tap. Same row, same order, honest second step.
Computer browser (a centred pop-up, the same parts)
No Instagram or TikTok on a computer, so the brand circles leave: Save Story size, Save post size (downloads), Copy link, and More where the browser has a share menu (Safari on Mac, Edge and Chrome on Windows; hidden elsewhere). Esc, × or a click outside closes. On the first profile visit it opens here too, once.

Recommendation and honest costs
Recommend A + B (C later): one full-screen share screen, opened once on the first profile visit and from Share profile after that. Costs: (1) a full-screen pop-up on the first profile visit interrupts the moment someone came to look at their own profile; it's shown once, and × or a swipe down closes it for good. (2) Opening it on that visit means it no longer waits for the launch's first-run slot, so a new person can see Picks' coach marks and then this in the same launch (rule 2066 says first-run things never stack; this needs an amendment, or the pop-up still waits a launch). (3) The Story / Post switch and the drawer's "What's our Blend?" row go; the profile cover moves to a swipe. (4) Real one-tap Instagram Story needs the Meta app id shipped; one-tap TikTok needs the Share Kit and TikTok's review. Messages with the image and Story as a background image are small code changes in ElsewhereRow. (5) rule 2071's ticket card, rule 1344's drawer and rule 147's "one share frame" all get amended, web and iPhone together.
