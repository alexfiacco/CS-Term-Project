Selected colour, v2

On cream-select-v1 the owner said "you can't really see it" and "we don't have as many blue accents on the app anymore, so it looks more basic with just cream and black and the red accents. So maybe we can tone it." Earlier, for Pitch, they wanted "something more subtle than the blue for selected tags". So the target is a chosen state that is clearly visible, keeps some colour, is calmer than today's blue, and is one style everywhere (today it mixes solid blue fills like Tonight's "Either" with blue outlines like "Any language", "Today" and the person chips). Four options below, each on the same three iPhone screens. Blue data (Blend %, match %, 4.5★, "91% for the room") stays the app's brighter blue #8FB3D9 in every option, so the reader can tell "chosen" from "a number". One red per screen: the ticket's oxblood offset, or Pitch's sentence bar.

Recommendation: B, toned blueA dusty slate blue #7E98B5 (the app's hue, about half the saturation) at 22% fill with a 1.5px outline at 75%, cream text, and solid #4C6683 segments with white text. The outline is 4.0:1 on the panel (Pitch's cream outline is 3.2:1, which is why it vanished), the fill is clearly bluer than an unchosen chip, and the text stays cream, so the only bright blue left on a screen is data.
Why not CBlue text on a blue tint reads well (7.1:1) but turns every chosen word blue, so on Tonight the chosen moods, "Any language" and the Blend % all look like the same kind of thing. It is today's look, slightly greyer.
Why not DCream at 22% with a solid cream outline is visible now (15:1 outline), but it is the "just cream and black" the owner called basic, and a solid cream outline next to the cream ticket competes with it.
One style, three shapesChips and person chips: tint, outline, cream text. Segments (always exactly one chosen): the same blue, solid. Rings, checks, tabs and switches: the toned blue as a line or fill. Pitch's chosen chips move from cream to this too, which is still quieter than today's blue.
