iPhone, Tonight as the moon's drawer (the web's Tonight.tsx is the same order). The amber brackets on phone B are what moves.
The chips, state by state
The four new moods are ordinary mood chips. Three on is the limit; at the limit the rest dim, except the opposite of one that is on, which swaps (the last tap wins, as the segment did).
Which four, and why not sixSlow burn and Fast-paced from Pacing, Intimate and Epic from Scale. Moderate and Medium go: as a filter they keep only films in the middle, which nobody asks for by mood. Check filter_set usage before removing them; if anyone uses them, they can stay in Browse only.
The limitToday there is no mood limit in code: the server keeps up to six (feelFrom, slice(0, 6)) and Pacing and Scale ride beside them. Proposed: 3 in all, the four new ones counting. "3 of 3" beside the label once there; the dimmed chips are disabled (like Who's watching's friends at 3). The server cap moves to 3 to match.
OppositesSlow burn and Fast-paced, Intimate and Epic. Tapping one while its opposite is on swaps them, never refuses, so the limit never blocks a swap. Owner question: should Light & easy and Disturbing, or Cozy and Scary, be opposites too? Not proposed here.
BuildNo new server words: the chips slow, fast, intimate, epic still send mood.pacing and mood.scale. iPhone: TonightOptions.moods gains the four (after Disturbing), moreGroup drops Pacing, Scale and Age rating, moreSet and the "and N more" line follow. Web: FEEL_MOODS in Tonight and MoodQuestions' More filters. The movie night room (its own Pacing, Scale and Age rating) should follow in the same change.
