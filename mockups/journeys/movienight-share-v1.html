Movie Night: share the result

Every night that lands on a film ends on a card anyone in the room can send: the host, a member, or a guest with no account. One tap opens the phone's share sheet with the image and a link to start your own movie night (montagefilm.app/mn). The link never carries this night's room. No QR code. The image is drawn on the server (the same renderer as every other share card), so a guest's phone gets the same card as the host's, and the web and the iPhone share the same picture.

Where each line comes from

  • Poster, title, year: the decided film (the host's tie choice first, else the most votes, else the room's top pick), as the room shows it.
  • N people: seats that were there (the same test as the next day's "How was it?"). M minutes: room opened to locked in; left off past 2 hours (a host who walked away) or when either time is missing.
  • Group: the room's group archetype from its saved picks, 2+ people only.
  • Agreed: "K of N voted for it". NN% for the room only with 2+ signed-in members (rule 203); never a guest's number.
  • Where to watch: the first streaming service in the sharer's country, else "Rent on …"; left off when none.

Privacy and the link

  • No names, no handles: faces are initials only, on every card.
  • The link is montagefilm.app/mn, never /t/<room> (owner rule). Signed in with a profile, it opens a fresh room; otherwise the page says what movie night is and starts with an account.
  • The image address carries the room (to draw it) but is never the shared link; it shows nothing a seat in the room does not already show, and it is not indexed.
  • No QR code (owner, 2026-11-04: "share card yes, not QR rn").

Tracked, and the switch

  • mn_card_opened {role: host | member | guest}, mn_card_shared {format, how: share | save | copy, role}.
  • mn_card_landed on /mn, and account_created's source reads card for a visit that came in through it, so "guests who later made an account" and "nights started from a card" (room_created {from: "card"}) can be counted.
  • Ships ON with a kill switch (MN_SHARE_CARD=off hides the row on web and iPhone and the image answers 404).
  • Copy keeps to the brief: never "taste match", never "Start a Blend", no "no app".
