  • Progress block on the invite sheet: S

  • iPhone install attribution (a "Who invited you?" step): L

  • In all, about M+ (2 weeks), mostly attribution

Honest notes

  • The hard part is counting, not the reward. Today the attribution is one localStorage key on the web, so on iPhone most real joins would be missed and the progress bar would look broken.
  • Unlocking a bigger Canon touches taste weighting. If the extra five count at full weight, a referral changes your recommendations.
  • At today's user count, few people will reach three. Expect a handful of unlocks a month until there's more traffic.

Recommendation

Build it after the match link, with reward A. The match link creates the links worth counting, and A is permanent and personal. Skip B for now, because it only makes sense once Plus exists. Ship the progress block and server-side attribution first, and leave iPhone install attribution until the numbers justify it.
