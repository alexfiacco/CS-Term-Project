b123 · One short step after an import's reveal
People who import 15 or more films skip the taste deck: import, reading, archetype reveal, Picks. The import can't tell us where they watch, or what they want less of, so this adds one screen between the reveal and Picks: where you watch, more of this, less of this. Recommended title: "Before your picks", under a "Tune your picks" label

"Two quick things before your picks" is off by one: the screen has three parts. "Before your picks" says where you are and that the picks are next, in three words. The caps label above it, Tune your picks, is b122's page name, so the footer line ("You can change all of this in Tune your picks.") points at a name they've just seen.

Also considered: "Tune your picks" as the title (clear, but sounds like work right after the reveal); "Three quick things" (a count to keep honest whenever a part is hidden, as theaters are outside the US); "Almost there" (b36's label, says nothing).

The Less of this rule (database only, no model call)

For an import of 15 or more rated, a genre is offered when its share of the person's rated films is half its share of the Picks pool or less (pool here: the 3,774 films with 100,000+ watches), up to four, rarest first, all off. Volume only. Tapping one sets the same soft "Show less of" key as Settings (rule 1417: rank points down, never hidden). No genre is singled out; Animation is offered only to someone who rarely watches it.

Open questions for the owner

1. "Where do you watch?" twice. Today a new account's first save asks "Where do you watch?" before the walkthrough (rule 1412). An importer would now answer here first. Recommend: answering or skipping here marks it asked (SERVICES_ASKED_KEY), so it is never asked twice.

2. Guests. An import can run before an account exists. Recommend holding the answers like the old flow's held prefs (flushHeldPrefs) and writing them at the save.

3. More of this source. Today's leans are the "What you're into" chips (b122). Recommend the step shows the top five of those same leans, so × here and × on the page are one setting. If the build has fewer than three, the section hides.

4. Thresholds. Half the pool's share and the 100,000-watch pool are guesses, checked on the owner's library only. Before building, run them across every import of 15+ and see how many get zero, one or four suggestions.

5. Outside the US. Theaters hide (rule 1564), and the step is two parts plus services. The title still works.

What it costs and changes

No model or API call: counts come from the import's own films joined to films.genres, at the build. Writes the same keys as Settings (viewing-prefs services, theaters, notMine; the leans list). iPhone and web in the same task (parity rule). Data-science log entry needed for the Less of this rule.
