Can I watch it tonight? Answered in Browse

Today, where a film is streaming shows only on its film page, and Browse hides streaming inside the Filters sheet. Proposed: three chips at the top of Browse results, On my services (your services, as saved for Tonight), In theaters and Free. Each card gets one button that does the thing: "Watch on Netflix" opens the app, "Tickets · 7:40pm" opens the cinema's booking page. A quiet hint says when a film is about to leave a service. This is the film version of "open now" and "reserve".

Today · Browse results

Posters and your match. To know if you can watch one tonight, you open each film, or dig into Filters › Streaming.

Proposed · On my services

Chips above the results; "On my services" is on. Each card says where, and the button deep-links to it. "Leaving Dec 1" shows for the last 30 days.

Proposed · In theaters

Near you, tonight. The button is the next showtime ("Tickets · 7:40pm"); the times under it are the rest of tonight. Repertory screenings count.

Proposed · Free

Free with ads, or free with a library card. Same card, the button says which.

Open for you

Showtimes data: TMDB has streaming providers (already used) but no showtimes, so "In theaters" needs a showtimes source or a partner. Ship On my services and Free first. Deep links: straight into the service's app where it allows, otherwise its web page. Grid or list: drawn as a list once a chip is on, since the button needs room; the grid stays the default.
