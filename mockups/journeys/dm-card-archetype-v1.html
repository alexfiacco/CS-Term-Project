A cooler film card in DMs, and archetypes by names

Two parts. Part 1 redraws the film card a 1:1 chat shows when someone shares a film (today: GroupFeed.swift filmCard, web GroupThread.tsx .gth-card, on origin/main), four ways, from the same thread with Sam, with the owner's addition: a film the reader has already seen says Rewatch, not Save. Part 2 answers "should we have archetype by users names in some places??" with where names show today, where the archetype already sits, and four spots drawn with and without it. People are made up; ratings read "5★" (Stars.short, commit bc0078ce, build 21).

Part 1 · The DM film card

What the card says now

  • A form, not a film: an outlined box (blue when sent, grey when received), a 64pt poster, then a ruled footer split by a second divider into Save | Details.
  • Details repeats what a tap on the card already does (the poster and title both open the film).
  • "sent by Jacob" in a 1:1 chat says what the side of the screen already says.
  • Save says Save even for a film you rated 5★. Only VoiceOver hears "Add to your rewatch list".

What every option keeps

  • Sent sits right, received left; mine reads blue (a blue-tinted card, or the blue bubble in D), theirs grey, like the text bubbles.
  • The line is always the reader's own (viewerFilmCards): unseen, the blue match stamp "91% for you"; seen, "Rated 5★" as a straight cream stamp. Sam opening the same card sees his.
  • A tap anywhere on the card opens the film. One save action, no Details, no dividers. Hold still reacts, swipe still replies.

The one red

  • The oxblood offset goes on the newest film card's poster only (as Blend puts it on the first new pick). Older cards sit flat, so a thread of shares never has two reds.
  • C's red is the C1 dash under its kicker instead, again on the newest card only.
  • Recommended: A, Print. It is the film share card shrunk to a chat, keeps the thread readable, and is the smallest change to build.
Save or Rewatch, on the card (the owner: "save should say rewatch list or something for already watched films")

The four states of the card's one action (option A drawn)

Same list underneath, as the film page: one watchlist; a seen film on it is a rewatch, and the profile's Watchlist tab already splits To Watch / Rewatches (RewatchCopy.swift, lib/rewatch-copy.ts). The film page's button reads Rewatch once seen (FilmScreen.swift, FilmActions.tsx) and fills when on. The card follows: seen = rated or marked seen, by the reader.

How it shows

  • One small grey line under the handle: Inter 12.5, --faint. Never blue, never Bodoni, never a chip. The name stays the loudest thing in the row.
  • Gated as everywhere else: only when namesArchetype says the profile has earned it (15 rated, FULL_ARCHETYPE_AT, rule 239; the same 15 as rule 202's match gate). Under it the APIs send "Just getting started"; in these compact rows show nothing instead, so a new person's row is simply shorter (Leo, below).
  • The display name through displayArchetype, so legacy labels read as today's (Tenderhearted, Comic Relief).
  • Data: search, suggestions and follow lists already carry it (search-profiles, people-lists, follows). Blend and Watch with need the label added to their payloads.
