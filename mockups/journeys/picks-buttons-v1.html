Picks stub, with less text

The cream ticket stub under every Picks card answers the film three ways: Seen it, Watchlist (On watchlist once saved) and Not for me. The owner asked whether the words could go. Now is build 28's iPhone stub (64 tall, icon 20 over sans 15). A drops the words; B keeps them small under the icons; C sets each icon beside its word on one line. Each phone is a For You card for The Conversation; under it, the same stub in the states it has to carry: answered, on Last night's card (where × means Didn't watch) and the longest words it shows. Red dashed rule: the stub's top.

56 tall, the icon 20 over its word in sans 13 semibold, 4 between (the web's size). The same words and states as Now; the stub is 8 shorter and quieter, and the poster gets the 8.

48 tall, icon 18 then the word in sans 15 semibold on one line, 8 between. Each third is 119 wide, so a longer word shrinks (to 80% at most, as the code allows) and, past that, clips (drawn red).

Recommendation: B

B does what the owner is after: the stub is lighter and shorter (56, was 64), but the words stay. That matters more here than on most icon rows, because the words carry state the icons can't: Seen ★★★★ shows the rating you just gave, On watchlist versus Watchlist is the only difference besides a filled bookmark, and a film you've seen saves to the Rewatch list. Sans 13 is the web stub's size already, so B also brings the two platforms together.

A is the cleanest picture, but it reads worst. The eye reads as "preview" or "watch the trailer" as easily as "I've seen it" (the poster sits right above it with a Trailer pill on it). The × reads as "close" or "skip", not "never show me this", and Not for me is the one answer that changes your Picks. On Last night's card the stub carries Didn't watch with the same ×, and Not yet is a clock, which on its own reads as "remind me". Seen's stars disappear. Press and hold helps nobody who doesn't know it's there, and nothing on Picks teaches it.

If the words must go

Ship A only with a first-use label: the words show under the icons until each answer has been used once (or for the first three cards), then fade to icons. Keep the VoiceOver labels and the press-and-hold label. Don't drop the words on Last night's card: its × and clock mean different things from the usual stub, so it keeps B's words.

Why not C

One line looks tidy at the default text size, but each third is only 119 wide. On watchlist, Didn't rewatch and Seen ★★★★½ don't fit at 15 next to an icon (widths below), so they'd shrink (the code's 0.8 minimum) or clip. At larger Dynamic Type every word wraps. B stacks the icon and word, so each word has the whole third.

Build notes (for whichever is picked)

One change in TicketPart (iPhone) and .pk-stub button (web); Picks and Have you seen these? share the stub (rule 609), so both change.
