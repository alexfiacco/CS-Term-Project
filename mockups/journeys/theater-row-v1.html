Theater row v1: "In theaters" as a row like the others

On the film page's Where to watch (rule 542), a film that's only in cinemas gets an "In theaters" row first. It is the one row that breaks the list-row rule from film-page-v8 (rule 1099): its tile is 36pt and oxblood where every logo is 44pt, so its name starts 8pt left of the others, and "Showtimes" sits alone on the far right in grey. Below, today against two rows built like the rest: tile, name, and a grey line under it, with the whole row one tap to Fandango's showtimes.

Recommendation: (b), "Opened Nov 1 · Showtimes ›"

It keeps what today's row already tells you (how long it's been out, which hints at how long it will stay) and still ends on the action, in blue, where every other row puts its kind. It reuses today's copy ("Opened Nov 1", or "Now playing" with no date) rather than "In theaters since Nov 1", which would repeat the name above it. (a) is cleaner but loses the date, and "near you" promises a location the link doesn't use yet (Fandango picks the city). Both drop the oxblood tile: red stays an accent elsewhere on the page, and the tile matches the logos at 44pt so all names start on one line. The web's TheaterRow (Result.tsx) changes the same way: the right-hand "Showtimes" goes and the line under the name carries it.
