Send, not share, where it opens Send to

The owner approved the paper plane (Lucide send) on every button whose drawer opens with Send to: friends and groups first, then Watch together, then Elsewhere. A button that only hands a link or an image to the phone's share sheet keeps the share icon. On the iPhone the swap is one line for most of them (ShareCircle draws Glyph.share); on the web it is one line in ShareFilmButton plus four direct buttons. Recommendations

  • The swap. iPhone: add Glyph.send = .send, rename ShareCircle to SendCircle drawing it (its five uses all open a Send to drawer), and swap the four direct Icon(.share)/Glyph.share calls in the send list. Web: ShareFilmButton draws send (covers seven places), plus Lately, Canon, Share your cut and the hold menu. Spoken names go from "Share …" to "Send …".
  • The two planes on Social: no fix needed. Both mean direct messages: the bar's opens them, a post's writes one, and the drawer says where it lands. The size and weight already separate them (bar 20 in ink with a count, top right; post 18 muted, inside the post with heart and comment, the order people know from Instagram). Keep the inbox fallback in the drawer only if a test shows people tapping a post's plane to reach their chats.
  • One mismatch to settle (flagged): the film's hold menu "Share" opens Send to on the web (PosterHold) but the system share sheet on the iPhone (FilmContextMenu). Make the iPhone open FilmShareSheet too, then both read "Send" with the plane.
  • Lists: there are no user lists with a share button. Lane and theme pages (Browse) share their link only, so they keep the share icon.
  • Inside the drawers the outside-share controls keep the share icon (the pitch drawer's Share button, Elsewhere's More), so the plane always opens Send to and share always leaves the app.
