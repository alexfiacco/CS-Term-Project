b112 · Top bar pages: filters and ⋯ go up top
b109 put one row on every grid (count ▾ · full sort name ▾ · filters · view · ⋯), and on some pages it runs over at 375pt. The owner's direction: on a page that has a top bar (a user list, a lane, theme or person page, a made-for-you collection), the filters icon and the ⋯ move into the top bar, at the right, and the row under the header becomes count ▾ · Sort ▾ (its full name) · view icon. Tabbed screens (Browse › Every film, Profile › Films, Watchlist) keep the full b109 row: their top is the tabs, with nowhere to put icons. A list page also trades "24 films" for You've seen 12 of 24, a muted stat with a thin blue line, and a ranked list numbers its posters.
