First-run journey · 3 of 5 · second pass

Film DNA forming, without the clutter

The rating deck's only job is the next rating. The draft put a 66pt radar, a strand line, a second progress bar and "9 to go" into a card at the foot, which also pushed the poster down from 200pt to 150pt. This pass keeps the radar but makes it small and quiet: it only moves when a rating moves it, and you tap it if you want a closer look.

What's cluttered in the draft

  • Progress shown three times: "6/15 to reveal" with the header bar, the card's own bar, and "Archetype at 15 · 9 to go".
  • A second headline. "Intensity, slow burn" in Bodoni sits under the film's title in the same face, so the two compete.
  • A smaller poster. The docked card shrinks it from 200 to 150pt (TasteDeckScreen, FilmSlide), and the poster is the thing being rated.
  • Always on. The pulse ring and the dashed outline draw the eye on every card, even when nothing changed.

The rule for this pass

  • One progress readout: the header's count and bar, as built.
  • No words for the DNA before 15. The strand words and the genre chips both go.
  • The poster stays at 200pt until 15, because nothing docks at the foot before then.
  • It moves only on change. After a rating, the strand that moved glows for 0.8s and then rests. Nothing animates while you're deciding.
