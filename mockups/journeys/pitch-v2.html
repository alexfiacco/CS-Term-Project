  • The poster wall is your watchlist (or Trending when it’s empty): posters already cached, tilted and dimmed behind a Display 40 title. It doesn’t move, so it costs nothing.
  • Typewriter placeholder: the 12 approved pitch lines (rule 1173) type out and erase one by one, with a cream caret. It stops the moment you tap the box, and stays still with Reduce Motion on.
  • Previous takes shrink to one row of clock chips under the box, so the ideas move up. The ideas become numbered script lines, which is calmer than a stack of pills.
  • Both options: Action! goes solid cream (the page’s one primary button), and the tick sits under the title block (accent-everywhere-v1).

Decide

  1. B’s poster wall and typewriter, with A’s index cards for the writers’ room Rec
    The wall gives the most atmosphere for no load time. The index cards are the most “Montage” of the ideas and match the tick.

  3. A as drawn: the slate (clapper bar, Scene / Take / Cast), script-style takes, index cards.
  4. Just two changes: Action! solid cream and the typewriter placeholder. Everything else stays.
