Choices made. The subline names the main choices, then counts the extra filters ("+3 filters"; a single filter is named, e.g. "Under 90m"). The More filters heading shows "3 set · Reset". Picking Friends shows the faces and the movie-night link right beside them.

Fold or list: list them, no fold

The owner said "make the user scroll", and once the ticket is pinned the fold has no job left: its only purpose was to keep the ticket close. Recommend a plain "More filters" heading with the eight sections under it. Put Length first because it's the one people reach for, then Genre, New or rewatch, Pacing, Scale, Decade, Language and Age rating. The heading peeks above the bar at the top of the drawer, which is enough of a hint to scroll.

Cost: the drawer is now always full height rather than fitting its content. That's the trade the owner asked for. The header and ticket are pinned, so the drawer never feels endless.

"or just pick for me": under the ticket, inside the bar

It's one 13px line under the ticket and adds 30pt to the bar. Putting it inside the ticket would split one button into two targets. Putting it at the end of the scroll would hide it again. With nothing chosen the ticket itself is "Just pick for me" and the line is dropped, so the bar is 64pt plus padding at the top of the drawer and about 128pt in all once something is set.

Subline rule: occasion (if not Just me), then mood, then services, then extra filters ("+N filters", or the name when there is one), truncating with an ellipsis. Nothing chosen gives "Picks for you".

Movie night: move it beside Who's watching

"Watching with others?" asks the same question as the segmented control right above it. Recommend dropping the bottom line. When Date night, Friends or Family is picked, the friend faces and a one-line "Let them vote too? Start a movie night" link appear under the control, which is where the code already puts them. "Just me" shows nothing. This also keeps the end of the scroll clean, with Age rating as the last item.
