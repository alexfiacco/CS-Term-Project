The film runs, refined: three cuts, and where it could live in the app

Every phone is 390 by 844 and runs the real timing. Fast path: unchanged from v1 (picks ready by the time the fan opens; no film). Slow path: picks arrive at 5.0s here (the live cap is 6s). The side cards never move. Picks loads under the overlay from the first frame, and the overlay only listens (rule 611).

Fixed in all three, over v1: the sprocket holes and the frame lines now move as one strip (in v1 they ran at two pitches and slid against each other); the frames carry a faint light, a small glow crossing a dim horizon, frame to frame; the run starts with a spin up instead of all at once; it stops on a whole frame with a tiny settle, and the light fades out so the resting card is the FanGlyph exactly. The fan then flies to the header 0.2s after the picks arrive (was at once), so the stop reads before it shrinks.

Reduce Motion, in every variant: no rise, no spring, no film, no light, no flicker. The lockup is still, fades in over 250ms and out over 300ms when Picks is ready (0.6s at the earliest), exactly as live today. Use the toggle above.

The streak icon, and the film, as the app's loaders

What the app has today. The in-app MontageLoader (iPhone Design/Loading.swift, web MontageLoader.tsx) is the fan turning on its Y axis every 2.6s: the same coin turn v1 called the cheesy part. About 20 places use it. Pull to refresh is the system spinner on iPhone (.refreshable on Picks, Profile, Social, Group) and a plain arc on the web (PullToRefresh.tsx). Pitch shows only "Pitching…" on its button while it works, often 3 to 6 seconds.

The fan (FanGlyph)

Three film-strip cards. Its centre card is the film that runs. FanGlyphView on iPhone, FanMark.tsx on the web.

The streak icon (FilmStrip)

A 30 by 20 cream strip, five sprocket holes top and bottom, three frames red, blue, red. On the "N-week streak" pill and its sheet. FilmStrip in ProfileScreen.swift, StreakStrip in WeeklyStreak.tsx. There is no flame.

The plain spinners

ProgressView() inside about 40 buttons and two search fields on iPhone; .spinner in buttons on the web. These stay.

The idea: one film language for every wait. The fan's centre card runs its film where there is room (40pt and up); the streak strip runs sideways where a wait sits in a row or a gap (it is wide and short, and its holes still read at 26 to 30pt, where the fan's frame lines are under a pixel). Both loop at the launch's speed and spin up the same way.
