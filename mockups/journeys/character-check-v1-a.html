Character check: did we lose it?

The owner asked whether Montage now looks too modern, too basic. Today's changes mostly took things away (divider lines, boxes, outlines, red offsets and stripes, the landing eyebrow, more left-alignment, fewer words). Each was right alone. This checks every main screen for the brand's signature moves and grades it: Clearly Montage, Some character or Could be any app. Part a: the verdict, the kit, and the seven screens that still clearly read as Montage. Part b: the ten that drifted, each with a light-touch fix beside it (one or two moves, no new features, no layout changes, no lines back).
Where each picture comes from (labelled on every frame): owner's phone = the owner's own iPhone screenshots from Nov 4 and 5 (real); next dev = Playwright on origin/main (e38876a0) with the real app/globals.css, posters and data blocked in this sandbox; drawn = redrawn in HTML from the Swift / TSX code and the shipped mocks (approximate). A few owner screenshots are a day older than main; where the code has moved on since (list rows, film page v8, Movie Night v4), the frame is drawn from the code instead.

Five touches, by impact

  1. Movie Night invite as a ticket. The room's name on a dark ticket with a torn stub, the red Admit and an oxblood spine. It is the first Montage screen a friend ever sees from a link, and today it is a numbered list. Part b, Movie Night guest.
  2. Film page below the stars: serif section heads and the stamp. "Your watches" and "People you follow" as the Bodoni 20 head the mocks already use, and "Spot on" as the tilted blue stamp. The most-opened page keeps its voice past the fold.
  3. Social Groups: Bodoni group names, one spine. The style guide already says group names are serif; today they are sans. The first "Start one with" card gets the oxblood spine as the screen's hero.
  4. Movie Night host: "Tonight's room" as an Admit stub. The room starts with the ticket it ends with (Admit 5), and the room's name in the italic. Ties the four steps together.
  5. The italic voice on three reading lines. The first sentence of Our read, the lane's mood line, Blend's "Cinematic soulmates". Same words, same space, the brand's voice where people actually read.

Then, smaller: an oxblood spine beside the lead row in list view; Privacy borrows About's short oxblood mark and the italic; the logo and wordmark at the foot of Settings.
Not touched: landing, Picks, Tonight, the grid, the film's top, the profile. They are the reference for the rest.
