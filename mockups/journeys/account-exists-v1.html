This account already exists

A guest finishes onboarding, sees the reveal, then saves with an Apple ID, Google account or email that already has a Montage account. Today the web and iPhone offer only "sign in to it instead" (the guest profile is left behind, nothing is merged) or "keep my guest profile". Proposed: say whose account it is, count both sides, and offer merge as the default.
