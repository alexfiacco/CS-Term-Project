# b115: the streak sentence on the reveal

On the reveal, the description of the main archetype gets one more sentence when there's a streak.

- The main archetype's tagline comes first, then the streak's sentence.
- It's the same line and the same Newsreader font.
- If there's no streak, nothing is added.
- If the runner-up is "close behind", it gets the same sentence.

Each sentence is written to follow any main tagline, so none of them uses the archetype's name. The "with a Nihilist streak" line under the name already says who the streak is.

| Streak archetype | Streak sentence |
|---|---|
| Disciple | And you follow a director from one film to the next. |
| Globetrotter | And you'll happily read subtitles to get there. |
| Old Soul | And the older the print, the better. |
| Aesthete | And how it looks matters as much as what happens. |
| Nihilist | And it lands better when the ending doesn't let you off. |
| Midnight Cultist | And you save room for something strange after dark. |
| Hardboiled | And you like a hard-edged crime story on the side. |
| IMAX Idealist | And the bigger the screen, the better. |
| Eyewitness | And you're drawn to what really happened. |
| Tenderhearted | And you're there for the people in it. |
| Animation Devotee | And you take animation as seriously as anything else. |
| Voyager | And you'll go anywhere a story takes you. |
| Comic Relief | And you always make room for a laugh. |
| Lovestruck | And a love story gets you every time. |
| Overthinker | And bonus points if it takes two watches to understand. |
| Contrarian | And you're happy to disagree with the crowd. |
| Nostalgic | And you keep going back to the ones you grew up on. |

## Examples, as they'd read

- **Overthinker, with a Nihilist streak:** "Always piecing it together. You leave with a theory. And it lands better when the ending doesn't let you off."
- **Voyager, with an IMAX Idealist streak:** "Other worlds, and the journey there. And the bigger the screen, the better."
- **Nihilist, with an Overthinker streak:** "Drawn to the dark and unresolved. And bonus points if it takes two watches to understand."
- **Tenderhearted, with a Comic Relief streak:** "Moved by people. And you always make room for a laugh."
- **Hardboiled, with a Disciple streak:** "Tough, cynical crime stories. And you follow a director from one film to the next."
