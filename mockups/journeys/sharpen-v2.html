Progress and unlocks · Sharpen your profile v2 · one home

Every unlock lives in Sharpen your profile

The owner: "all of these ideas should be in Sharpen your profile… not something different. Unlocking Film DNA and stuff is part of it." So there is one home for progress: Profile's Sharpen row and its drawer (rule 84). The "Your taste so far" list (TasteSoFar, 15 / 30 / 50) goes, and there is no separate hub. The small hints elsewhere (the Picks bar, the deck's locked DNA, "Sam needs 4 more") stay where they are and open Sharpen when tapped. The owner has not answered the open questions yet, so everything below uses the recommended defaults, each marked Proposal.

After 30: keep a minimal row, not the full one

Why not keep the full row: after 30 there is nothing left to fill. A ring with no goal is a level meter in disguise, and the 50 / 100 / 150 goals it used to chase are model limits, not things you can see.

Why not remove it: it is the one home. Import, "What's our match?" and friends who are waiting still live in the drawer, and the in-place hints need somewhere to open. If the row vanished at 30, those would lose their home just when people have the most friends to compare with.

So: one quiet line, roughly 48pt (about 66 today), with no cream button. ✕ retires it for good, as today (montage_hint_seen:setup), and Edit profile's "Show setup steps" brings it back. The old auto-retire at 150 goes, since 150 is no longer a goal. Proposal

A friend waiting is the one thing that can bring back a second line after 30. Nothing brings back the cream button.
