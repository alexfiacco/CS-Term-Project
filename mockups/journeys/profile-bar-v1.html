Profile bar v1

The user profile's bar (v19c) in its two states. At the top it has no ground of its own and sits on the backdrop. Once the page scrolls under it, it turns solid page black from the top of the screen down, with a hairline at its foot, so the bell, ☰, Back and ••• never sit on top of moving text or posters.

The bar

  • One row, 44, both states. Yours: the @handle in sans 15/600 on the 16 edge, then the bell and ☰. Someone's: Back, the @handle beside it, then •••. Every glyph is 24 in a 44 target.
  • At the top. No fill: the backdrop's own dark top band (page black .8 fading to 0 over 96) keeps the glyphs legible, as v19c. Without a backdrop the bar sits on plain page black, so the two states look the same.
  • Scrolled. As soon as anything passes under the bar, the bar and the status bar above it fill solid --bg (one block from the top of the screen, never see-through), with a 1px --line hairline at the foot. It fades in over 0.2s (the shared collapsing bar's timing) and goes back to clear at the top. The @handle stays put in both states, so nothing jumps.
