When it shows

100+ logged and under 50% rated

Recommended: 100 or more logged, under 50% rated, and at least 40 seen-but-unrated films that matched the catalog.

  • The 40 make sure 20 informative ones exist after the filters below. Once per import (montage_hint_seen:import-sharpen); a re-import that crosses the line again does not reopen it.
  • Live, imported profiles: 8 of 10 have 100+ logged. Under 50% rated: 1 (35%, 1,126 logged). Under 60%: 3 (35%, 55%, 57%). The other 5 are 83% to 99% rated.
  • 50% keeps it for libraries where unrated watches carry real weight in the read (the one model weighs unrated films by the unrated share, scoring v49). 60% would also catch two people with 700+ unrated each; the Explore tile "Rate what you've seen" (50+ unrated) already reaches them later.
  • Under 30 rated, page 1's firm-up card takes the slot; with a big library it is the same card, running on to 20.
How the 20 are picked

Informativeness, from data we have

  • Unsure of it: lib/taste/predict-match.ts's predicted rating sits near their own average, or its two halves disagree. A film we cannot call teaches most.
  • Central to their lanes: films.embedding close to their taste centroid (profiles.embedding), and the film's archetype lane among their top lanes and streaks (lib/taste/archetypes.ts).
  • Directors they return to: films.directors against profiles.director_affinity, or 3+ of that director already rated in the library.
  • Splits the crowd: no per-film rating spread is stored today. v1 uses the gap between films.letterboxd_avg_rating and tmdb_vote_average, and the spread of Montage's own user_ratings where 5+ people rated it. A true spread needs a histogram at catalog ingest (new data; not needed for v1).
  • Spread the 20: greedy, at most 2 per director, covering the nine strands (dna-labels.ts film labels), no film the deck has shown before.
  • From 15, rule 853 lets the card say why ("A director you return to", "In your slow-burn horror lane"); the line names one true reason, never a guess.
The end state

Only claim what moved

  • Before the deck, keep their tasteDnaV2 strand scores. When it closes, the profile rebuilds as any rating batch does (identity refresh), and we compare.
  • A strand moved when its score changes by 3 or more (the radar's smallest step you can see). One or more moved: "Your read is sharper: N strands moved" with those strands, up or down. The count is the real diff.
  • None moved: "Done. Your picks keep learning", and a plain line that it held steady. With 412 rated this is the likely case, which is why both screens exist.
  • A rebuild that renames the archetype goes through the existing rename copy (identity flip), not this screen. Stopped early (say 12 of 20): the same end, counted on 12.
  • No confetti, no badge; the oxblood rule is the one red.

What changes

The import gate only, not the engine

  • Keep lib/taste/profile.ts MIN_RATED_FILMS = 10: it decides whether a library is read at all (status complete). Raising it would turn stored 10 to 14 builds and quiz reads into insufficient_data on their next rebuild. The display gates are already 15.
  • Add lib/taste/maturity.ts IMPORT_READ_AT = MATCH_SHOW_AT (15), and Swift's ViewerRated.matchShowAt.
  • Web app/_components/HomeShell.tsx: drop the copied MIN_RATED_FILMS = 10 (L5; maturity.ts is client-safe, FilmGate and MatchUnlock import it); at L1440 to 1460 a complete result under IMPORT_READ_AT goes to the gate and deck, and the "needs at least 10" message goes.
  • Web lib/taste-test/import-seed.ts: attach the seed under IMPORT_READ_AT, not only on insufficient_data; seen films carry their log date. The two routes that call it are unchanged.
  • iOS ImportScreen.swift (L775 to 790): the literal 10 and the message go; TasteDeckModel takes the ordering and the tag.
  • Unchanged: ImportFacts.swift L129 (a fact's own minimum), TENSION_MIN_RATED (10), STEP_ONE_AT / STEP_TWO_AT.
  • Rules: 202 and 239 stand. A new rule records the top-up and narrows the owner's 2026-11-01 "a sparse import is a first look" (option A): under 15 that first look becomes the Not now path.
