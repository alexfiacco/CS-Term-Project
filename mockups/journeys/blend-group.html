Blend · 5 of 6 · groups

A movie night can end with "Invite Jo to Blend"

A group's Blend works the same way as a pair's, for everyone in it. It's offered once, at the moment the night is decided and everyone is happy. If the night came from a group, the Blend is kept for that group. If it was a one-off room, keeping it makes a group of the people in it, since a kept Blend is built on a group (see the decision page). The list lives at the top of the group's Watch tab. Every pick shows each person's number: inline up to four people, as a row of faces from five.

Rule
Up to 4 people: names inline, "You 91 · Jo 88 · Sam 84 · Mara 72", you first and in blue. From 5: a row of faces with each number under it, the lowest in grey. More than 7: the 6 lowest plus you, then "+3".
  • SThe numbers are the movie night's own perMember scores (rule 880's room order), so the Blend and the vote never disagree about a film.

Changes and effort

  • The Keep card on a decided night, web and iPhone: S
  • Room to group for a one-off room (members from the seats, asks to the others): M
  • The Blend section on the Watch tab, both score layouts: M

Honest notes

  • Making a group from a room is the riskiest step: it creates a social object people didn't name. It gets a plain default name ("Friday's four") that anyone can rename, and anyone who says Not now is simply not in it.
  • Seven faces with numbers is dense at phone width. It holds at 22px faces and 11px numbers, but go no further than 7.
  • Group picks are the most expensive to build (one score per member per candidate). They're cached per Blend and rebuilt only when something real happens.

Recommendation

Offer Keep once, on the decided night. Put the group's Blend at the top of Watch, not in a new tab. Use the same scores as the room.
