"Your take" after rating, made inviting

Today the take is a small one-line field under "When did you watch it?" (rule 612), easy to miss and silent about what you just rated. Popcorn puts a whole screen after rating for the same thing. The owner: "Isn't that just your take, which we have? It can just look better." So nothing new is built: same drawer, same /api/film-takes "post" with body, audience and spoiler, same 500-character cap, same Everyone check. What changes is the look: the film you just rated at the top, a headline keyed to your rating, a real text box, one line on where it shows, and a cream ticket to post it.

Recommendation: B. Each step asks one thing. Step 1 is the date question people already know, still one tap. Step 2 gives the take its own moment with the film in view, and it fits above the keyboard, which A does not (A's two headings compete, and the date scrolls under the header while typing). It costs one extra tap (Skip or a swipe) for people who don't write, which is the point: the take is offered, not buried.
