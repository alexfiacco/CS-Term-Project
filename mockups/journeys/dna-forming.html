Recommendation: build it, but keep it humble. This is the strongest of the five as a signature moment: it turns a progress bar into something that is visibly yours, and it is the one piece of onboarding Popcorn has no answer to. Two honest risks. First, from 5 to 10 ratings the shape will swing a lot, and a radar looks more certain than chips do; the dashed outline and "so far" are there for that, and it should not show strand percentages until 15. Second, the deck's estimate (probe films only) and the reveal's read (the full model) can disagree, so the shape may jump at the reveal. Either accept a visible "settle" animation there or draw the reveal's radar from the same probe estimate until the build is in.

Keep the poster wall lighting up behind it. Drop the radar on the smallest phones (under 700pt tall) where the poster already shrinks to 150pt.
