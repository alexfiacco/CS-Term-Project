Every sub-drawer, same shape

  • Who's watching: friend chips with faces (up to 3, a "3 of 3" count), then "Rank by" as three one-line options: Both like it, Disagree, Emma enjoyed it.
  • Streaming: your service chips with logos, "+ Add", then Stream, Free, Rent, Buy as chips; "Data from JustWatch" as an 11pt credit at the foot.
  • Status: Any, Unseen, Seen (segment), then Saved, Not saved, Rated, Seen, not rated.
  • Every sub-drawer ends in the same bar: quiet Clear for that filter, cream Show N films that closes everything.
  • Mood: as shown; the 18 chips, "2 of 3", Kind of funny when Funny is on.
  • Genre: 18 chips. Decade: decade chips, a picked one adds "Years" chips. Length: Any, Under 90m, Under 2h, Under 2.5h. Popularity: Any, Popular, Hidden gems.
  • People and themes: the existing search panel, in this drawer.
  • Language: Any, English and 8 more, "More languages". Age rating: G to Not rated.
  • Short sub-drawers (Length, Popularity, Status) sit low and only as tall as their chips.

For the owner

  • Stacked sub-drawer (shown) or push in place inside the one drawer (the way the people search slides in today)? Stacked keeps the list visible behind; push is one fewer layer. Recommended: stacked.
  • Length and Popularity could be inline segments on the main drawer instead of rows (one tap less) at the cost of two busier lines. Recommended: rows, as shown.
