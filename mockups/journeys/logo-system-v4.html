Montage brand / logo system v4

Led by the M

The M is Montage’s own letter, and unlike a ticket it covers film and TV alike. Six directions put the Bodoni M at the centre and let the red/blue tick, the card and the fan support it. Every icon is flat: red #c24c56, blue #5a86bd, cream #efe7d6, ink #141210 on #0b0b0d.

Construction rules
  • The M: Bodoni Moda 800, optical size 10 (heavier hairlines than display cuts), letter centred on its stems, not its serifs: nudged 0.5 units right.
  • Icon grid 100. The M’s cap height is 40 in the icon (0.4 of the tile); the tick is as wide as the M’s stems (40) and one tenth of that thick.
  • Tick at −6°, always red then blue, with a gap below the M equal to the tick’s thickness × 2.
  • Cards and frames: corner radius 8 at 100, outlines 3.5 thick; at 32 and below, outlines thicken to 6 and the tick to 8.
  • At 16 every direction keeps the M; hairlines are allowed to drop, the stems carry the letter.
