Recommended: B, with A's ticket for the Nº

  • It reads in a second. A Story is seen for about two. The name at 230px over posters is the whole message; the streak sentence and the bar reward the second look. A's cream block and C's type-only card both need reading first.
  • The posters do the selling. Se7en, The Prestige and The Lion King at full size say more about Alex than any label, and they're the thing a friend replies to. The wall behind is their other loved films (the reveal card's personWall), graded and dimmed so it never competes.
  • Brand, held back. Oxblood only as the shadow under the three posters and the small ticket; the tick once, under the name; blue only in the bar and the tick. No glow, no rotation, no grain.
  • One card for both doors. The reveal and the profile share the same image, so a person's type looks the same wherever it's posted. Drop the radar from the shared card (it stays on the reveal screen); the Criterion cover remains the profile's link preview and the Post choice for "my whole profile".
  • Signed out at the reveal: no handle and no Nº yet. The card drops the ticket and the handle line, and the URL becomes montagefilm.app alone. The rest is the same.
