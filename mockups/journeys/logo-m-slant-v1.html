Montage brand / alternate mark

The M stands on the slanted tick

The tick returns to its −6° slant, rising to the right. The M’s stems end on the slant, so the left stem is longer than the right and the letter stands on the tick. Drawn on a 100 grid: hairlines 3, thick strokes 12; stem ends flare 1.5 each side over 5 units, and the top serifs are bracketed with a 4-unit fillet, so no stroke ends in a square cut.
