Brand switch v2: the 2 to 4 option switch, without the blue pill

Fix 1's ticket-stub strip is out. Here are three fresh directions for every 2 to 4 option switch: Unseen · Seen · All, Notifications · You, Just me · Date night · Friends · Family, To Watch · Rewatches, Post · Story, Red · Blue, Both · Jake · You, and Followers · Following. Each one replaces the filled --select-deep capsule (SegmentPill, web .seg). In all three, blue goes back to meaning only data and "you": match %, stars, Blend. "Now" is the owner's own iPhone screenshots. Tokens come from globals.css on origin/main 938282d3.

Decide

  1. A, the Bodoni word selector, for every switch. It is the only direction that could not come from a template. It reuses the two things that are most Montage, Bodoni and the red and blue tick, and it costs nothing in height (36, the same as --seg-h). The tick changes meaning slightly, from "this is the title" to "this is the chosen one". On a drawer, the title and the chosen word both carry it, so rule 1253 (the tick only in .rdu) would be amended to: "the title, or the chosen word of a switch".
  2. B, small-caps underline tabs, if you want the switch to be quieter than the content. It is the most legible and the most conventional. Its risk is that it reads as a third tab level under Taste · Films · Watchlist · History.
  3. C, the quiet chip with a dot, if you want the switch to still look tappable. Its risk is that on Tonight it sits right above the mood chips, so a one-of switch and a many-of filter look alike. Chips v1 would then have to give the filter chip a different "on" state.
  4. Mixed: A on drawers and page sections, B inside the Filters and Tonight drawers, where several switches stack (Who's watching, New or rewatch, Popularity, Seen). This is acceptable but it means two systems. I would try A everywhere first.
  5. In every direction, counts sit beside the word in tabular UI figures ("Notifications 3", "To Watch 828"). The word itself never turns blue.
