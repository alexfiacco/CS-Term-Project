b91 · Poster grow: diagnosis and the fixed open

Diagnosis (iPhone, build 38: PosterFlight.swift, FilmCover.swift; rule 1725)

The flying copy is a window snapshot of the tapped tile's frame (resizableSnapshotView in PosterFlight.capture), and the probe sits on the whole filmZoomSource tile, so it carries the match % stamp, the 0.97 press and the dimmed "Opening" loader (on a Picks card, the full-bleed poster and its fade, stretched by separate x and y scales); it is added to the UIWindow over the cover, so it rides over the backdrop for the whole flight. It flies to a header rect measured once (the last registered header probe, less a guessed 24pt rise) while the page moves under it (the whole page, header included, is offset by the rise; the seeded page is swapped for the loaded one; before b39-nav the pager also inset by the status bar), then the copy is cut and the real header shows: the jump.

What b39-nav already covers (c0e81d64)

It decides once per shown item whether a cover flies, stops a closed page flashing back, and drops the pager's status-bar inset (one source of the jump). It does not touch the snapshot, the stamp, or the moving target. Web (lib/poster-open.ts) already flies the poster's own image pixels on a canvas, so it has no stamp; reasoned from code, not reproduced on a device.

Recommendation: fix, small and contained to PosterFlight

(1) Fly a clean 2:3 image from the poster's cached image (ImagePipeline.cached), never a screen grab: no stamp, no loader, one uniform scale. (2) Keep the header poster out of the rise (only the blocks under it rise, as on web), so its spot is fixed from the first frame. (3) Take the target from the page on screen after layout, and re-read it on the last frame; hand off when they match. If (1) to (3) cannot ship clean in build 39, remove the flight: the rise from the bottom is already the fallback and needs no code.
