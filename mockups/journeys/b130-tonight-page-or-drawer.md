# b130: Tonight, a drawer or a page, and "Pick with these 3"

Owner, build 40: should the mood picker be a drawer or a full page; with three moods chosen should the button say "Pick with these 3"?

Today: the popcorn opens "How do you want to pick?" (rule 1328), and Just me opens Tonight as a full-height drawer over your tab (rule 648), answers kept at the app root so an accidental swipe loses nothing (rule 1177). Button: "Pick one for me" with nothing chosen, "Pick with these" with anything chosen, "or just pick for me" under it.

**Recommended: keep the full-height drawer.** It is a short errand on top of what you were doing. A page (frame 2) would give the choices and the result 84pt less height to a tab bar you don't need mid-pick, and put a page under a drawer (the chooser). The case for a page is that it feels like a destination; Picks already is that. Worth watching: the drawer's swipe-down can catch a scroll at the top of a long result (safe, but annoying).

**Button: recommended** "Pick with these 3" for two or more, counting every choice (moods, genres and everything in Anything else?, the same set the ticket names), not people. Exactly one: name it ("Pick with Tense"). Counting moods only undercounts once a genre is on, and "these 1" is broken English. Cost: the words change width as you tap (the button doesn't move).
