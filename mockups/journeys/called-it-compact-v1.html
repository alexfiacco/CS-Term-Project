Called it, small

The record from taste-model-trust-v1 section 01, redrawn small: no banner, no big number, and no "still learning" line. The message is the other way round: every rating makes our read on you sharper. Three options, each shown at a steady state (14 of your last 20) and an early state (2 of 3). All three use the same count and the same sheet: guesses we showed you before you rated, verdict kept at rating time (lib/how-we-did.ts, rule 1183, verdictFor within 10 points). It ships later, once the data is good; this is the design. Recommended: A.

Rules for all three

GuessesLine
0 to 2Nothing shows
3 to 19Called it on 2 of 3 · rate more and it gets sharper
20 and upCalled it on 14 of your last 20 · sharper with every rating
  • Same count everywhere: stored verdicts from how-we-did.ts, only rows with displayed true. Held-out guesses never count.
  • Last 20, not 40: most people have under 20 shown guesses today, so 20 keeps the window reachable and recent.
  • Small type only: 13px muted line, the counts in ink. No big numerals, no meters, no tick strip.
  • No softening, no trend headline: a falling record is never announced; it just reads lower.
  • Words: "Called it", "our guesses for you", "our read on you". Never "taste match". Ratings as 4.5★. No em dashes.
  • One red per screen: the Film DNA dash on the profile, the poster shadow on the film page, the label dash on the sheet.
  • Web: same line under Film DNA; the sheet becomes a centred dialog with the same rows.

Recommendation: A

Ship A, the line under Film DNA. It is the most subtle, ties the record to the read it measures, and its early state needs no special case. Add C later with the same string if people want it on the film page. Skip B: a header chip turns the record into a badge that shows up in every screenshot.
