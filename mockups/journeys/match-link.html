Honest notes

  • Eight ratings is thin. A single number at 8 would swing by 15 points once they rate more, and people would notice. Show a range, or don't show it at 8.
  • A cheaper first step is to keep 15 films and ship only the card and the landing. That's about 1 week, and it still beats today's generic invite.
  • The sender's card shows their archetype publicly, so it follows the profile's privacy. A hidden profile can't send one.

Recommendation

Build it, top priority for growth. It's the one share that asks the receiver a question about themselves. Do it in two steps: the card and the landing at 15 films first, then the 8-film early range once you can check it against pairs who later rated 30 or more. This also feeds the referral count.
