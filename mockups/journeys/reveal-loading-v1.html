The reveal: waiting for the first picks

The owner: while the first picks load after the reveal, nothing says anything is happening. Below is today's iPhone reveal, drawn from the code at the moment the name has developed and the picks are still coming, then three ways to show the wait. Each one uses a loader the app already has. Frames are 390 wide and show the whole scroll; the dashed line marks the bottom of a 390 × 844 screen.

Where the wait is (RevealScreen.swift, RevealModel)

~1 sThe name. instantRead posts /api/quiz-forming (reveal), asked again after 1.5 s if it fails. The name develops from a blur over 1.4 s. The reveal looks finished here. a few s, up to ~2 minThe build. build → /api/profile-from-ratings, then poll on /api/profile-status every 1.4 s. The server reads each film (capped by ENRICH_MAX_MS, 120 s), computes the profile, then waits up to PICKS_WAIT_MS (10 s) to cache the picks. The only sign is the 45 s stall note. ~1 s warm, ~12 s cold, 25 s capThe picks. loadPicks posts /api/weekly-pick (mode: "top") once built, retrying a 409 every 2 s within 25 s; nothing comes back after that. up to 5 sThe posters. show downloads and decodes all three before picksReady, so the fan can play without waiting. totalFrom the name to the fan playing: usually 3 to 15 s, and 30 s or more on a cold server. On the web the same wait is in LockedIdentity.useFirstPicks (the same 25 s budget).

What it shows today, and the loaders we have

  • iPhone: the logo's three cards sit still, small, in the middle slot (FanPicks at rest, nothing animating), over one 13 pt --faint line, "Reading your taste" then "Finding your first picks". On a 390 × 844 screen that's about 560 pt down, below the Film DNA.
  • The buttons look ready. Signed in, a tap before the build finishes shows the button's MiniLoader (rule 1006). Once the build is done, See my picks moves on even though the fan hasn't played yet.
  • Web: three skeleton tiles plus the same line, pulsing (.rpicks-wait). It does better than the iPhone, but it's also low on the page.
  • Loaders we can use: MiniLoader (the fan breathing, 0.55 to 1 over 2.6 s), LoadingStrip (the streak strip, 30 pt, sliding one frame per pull on the Soft claw clock, label meta --faint), MontageLoader (the claw fan with LoadingWords), Skeleton / PickSkeleton (a --panel-2 block with a sheen every 1.8 s).

Pick: C, plus A's honest button for signed-in accounts

C shows the wait where people are already looking, right under the name, using the loader the app keeps for "something is coming in this row". It doesn't block anyone and it doesn't add a screen. It also has real progress behind it (/api/profile-status). A on its own is below the fold and would hold back the save. Its one good idea is worth keeping signed in: when built && !picksReady, See my picks should show the strip instead of leaving early. B fixes a different moment (after the tap) that Picks already covers with skeletons, and it adds a step to onboarding.
