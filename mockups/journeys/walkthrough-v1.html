Home tab: my honest read

  • Browse as home: no. Browse is a catalogue with a search box and shelves. "What do I do here?" gets worse there, not better, and it buries the thing only Montage does (a pick for you, with a %).
  • Landing on Tonight: no. The popcorn opens a chooser and a drawer over the screen, not a page. Opening a sheet at every launch blocks the app and gets dismissed by reflex.
  • Picks, made self-explanatory: yes. The confusion is the first visit stacking Three for tonight, the tabs and the pick. Show one big pick, point once at the popcorn, and hold Three for tonight to the second visit (it is still owed, nothing lost).

Pointer to the popcorn: B over A

  • B teaches on the real button at the moment it is useful. It reuses the existing coach-mark pattern ("Swipe up for the next film · Got it", montage_hint_seen:picks) with a new key, so only one coach mark shows at a time: the popcorn one first visit, the swipe one after.
  • A adds a card to a walkthrough people already find long, and the proposed card 1 already teaches the popcorn.
  • The popcorn's cream pill gets a 2px select ring while the coach mark shows. No scrim, nothing dimmed: Picks stays usable under it.
