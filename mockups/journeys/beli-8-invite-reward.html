Invites earn a bonus. They never unlock the basics

Beli makes people invite friends to get in or to use features, and reviewers resent it. Montage does the opposite. Everything works without inviting anyone, and sign-up is never gated. Inviting is only a thank-you on top: when 3 friends join from your links, your Canon (your favourites shelf) grows from 10 films to 15. The invite sheet shows progress ("1 of 3"), every way you share counts (profile, movie night and match links), and the friend lands on an open, free sign-up. This builds on referral.html (reward A).

Today · Invite a friend

As built (InviteSheet): a link preview and share targets. Nothing shows that anyone joined, or gives a reason to send a second invite.

Proposed · a bonus, 1 of 3

A progress block with three slots: joined, signing up, open. The line under it says plainly that nothing is locked and it never expires.

Proposed · the share sheet

The system share sheet with the message filled in. The link carries your invite code, so a join counts whichever app it goes through.

Proposed · what the friend sees

An open door, not a gate. Anyone can join with or without the link; the link only credits you and shows your match once they've rated.

Proposed · the bonus, unlocked

Three friends joined: five more Canon slots, for good. Someone who never invites keeps the full 10-film Canon and every feature.

Open for you

When a friend counts: drawn at 15 rated films (so throwaway sign-ups don't count). Taste weight: the extra 5 Canon films should share the Canon's weight, not add to it, so the bonus doesn't change your picks. iPhone installs: crediting an App Store install needs a "Who invited you?" step in onboarding (optional, skippable).
