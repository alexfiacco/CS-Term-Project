The rule

CountSort, full name ▾⋯

1 · Count

  • A choice: the count is the control, 15 semibold ink, muted ▾, 44 tall. N unseen ▾ (Unseen / Seen / All), N to watch ▾ (To watch / Rewatches), N lists ▾.
  • No choice: the same words, 15 medium muted, no ▾: 30 films.
  • Never caps, never on a line of its own.

2 · Sort ▾, its full name

A chip with the sort's full name, the same words as its menu, no "Sort" before it: Best for you ▾, Highest rated ▾, Letterboxd rating ▾. Never shortened. Direction inside its menu.

3 · Filters icon

Sliders in the ⋯'s quiet 32pt circle, 44pt tap. Shown when the page holds 40 or more films in all (before Show and filters, so it never blinks when you switch Unseen to Seen), or when a filter is on. On: a red badge with the count, the row's one red accent. Clear all is inside the sheet.

4 · View icon

The same circle, just left of the ⋯. It shows the view you would switch to; tap flips it. Never a two-icon pill.

5 · ⋯, fixed order

  1. Select films
  2. Add all N to a list, only when every film is loaded

The three icons sit together at the right edge, 12 apart; they never wrap or hide.
