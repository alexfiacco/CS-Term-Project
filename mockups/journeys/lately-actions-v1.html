Lately: where share goes, and long titles

The owner's question: "Maybe put share icon left adjacent to the heart and left aligned under the profile picture. Would that look better?" Today the share icon floats at the far right of the text column, just left of the poster, a long way from the heart and comment it belongs with. Three ways to place it, the same four posts in each, at 390pt. B and C also carry the two other fixes from the screenshot: the title on its own line (no more hanging second line) and "you gave it 4★" for your rating, so the poster's stamp is plainly theirs.

Scan line

All three keep 44x44 per action (12pt padding round an 18pt icon). In C the three targets sit 44 apart with no gap, like the post drawer's flat bar. A's share is near the poster's own target; C moves it clear of it.

Every row on the iPhone is a photo or poster column then a text column (Your people, chats, comments, Discussion replies). Nothing starts under a photo. B would be the one row that breaks it. The post drawer's action bar already runs like, comment, share left to right.

2. Long titles: one rhythm for every post
The owner: "the title takes up two lines and it looks quite awkward." Cause, iOS: postHead (SocialScreen.swift:470-485) is an HStack(alignment: .firstTextBaseline) of three views, so the title Text (:479, lineLimit(2)) wraps inside its own column that starts after "Sam watched ", and its second line hangs under the title. The web's .lf-hd is an inline paragraph, so there it wraps back to the name's edge instead: the two already differ. Three fixes, each with C's actions and the new wording, using The History of Concrete and Everything Everywhere All at Once.
1. Proposed: the title on its own line

"Sam watched" on line 1, the title alone on line 2 in Bodoni 18, one line, cut with … only past 238pt. Every post is the same height whatever the title. The name and the title are two separate lines, so they stay two separate 44pt targets.

2. One line, the title cut

Same height as today's short posts, but the title gets only what "Sam watched " leaves (about 140pt): "The History of Co…", "Everything Ev…". The film is the subject of the post and it is the part that gets cut.

3. Smaller serif, wrapping from the left

Bodoni 16, inline. Fixes the hang (line 2 starts under the name) but both long titles still take two lines, so posts change height with the title, and 16 is off the type scale (card is 18).

Recommend 1

It is the only option where every post has the same rhythm: kicker, title, meta, actions. It reads like every other film card in the app (the Bodoni title as its own line), it gives the title the full 238pt so almost no real title is cut, and on the iPhone it removes the three-view HStack that causes the hang: a VStack of the name line and the title line.

The cost

Each post is 113pt against today's 91 for a short title (112 for a wrapped one): one more line. With the poster at 84pt plus its stamp, the text column ends just past the stamp, so the post is barely taller than the poster block beside it.

Your rating

The stamp on the poster is their rating and nothing else. The meta line says yours in words: "1d · you gave it 2.5★", never a bare "2.5★ yours" next to their "4★". With no rating of yours it stays "56% for you".

Recommendation: C with title option 1

Share right after comment, in the text column (C), not under the photo (B). It answers the owner's real complaint (share is stranded far from heart and comment) without breaking the photo-then-text column every row in the app uses, keeps each target 44pt, adds no height of its own (B adds 23pt a post), and is a one-line change on the web (.lf-a.lf-share loses margin-left:auto) and a two-line change on the iPhone (postActions: no Spacer, no trailing -12).

Title on its own line (option 1), so every post has the same rhythm and the hanging second line is gone; "you gave it 4★" in the meta, so the poster's stamp is only ever theirs.
