Movie night and Tonight · say when it's on a watchlist

The room already leans on watchlists: a film two or more of you saved gets up to 15 rank points (watchlistBoost, 5 a person) and a slot of its own, with the blurb "2 of you have this on your watchlist." But that blurb is never drawn: the iPhone does not decode it and the web never renders it. The only trace is an "On your watchlists" tag on the seeded ticket. Tonight's watchlist lane shows only as a filled bookmark. Proposed: one quiet line with tiny faces, where you decide. Names are stand-ins.
