Pitch: the wait, made to look like work on your ask

The owner: "Pitch loading page can look better." Today, after "Pitch it", the drawer keeps the whole ask on screen (the box with the mic, Recent, Or try) and only the cream button changes, to a disabled "Pitching…". Nothing moves for 3 to 6 seconds. launch-options-v3 approved the Soft claw motion and put the 56pt fan where the results land; this round redesigns the page around it. Four directions, each at 390pt in three states: the start (just after the loader appears), mid-wait, and the handoff into results. Every phone is live: the fan is launch-options-v3's own code (Cut 2: a 0.2s sine pull, a 2 percent dim, 1.8 pulls a second), and the Play buttons run each direction with a normal, fast, borderline or slow answer.

Shared by all four. The pitch here is the live example chip "Like Arrival but scarier". The captions are built from what parseKeywords and anchorFromSentence in lib/recut.ts read on the device, before the server answers (both are pure and already ship to the web client): the word it read ("scarier"), the film it starts from (Arrival), then the ranking. The final chips still come from the server's answer. The results they hand off to carry the idea discussed with the owner: a faint grained backdrop of the top pick behind the header (the DM card A treatment: grayscale 35%, saturation 62%, brightness 50%, film grain), with the slim pinned bar (sentence and chips) taking over on scroll; (4) builds the whole wait around that header. One red per screen: while the fan runs, its red card is the red, so the kicker drops its dash ("Your pitch", as the answer reads) and the quote's bar stays grey until the answer lands, when it turns red as the fan leaves.

The timing every direction keeps
0 to 0.4s
Nothing new appears. The tap is answered at once: the button reads "Pitching…" and the box locks. An answer inside 0.4s goes straight to results with their normal entry, so a fast pitch never flashes a loader.
At 0.4s
The wait begins. The ask steps aside over 0.35s and the loader starts, with a first caption that names a word from their own sentence.
Once shown, 0.7s or more
No flicker. If the answer lands at 0.5s, the loader holds to 1.1s and then hands off. A loader that appears and vanishes in 0.1s reads as a glitch.
At 6s
Reassure. The caption turns to "Still on it. Long pitches take a little longer." The motion does not speed up or change; only the words do.
At 12s
An exit. "Taking longer than usual." with a quiet Cancel that keeps the sentence in the box. The server may still finish and count it, so the finished pitch lands in Recent, which reopens free.

Recommendation: (4) Develop, with (1)'s words

The wait becomes the dark before the picture. The ask folds at once into the slim pinned bar the results will keep (your sentence, the chips as they are read), and the rest of the drawer goes to a dim, faintly grained screen with the Soft claw fan and a caption about their pitch. When the answer lands, the top pick develops out of that grain as the dm-card-v4 A backdrop, its poster and title arrive on it, and the list follows. Nothing moves twice: the bar never moves, the grain becomes the backdrop's grain, and the fan leaves from the spot where the top pick appears.

Why over the others. (1) Focus is close and the cheapest; it is the fallback if the owner wants the results page left as it is, since (4) assumes the pinned bar and the backdrop header. (2) Skeleton has the least layout change but is the least Montage: grey bars for 4 seconds feel like any app, and skeleton rows promise a list before we know one exists ("Nothing fits all of that" would replace five rows). (3) Reel is the most cinematic but costs about 1s after the answer, and the frames it shows are the catalogue, not the picks, which is honest only while blurred.

Fast and slow, in (4)

  • Under 0.4s: no loader. The ask crossfades to the results and the backdrop develops anyway (0.9s), so even a cached pitch arrives like a picture, not a page swap.
  • 0.4s to 1.1s: the bar and the grain show; the fan opens and holds until 1.1s, then the picture develops.
  • Over 6s: the caption reassures; the grain and the fan stay as they were. At 12s, Cancel.
  • Reduce Motion: no grain shimmer, no claw; the fan breathes in opacity (MontageLoader's 0.55 to 1, 2.6s), captions swap without the rise, the backdrop fades in at its final blur.

What it takes to build

  • Captions: web: parseKeywords and anchorFromSentence on the client (pure, already in lib/recut.ts); the first read word and the anchor fill the captions, the generic line ("Reading your pitch…") when nothing reads. iPhone: the cue words already in Recut.swift plus a small anchor reader, or the generic lines.
  • The count: "1,200 films" is the pool size from the last answer's considered (or the status call); with no number, "Ranking the whole catalogue for you…".
  • Timing: a 400ms delay and a 700ms minimum around busy, in RecutSheet and RecutScreen; no server change.
  • Backdrop: the top pick's poster (a real backdrop where we have one) with the CSS and SwiftUI filters dm-card-v4 A already specifies.
