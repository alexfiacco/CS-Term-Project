Films together on someone's profile

The owner's idea: on a friend's profile, how many films the two of you have watched together, "like Beli does it right under the profile username", or next to the streak, e.g. "2 films together". Drawn on iPhone and web from origin/main (ProfileScreen.swift ProfileHeader, Result.tsx ProfileHeader with .pv2-head, MatchPill / PublicIdentity.tsx .match-pill, WeeklyStreak, GoalChip), spacing and heights as the code sets them. A puts a small line under the @handle, B a chip on the streak's row; both open the same list. You are looking at Emma's profile (a made-up member). Today is Fri, Nov 6.

What the number counts

  • Distinct films where either of you tagged the other in Watched with and it was not answered "Not me" (activity_feed kinds watched_with and watched_with_added), plus movie nights you both sat in that locked a film (theaters.decided_slug).
  • A night and a tag of the same film on the same day count once. A rewatch together on another day is a new row in the list but still one film in the count.
  • Shown from 1 up, nothing at 0: "1 film together", "4 films together".
  • Tap: "Films with @emma", newest first, each with its date, how it got there, and both of your stars where they show.

Who sees it

  • Only the two people in it. You see it on Emma's profile, Emma sees it on yours. Sam opening Emma's profile sees nothing (the first frame is what Sam sees).
  • Never on your own profile, never on a shared card or a public link.
  • Gone if either of you blocks the other, and quiet while either has "Share my activity with friends" off (tags already need it on).

Where the archetype is

On the profile the archetype is the Taste tab's card (the spine, the eyebrow, the name, the streak words), drawn under the tabs here. Rule 1130's grey line under the handle lives only in People rows, Suggested cards and the Watch with picker, and the owner turned it down on the Blend header. So the line under Emma's @handle is free: A takes it, and nothing has to move to make room.

Recommendation: A, under the username REC

  • It never changes the header's height. On iPhone and web phone it fits inside the photo's 64 that the name and handle are already centred on; with a Founding badge it shares that row. Rows do not look different from profile to profile.
  • It is about the two of you, and sits with who Emma is to you, as Beli does. The streak row is Emma's own record (her streak, her goal); a "together" chip there reads as one more badge of hers.
  • B has no room. The streak's row never wraps (rule 1105); streak + goal + together is about 436pt in 350. B also adds a whole row when Emma has no streak or goal.
  • One extra read per profile view, only when it is someone else's and you are signed in; nothing new is stored.

Data caveats

  • Tags only exist since Nov 4, 2026, so the tag part starts at 0 or 1 for almost everyone. Movie nights go back to September and carry the first weeks.
  • Movie-night seats are not a reliable record. theater_members rows are deleted when someone idles 15 minutes (sweepIdleParticipants), says Not tonight, or is removed by the host; theater_events keeps only 50 per room. Someone who closed the app during the film may have lost their seat. A durable count wants the room's members written down once the film locks (one row per person, or the lock event's detail).
  • Today's "together" score in tagOptions counts tag rows, not films, and misses a tag Emma made that you have not answered (it reads your own tags and the ones you added). The counter needs its own read: both directions, live kinds only, distinct by film.
  • A night with no locked film has no film to list, so it does not count. Stars come from each person's live rating, not the tag's copy, so a later re-rate shows.
