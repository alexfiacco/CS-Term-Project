Weekly taste challenges

Three small jobs a week, each picked from a gap in your own profile, so finishing one makes your Picks better rather than earning points. They live in the weekly streak drawer and in one slim row on Picks, and a finished challenge counts the week. There are no XP, levels or badges.

Honest notes

  • Two of the three examples are rating challenges, and today only a logged watch counts for the streak. Either a finished challenge counts the week (proposed), or challenges sit next to the streak without feeding it.
  • "Watch a film you split on" can take more than a week. Keep it as the optional third, and let it carry over once.
  • The "before and after" lines only work where the change is real. When a challenge moves nothing visible, say "done" and nothing more.

Recommendation

Build it, second after the match link. It's the one idea here where playing makes the product better, it reuses the streak drawer, and it needs no new screens. Start with the drawer section alone. Add the Picks row only if fewer than about 1 in 5 streak users open the drawer each week.
