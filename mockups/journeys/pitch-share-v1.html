Pitch sharing, v1: the house share button, drawer and chat card

The owner: "Make the share button the same as other share buttons on pitch and allow to send to users and groups too." Today a pitch's Share is a blue word that hands the link straight to the phone's share sheet, so a pitch can't go to a Montage friend or group at all. Here it becomes the share circle that Pitch's own "Your pick" view and the film page already use, and it opens the film share drawer's Send to row on top of the Movie Night "Share the result" drawer (Story or Post, Share, Save image, Copy link). A pitch you send lands in the chat as a pitch card drawn like the approved DM film card A. Drawn at 390pt from origin/main (Recut.swift, Recut.tsx, ShareFilmSheet.tsx, ShareResult.tsx, FilmShareSheet.swift, lib/dm.ts). Mock only.

1 · The share button: today, proposed, and the two it should match
Today · Pitch results (iPhone)

A blue word, no icon, between "Your pitch" and the ×: a ShareLink straight to the system sheet. The web is the same word, a .linkbtn beside the quoted sentence. No Montage friends or groups anywhere.

Proposed · Pitch resultsRecommended

The share circle (36, the share glyph, ShareCircle / .icirc with Icon "share") left of the ×, 10 apart, in the drawer's header row on both platforms. It opens the pitch share drawer (section 2). The web moves it out of the quote row into the header.

On main already · Pitch · Your pick

The other share button on Pitch, the one to match: Back and Share circles around the kicker (rule 1106, ShareFilmButton / ShareCircle). The film page, Tonight and Movie Night tickets use the same circle.

Proposed · the shared /pitch page

The same circle at the end of the page bar. Signed in, the full drawer (you can pass on a friend's pitch). Signed out, the drawer without Send to. Pitch it for you stays as the one cream action. Today the page has no share at all.

2 · The share drawer: Send to on top, then the card, then Share / Save image / Copy link
OpenRecommended

Header: the Pitch mark, "Share your pitch" with the C1 dash under it (24 × 3, 3 below; the drawer's one red), and the sentence in Bodoni roman. Send to: the film drawer's row as is: friends, then groups, all rounded squares with their names, the search icon, + New group. Then Movie Night's Story / Post switch, the card, and Share / Save image / Copy link.

Two picked

As the film drawer (rule 650): picking swaps the lower half for the note and one Large Send to 2; each goes through its own send in parallel, a failed one stays picked with "Try again". Clear the picks to get the card back.

Sent to Sam

The toast "Sent to Sam" with See chat (one recipient; "Sent to 2" with none for several). Sam's face keeps a quiet blue check and reads Sent until the drawer closes; a tap there sends again. The drawer stays open for Story or Copy link.

Why this shape

  • Send to is the film drawer's own component (SendToGroupSection on both), so friends, groups, search, the note, + New group and Send to N come for free; only the two send calls change.
  • The card part is Movie Night's (ShareResultRow's drawer): the switch, the card, three pills. Pitch already has a server card; it just needs a Post (4:5) beside the Story.
  • Post first, as Movie Night: it is the one that reads in a group chat; Story is a tap away for Instagram and TikTok.
  • No Elsewhere row: Share opens the phone's sheet with the image, the words and the /pitch link, which already covers Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram and X.

One red

  • The drawer's red is the C1 dash under "Share your pitch".
  • The card shows the oxblood spine that the Post really has (rule 1109). It is the image being shared, like a poster, not the drawer's trim. If the owner counts it, drop the dash only while the card is showing.
  • The results screen keeps its quote bar as its red; the new circle is ink, not red.

4 · Recommendations and what the server needs

Recommendations

  • Button: replace the word with ShareCircle (iPhone, Recut.swift header) and .icirc + Icon "share" (web, in PortalModal's header, out of .recut-quote-row), left of the ×. The same circle on /pitch/<id>'s bar.
  • Drawer: a SharePitchSheet built from ShareSheetFrame + SendToGroupSection + Movie Night's switch and pills; iPhone the same parts as FilmShareSheet and RoomShareResult, header via DrawerHeader/DashUnder (rule 1127).
  • Card wording: "Sam sent a pitch", "Pitch it for me", "Details" (chat) / "Movie night" (group), "See your pitch" (yours). Never "Open", never "taste match".
  • The quote: today's results set the sentence in italic on the screenshot; keep it Bodoni roman everywhere (house rule), as drawn here.
  • Track: pitch_shared { via: friend | group | share | save | copy, format } and pitch_card_opened { from: dm | group, action }.

Server

  • A new message kind "pitch" in group_messages (a chat is a group of kind dm, so one change covers both) with a pitch jsonb snapshot: { id, sentence, read: [chip labels], top: [{ slug, title, year, posterUrl }] }, stored like the film card's snapshot. SQL: pitch-share-v1.sql beside this mock, RLS stays on, not run.
  • Routes: POST /api/dm { to, pitchId, note? } and POST /api/group/thread { groupId, pitchId, note? }, both through a new addGroupPitch (lib/group-thread.ts), with notify text "Sent a pitch".
  • The card's Post: /api/recut/card/<id>?size=post (1080 × 1350, CARD_SIZES.post exists in card-kit) beside the Story; bump CARD_V.
  • Old builds: also fill body with "Sent a pitch: “…” montagefilm.app/pitch/<id>" when there is no note, or hide kind pitch from builds that predate it, so they never show an empty bubble.

Privacy checks

  • Sender: signed in; the pitch exists (getRecut) and Pitch is on; a chat only between friends with no block (openDm), a group only as a member; the same rate limit as a film send.
  • Recipient can view it: a pitch is already public by its link (/pitch/<id>, server-only recuts table), so anyone in the chat may see it; the check is that the server builds the snapshot from the stored row and never from the client.
  • Nothing private leaks: the snapshot is what /pitch/<id> shows: no "with" friend (sentence through stripFriendPhrase, no with chip), no match %, the original sharer's handle only if it was stored as public.
  • Reads: unchanged membership check in the thread route; "Pitch it for me" uses op: "shared", free and uncounted.
