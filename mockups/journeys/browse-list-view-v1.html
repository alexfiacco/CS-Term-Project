Browse as a list: one switch at the end of the bar

The owner's idea, mocked before it is built. Every Browse page with a poster grid (collections, World cinema shelves, lanes, themes, moods, genres, decades, Every film) gets a grid / list switch at the end of the Sort and Filters bar. The list is for choosing: each row is a poster thumb with your match, the title, year and runtime, your people's rating, one line on why it fits you, and "Watch on <service>" when it is on one of yours. The choice is one setting for all of Browse, saved to your account, so web and iPhone open the same way.

Today · a shelf, as a grid

The grid stays the default. The switch sits at the end of the bar, the grid icon in blue. Under each poster: the title and your people's rating (rule 1074).

Proposed · the same shelf, as a list

Rows, no lines between them. Thumb with the match stamp; title; year · runtime; your people ("4.2★ · 3", "@sam_r 4.5★" or "12k ratings"); "Because you loved…" (the Picks line, only for 70%+); one button when it streams on your services.

Proposed · another page, same choice

One setting for all Browse. Picking list on a shelf opens Every film, genres and moods as lists too, on the web and the iPhone (saved to the account, not the device). With On my services on, every row has its button.

Open for you

Default: grid for everyone until they switch; the switch remembers per account. Why line: the Picks "Because you loved X & Y" (your own enjoyed films closest to it, no model call), asked for rows as they scroll in, 70%+ only; a row without one is simply shorter, never padded. Button: only when the film is on one of your services (or Free is on); a film only in theaters gets no button (no showtimes source). Flag: built behind its own switch.
