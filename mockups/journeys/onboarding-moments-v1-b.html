Watching one tonight?

The first session ends with a plan and a reason to open the app tomorrow. Everything under it exists: the "Did you watch" local notification (Watching this tonight, next morning at 10:00), How we did ("We said 91%, you gave it 4★"), the weekly streak strip.

  • Tonight is a compact button on each of the three; one tap opens the drawer. The drawer leads with the film and carries the one red dash under "Tonight".
  • The notification ask moves here, from the tour's last card, so iOS's prompt follows a reason the person just chose.
  • Tomorrow's rating lands in How we did, and with moment 7 the streak's first week.
  • Nobody is forced: the line under the title is the only nudge, and "Rate more films" stays below.

Halfway to your read

Ratings 1 to 14 are the longest stretch with nothing back, at the point people are most likely to close the deck. One toast at 8, in the slot the 15 toast already uses, 3.2 seconds, once a run.

  • Middle phone, within the rules: it speaks about progress only. The Film DNA ring brightens at half; the wall keylines the posters they gave 5★, the only new mark, so the 8 feel like theirs.

  • Never a name or a % (rules 202, 239). The poster keeps its 200pt.

Moment 3 · High impact, M effort

What we found, while it reads

The import is the richest data we get and the longest wait. Instead of a counter alone, short facts from their own export appear one at a time, about every five seconds, each a small reveal. Then the facts dim to a strip and the archetype develops on top of them, as today.

  • Facts only from the export (voice rule: never invent): counts from ratings.csv and diary.csv read on the phone, directors once matching has them. A fact we cannot back is not shown.
  • Soft claw (rule 1134) replaces the bare progress view at the top; the three stages fold into one line at the foot.
  • No red on the running screen beyond the fan's own card. The hand-off keeps the reveal's oxblood rule as its one accent.

Moment 4 · Medium-high impact, S effort

Send to a friend, see your Blend

The web reveal already has this line (LockedIdentity, rule 197); the iPhone reveal does not. It is the only Blend seed for someone who came without an invite, and it sits at the peak, when the archetype is fresh and worth sending.

  • A row under the picks: two square faces (theirs and a question mark), the line in Bodoni, the web's sub "They rate a few films too. You both see the %.", a compact Send.
  • Send opens the one share sheet with the archetype card; signed out, the link is the public archetype page until the profile is saved (rule 851).
  • The friend lands on the invited welcome that is live today, and their reveal ends on "You and Sam: NN%" (rule 198), which is the loop.
