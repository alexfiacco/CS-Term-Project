Pitch icon and button

On the cream button all of it is drawn in cream ink, so at 18pt the three overlapping rectangles and the chevrons merge into something that reads as a briefcase or a little robot, not as posters. Where the mark appears: Browse's Pitch button (18, iOS PitchButton and web .pitch-btn); "Pitch this instead ›" under search (22, on a panel tile, blue lifted poster); the Pitch share sheet (22); a chat's pitch card, "Pitch it for me" (15, cream pill). Recommendation: B with quotation marks.
  1. Icon, quotation marks (2). A pitch is something you say, and the app already shows every pitch as a quote with the red bar, so the mark previews the thing you get. It is two shapes, so it stays clear at 15pt on the chat pill and at 18pt on the button, where the current three rectangles and chevrons blur. Runner up: the speech bubble (4), but it sits too close to the messages icon in the header. The slate (3) and megaphone (6) say "film" or "announce" rather than "describe what you want"; the nib (5) reads as a drawing tool.
  2. Treatment, B. In this app cream fill means "the main thing to do here": Action! in the drawer, Just pick for me, the Tonight moon. Browse's main thing is browsing and search; Pitch is an alternative way in, so it should not be primary there. B still has the most contrast of anything on the row (a full cream outline and cream words), so it stays findable without being the brightest object on the screen. C borrows the select blue, which in this app means "chosen", so the button would look already toggled on.
  3. Where it changes. The mark swaps everywhere it is used now (button, Pitch this instead, share sheet, chat pill). The drawer's "Action!" stays cream filled: that is the real commit, and it is primary in that sheet. The walkthrough card gains the mark beside "Browse · Pitch" so the tour teaches the same shape you will tap. No red is added anywhere; the only red on each screen stays the pitch bar or the caps dash.
