Montage Pro (a proposal, not for building now)

A restrained paid tier for depth and identity. It never touches what makes Montage work: matches, Picks, Film DNA, archetypes, taste match between people and movie nights stay free, with no counts, caps or blurred previews. $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, with a 7-day trial and a reminder two days before the charge. The name fits the code: lib/pro.ts already calls the entitlement "Pro".

  • In all, about L (5 to 7 weeks)

Honest notes

  • The money is small at first. At about 1,000 monthly users and 2 to 4% paying, that's 20 to 40 subscribers, roughly $250 to $500 a year after Apple's 15%. Pro is a signal that Montage can last, not a business yet.
  • A paywall that arrives before people love the app costs more goodwill than it earns. The teardown already says to wait for retention data and about 1,000+ monthly users.
  • Every feature on the list has to be good enough to pay for on its own. "Taste over time" is the only one that's close to built.

Recommendation

Skip it for now. Revisit at about 1,000 monthly users with 4-week retention data. Keep the page as the design to build to. In the meantime, build the free pieces that Pro would sit on (the year card, the referral's Canon work, the monthly snapshots), so Pro is mostly billing when its time comes.
