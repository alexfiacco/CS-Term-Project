Recommendation

C: Pitch leads the Browse card

Length. C keeps the walkthrough at five cards on the web and six on the iPhone with the soft ask, the same count rule 1142 left plus the restored last card. B makes it seven, and the deck has been cut to stop it feeling long twice already (Lately in v3, notifications in 1142).

Clarity. Pitch is the one thing in Browse that has to be taught: what to type, and that it comes back ranked for you. A puts it in the second half of a sentence, after "Browse by mood, genre or decade", so it reads as an extra. C gives Pitch the title ("Pitch me a film", the drawer's own words), shows the button where it lives, and shows a sentence turning into films.

Two people. C's sample pitch has @Emma in it, so the card quietly shows the two-person pitch without needing its own line. The sentence stays about one person, so it fits in two lines.

What C gives up. The Made for you tiles and Every film's posters leave the tour. Browse is still named in the label, and the last line ("Or browse them all") leads there. If the owner wants Browse's posters kept, A is the fallback, and it costs no extra card either.

What the build would change

TOUR_VERSION

  • Keep 8 for existing accounts. Both apps show the tour again when tour_v is below TOUR_VERSION, so raising it re-runs the whole deck for everyone. That is too much for one card, and existing members already get Browse's "New:" Pitch tip.
  • New accounts start at tour_v 0, so they get the new card with no bump.
  • If the notifications soft ask raises it to 9, ship Pitch in the same release and on the same bump. Don't bump twice.
  • Not recommended: re-showing just the Pitch card once to tour_v 8 accounts. It needs a second, one-card path through both tours, for something the Browse tip already covers.

Behaviour

  • Only when Pitch is on (recut.enabled, the same check as the Browse button). If it is off, card 3 falls back to today's Browse art and copy (A and C), and B's card is left out.
  • The art is an example. It never runs a pitch, never spends a free pitch, and never calls /api/recut. As with the Tonight card's moods, tapping an Or try chip only swaps the sample.
  • Under 15 rated, the scores read "?%" as on every other card ("You ?% · Emma 84%"), never a made-up number for the viewer.
  • Closing a walkthrough that showed the card sets pitchTipSeen, so Browse doesn't teach it a second time.
  • The sentence keeps its two lines. iPhone: a short form for ViewThatFits. Web: the under-500px variant.
