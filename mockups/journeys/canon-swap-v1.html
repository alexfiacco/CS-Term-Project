Canon swap v1

"Your Canon is full" on the film page (build 25). Today: ten pill buttons reading "Swap out" in bold sans and the title in Bodoni, then Cancel. Proposed: a calm drawer header with the red Canon ★ and the tick, the ten films as plain rows you choose from, and one cream button that says what will happen.

What changes

  • Header. The filled red Canon ★ (20, --red, as the profile's "★ Your Canon") before "Your Canon is full" in Bodoni 22, the red/blue tick 3 under it (a drawer title, an allowed place), then "Swap one out for 12 Angry Men?" in Newsreader 16 with the film in ink. One voice per line: no more sans + Bodoni mixed inside a button.
  • A, the list. The ten Canon films in Canon order as 56 rows: a 28×42 poster at the 2 radius, the title in sans 15/600 on one line with an ellipsis (The Lord of the Rings no longer runs off a pill), the year in 13 muted, and a quiet "⇄ Swap" in 13/600 muted on the right. A tap chooses the row: the select fill and 1.5 edge at the 12 radius, the solid select circle with a dark tick in place of "Swap". Nothing is swapped until the cream Swap in 12 Angry Men (44, pill); Cancel is a 44 text button. The list scrolls inside the drawer; the half row and the fade say there is more, and the button row stays put above the home bar.
  • A, on open. No row chosen and the cream button at 40% until one is. One deliberate step instead of ten destructive buttons.
  • B, the grid. The Canon as the profile shows it: 5×2 posters at the 6 radius with their Nº ranks (Nº in sans, the figure in Newsreader). Tap one: a select ring and the tick circle on it, and the row under the grid spells out the swap ("La La Land → 12 Angry Men, Nº 3"). The whole drawer fits with no scroll (about 540 tall vs about 730), but the titles are only on the posters.
  • Every row, button and poster is at least 44 tall; spacing is 4/8 with the 16 gutter. Icons are Lucide (star, arrow-left-right, check, arrow-right).

Pick

A. It reads like a choice, not ten alarms: titles are legible and never cut mid-word, the year tells two films apart, and the one confirm says exactly what happens. B is prettier and shorter, and worth keeping in mind if the Canon editor ever moves into this drawer, but a ten-way choice by poster alone is harder for films whose poster carries no title.
