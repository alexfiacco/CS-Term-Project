Profile header v3, after X, Instagram and Beli

Owner, 2026-11-06, sent three profile headers (X, Instagram, Beli) and asked to keep C. What the three have in common, and what ours lacks: the name in a bold sans, not a display serif; numbers bold, labels regular; buttons as soft filled or outlined rounded rectangles about 34pt tall; and nothing louder than the name in the header. The idea here: who you are reads in the app's sans (social), your taste reads in Bodoni (Montage), so "Nihilist" stays the one serif hero. Photos stay rounded squares (house rule). Anonymised (Emma Hart).

Instagram: photo left, name over three numbers beside it, bio under, two grey filled buttons. Our C follows this.
X: name, handle, bio, then "39 Following · 9 Followers" under the bio, outlined buttons. Counts go below the bio here, so either order is normal.
Beli: centred photo, counts, outlined buttons, then the streak and the yearly challenge as cards below the buttons, not as pills in the header.
