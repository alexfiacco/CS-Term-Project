Pitch: place and setting

A pitch can now say where and when: a place (Tokyo), a setting or setup (a train, a road trip), a season, a time of day, an atmosphere. Each one is a chip in the chips' own style ("Set in Tokyo", "At night"), removable like the rest, and a soft boost, never a filter. Films carrying more of the asked settings lead; the reason line starts with the setting in the person's own words. Same on web and iPhone (the iPhone draws the server's chips and lines). One red per screen: the sentence's bar. Match % are illustrative; the middle list and its lines are the eval's output (TMDB keywords only, before our own tags).
