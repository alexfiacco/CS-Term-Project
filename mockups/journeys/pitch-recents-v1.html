Pitch: recents, chips that teach, and a friend in the sentence

Three additions to the Pitch drawer as it is live on main (RecutScreen in Recut.swift, on the app's own .drawer; the web's RecutSheet mirrors it). Recents: your last three pitches, one a row, each with × to take it off the list; a tap opens that pitch again and does not spend a free one. Chips that teach: the "Or try" examples, rewritten so each one shows a different thing the parser in lib/recut.ts reads in full (a film to start from, a feeling, genre and length, decade and language, a friend). @friend: "for me and @Sam" ranks for both of you, by the lower of your two matches with your Blend's films first, the same ranking Browse's "For you and @friend" uses. Everything else is the live drawer: fonts, colours, spacing, the round close, Sorted for you on its own line with the count and "Just pick one" on one baseline under it, and one red per screen.

From an empty drawer to results for two

The live drawer first, then the recommended version (A for placement, A for @friend) in five states.

Live today

What ships now. The box, Pitch it, the free count and three fixed examples. Nothing remembers what you asked yesterday, and nothing says you can pitch for two.

1 · Empty, recents and chips

Recents under Pitch it, chips under recents. Three recents at most, newest first, each with ×. A tap reopens that pitch's list, free. The five chips each teach one thing the parser reads. The drawer is as tall as this content (.fit), not a fixed half.

2 · Keyboard up

Tap the box and the drawer rides up. Recents stay above the keyboard, where a thumb is; the chips slide under it, which is fine: you are writing your own. Typing filters recents to the ones that share a word.

3 · Typing @

@ opens the friend list in place of recents. Your friends who share their ratings, last watched together first, then by Blend. "@s" narrows it. The chip "Something for me and @friend" types the words and opens this list in one tap.

4 · A friend tagged

Sam becomes a token in the sentence: face and name, one backspace removes it. The line under the box says what changes. One friend per pitch; more people is a movie night (see the note).

5 · Results for two

Same answer layout, ranked for both. "With Sam" is a chip like the others, × to rank for you alone. Each row shows both matches; a film already in your Blend says so. The quote's bar is this screen's one red.

Options

Left: where recents and chips sit in the empty drawer. Right: how the friend shows in the sentence. The recommended pair is the one drawn above.

Recents and chips · A Recommended

Stacked: box, Pitch it, Recent, Or try. Everything visible without a tap, read top to bottom in the order you'd use it. Costs height: the drawer opens taller than half.

Recents and chips · B

Chips as one swiping row under the box. Shorter drawer, but four of the five chips start off screen, and a chip half cut off teaches nothing.

Recents and chips · C

Recents only once you tap the box, like a search history. The empty drawer stays half height. But reopening last night's list is a tap you make without typing, so hiding recents behind the keyboard hides their best use.

@friend · A Recommended

A token in the sentence: face and name in a soft blue tag. You can see who it's for in the words you wrote, and the server gets an id, not a guess at a name.

@friend · B

Plain blue "@sam_r" text, with the friend repeated as a chip under the box. Simpler to build (no token in a text field) but says it twice, and a handle reads as code next to a sentence.

@friend · C

Not in the sentence at all: a "Watching with" row of faces under the box, Tonight's Who's watching. Clear, but it is a form beside the sentence, and Pitch's promise is one sentence.

Recommendation

Placement A, @friend A. Stacked recents and chips cost some height, but every piece is visible and does one job: recents bring back a list you already paid for, chips teach what Pitch reads. Cap recents at three and chips at five so the drawer never needs to scroll empty.

Chips picked from what the parser reads in full. Each one produces only chips and no "Not used" line: Like Arrival but scarier (From Arrival, Scary ↑), Feels like a rainy Sunday (Warmth ↑), A slow-burn thriller, not too long (Pace ↓, Tension ↑, Thriller, Under 95 min), A 90s crime film, no subtitles (Crime, 1990s, No subtitles), and the friend chip. Two suggested lines were left out on purpose: "for tonight" reads as Not used: tonight, and "under 2 hours" is not read (the keyword parser knows under 90, 95 or 100, and "not too long").

One red per screen. On the ask, the red dash before PITCH (RedDash, as rule 1120 puts it before a page title). On the answer, the quote's live red bar, so the kicker has no dash there. The token, the picker and the match numbers are blue, which means "yours".

Tonight and Movie Night: who owns "for me and @friend"?

Keep it in Pitch as a lens, and hand off at the end. All three already rank for two the same way (the lower of the two matches; Browse's lens and Tonight's Who's watching), so Pitch adds no new engine, only a way to say it in the sentence.

  • Pitch is "I can describe it": words, plus one friend.
  • Tonight is "I can't describe it": moods and Who's watching, up to three. Its "Pitch it ›" already hands its moods to Pitch; it should hand its one friend too, as a token.
  • Movie Night is "we have to agree": a room where people vote. Pitch should not grow voting. With a friend tagged, the end of the list offers "Start a movie night with these", seeding the room with the shortlist.

One friend per pitch. Two or more people is where Tonight's Who's watching and Movie Night are better. Typing a second @ shows a line pointing there instead of a second token.

What it takes to build

  • Parser. The client sends the friend as an id (with: [friendId]), never as a name to read. lib/recut.ts strips the "for me and @x" phrase before the keyword parser and the model see the sentence, so "@sam" is never an anchor and never Not used. RecutQuery gains with, which chipsFor shows as "With Sam" and withoutChip removes. A re-rank keeps it.
  • Ranking. recut-server.ts scores the shortlist for the friend too (the same pair scoring as Browse's lens: lower match leads, only friends who share ratings), lifts films in the pair's Blend, and returns both matches per row and an inBlend flag.
  • Recents. No new table. recuts already holds each person's sentence, id and time, so recents are their last three rows. The × needs one column, alter table public.recuts add column if not exists hidden_recent boolean not null default false;, so the share link keeps working. RLS is already on with no policies (server only), so nothing else changes. The owner runs that SQL.
  • Reopening a recent is free: it loads the stored query and re-ranks (op: "rank"), never counted.
