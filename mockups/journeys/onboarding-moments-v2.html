Your link, claimed

When: the handle step, right after the save drawer, for every new account (taste test and Letterboxd alike). It replaces the small archetype chip on the iPhone; the web gains it above the field.

  • The card is the profile header friends land on: the rounded-square face, the archetype, its streak, the count, the handle. The link under it follows the field as they type, and dims while a handle is Taken.
  • One red: the oxblood rule under the name, as on the reveal. The field's ✓ Available keeps its blue.
  • Under 15 rated (a thin import), the card reads "Your first look" in place of a name, as rule 239 asks.
  • Why: the one chore after the peak becomes claiming something worth sending, and it sets up the reveal's Send.
  • Web difference: the web keeps its eyebrow, "Your handle" and its lead line, and has no "Who invited you?".

We guessed it

When: from the 16th rating to the 30th, on film cards only, once the forming read is fresh. Never before 15 (rules 202, 853) and never on a run back from the reveal.

  • Before: under the stars, faint, "We have a guess. Rate it to check." The guess is sealed with the card before it shows, so it can never follow the tap.
  • After: the line under the stars becomes "We guessed 4.5★ · Called it ✓", grey then blue (rule 1069's words). A miss reads "We guessed 3★ · You liked it more than we thought" in cream, never red. The deck waits 0.6 s longer on these cards.
  • At 30 the end slide adds one line, "We called 11 of the 15 we guessed." No badge, no confetti (rule 850).
  • Why: each tap pays once a read exists, as Beli answers every ranking, and the 30 feels earned.
  • Web: the same line under TasteTest.tsx's stars; no wall behind it.
