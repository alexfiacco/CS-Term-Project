Recommendation: keep the bell

It is the only candidate a first-time user reads as "notifications" without learning it. The tray and envelope say mail, the bubbles say chat, and the three cinema shapes (stub, frame, ring) say tickets, films and recording: the marquee already failed for this reason, it read as a ticket.

It collides with nothing. The app's other bells (Blend mute, Sharpen reminder, the push promise in Blend's how-it-works) all mean "notify me", so the header bell agrees with them. Ticket, film, chat, message and mail are each already taken.

It holds the badge best. The bell's open upper right takes the capsule without covering the shape; on the envelope, frame and ring the badge sits on a corner or a dot and they read as two marks.

What the bell gives up, and how to pay it back

Brand. It is generic, but the header row already has two brand marks (the fan and the Bodoni wordmark) a few points away; a third cinema shape would crowd them, as the film frame row shows.

The You tab. A bell does not say "your own activity". That is fine: the button's label is "Inbox", and You is the second tab inside, not the door. Keep the accessibility label "Inbox, 3 new".

If the owner wants a second choice: the plain tray (2), never the arrow variant, which is the download icon. It matches the word Inbox but reads weaker as notifications to phone users.
