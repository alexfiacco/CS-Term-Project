"Watched with": who was there

A film night is rarely solo, but a Montage log is. Proposed: one optional row on the "When did you watch it?" step, Watched with, where you tag friends who were there. Each tagged friend gets a one-tap "Add it to my films?" (nothing lands on their profile without them). Lately then shows the night as one post ("@jules and @sam_r watched it together"), and your log line on the film page keeps who was there. It also tells Montage who you watch with, which fills the "For you and @sam_r" chip.

Today · When step

Stars, then when. Who you were with isn't asked anywhere.

Proposed · Watched with

One row under When. Friends you watch with most come first; Friday's movie night is offered as one tap. Optional: Done works with nobody tagged.

Proposed · the friend's side

@jules gets one Activity row. Add it logs the film with the same date and asks for their own stars; Not me removes the tag quietly.

Proposed · Lately

For a mutual friend, one post for the night instead of two separate watches, with both people's stars.

Proposed · film page, your log

Your watches keep who was there. A line counts nights together and opens your taste match with that friend.

Open for you

Who can be tagged: mutual friends only (drawn), or anyone you follow? Visibility: the tag shows only to people who can already see both friends' activity; someone with sharing off can't be tagged. Auto-add: drawn as a confirm on the friend's side; auto-adding would be faster but logs films for people without asking.
