World cinema: ready-made shelves in Browse

Country or language, crossed with a genre (and sometimes an era): Korean thrillers, French New Wave, Japanese animation. Not lanes (plot types that drive taste) and not themes (what a story is about): each is a saved Browse filter with a name, a line and a page. Counts and titles below are the live catalog (films only, no duplicates, with a poster). Posters are stand-ins with the real title on them.

The proposed shelves

Each rule is a filter on fields films already have: original language, production countries, genres, year. "Strong" = 10,000+ Letterboxd watches and 3.5★+; a shelf needs a dozen to stand up. Top six by Letterboxd watch count. Red = thin, keep only if you like it.

How they'd be built

Filter presets, not listsA shelf is a Browse preset with a title, like Hidden gems today: { languages: ["ko"], genres: any of Thriller, Crime, Mystery }. New films join on their own; the page ranks by your match like every door. One list in lib/browse-doors.ts feeds the sheet, the pages, the sitemap and iPhone.
Two new filter bitsStrict language (Browse's language filter lets animation and silent films through, which would put Ghibli in Korean thrillers), and a countries filter (Hong Kong, Taiwan, Mexico, the Nordics). Countries are filled on 52,114 of 70,088 films, so country rules undercount; language rules don't.
A light hand where rules missCo-productions leak: "United Kingdom" alone gave British crime The Dark Knight and Fargo, fixed by "UK first, no US". French New Wave by years alone takes in Le Samouraï; a director list (Godard, Truffaut, Varda, Rohmer, Chabrol, Demy, Resnais, Rivette, Malle) would make it exact. A short per-shelf exclude list covers the rest.
