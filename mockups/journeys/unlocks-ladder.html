What moves the bar (small tasks only)

  1. Rate a few in "Have you seen these?" (Picks bar, Profile bar, Sharpen).
  2. Seen it on Picks: already counts and bumps the count live (rule 850).
  3. Search a film and rate it from its page: already counts.
  4. Import from Letterboxd: one jump, one summary card.
  5. Compare with a friend: "What's our match?" link or a nudge. It moves the friend match, not your count.

Never: watch counts, weekly targets, streak goals.

Owner questions: which numbers are public

  1. Q1 · 30. Name it as a goal ("Rate 8 more to firm up your Film DNA", which implies 30), or only "firms up as you rate", with no count? Today 30 is printed in four places.
  2. Q2 · 50, 100, 150. Drop them from Sharpen and TasteSoFar? (Recommended.)
  3. Q3 · "Film DNA at 15". A dashed read at 15 and solid at 30 (as mocked), or the full radar at 15 with no 30 rung? Code says 30.
  4. Q4 · A friend's count. Show "Sam needs 4 more" (shows Sam's count to followers), or "Sam is still rating"?
  5. Q5 · The nudge. Allowed? Once per pair, push or in-app only?
  6. Q6 · 15 itself. Rule 850 hides it, while five other strings print it. Make 15 the one public number everywhere, or hide it everywhere?

Where this turns gamey or naggy

  1. Too many bars at once. The Picks bar, the Profile bar, the Sharpen ring and the deck header all say "rate N more". Rule: one per screen, and they share one count.
  2. Goals past 30. 50 / 100 / 150 are model limits; as goals they are levels in disguise.
  3. "A match you can trust". It tells people the match they had was not trustworthy.
  4. Nudges. Once per pair, never from a group.
  5. Toasts for every step. Two moments in total (15, 30), plus one import summary.

Skip: a separate unlocks hub, a locked movie night, moments at 50+, any count on someone else's profile without Q4.
