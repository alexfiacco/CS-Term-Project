Chips that pick films

Today Genre chips mean any of these, Feel-good quietly adds Comedy, Family and Romance, and "every chip" holds only when plenty of films fit, otherwise it falls back to two of three without a word. Feel-good + Action + Thriller gave Legally Blonde (the one watchlist slot) and Tangled. Proposed: every genre chip is required, moods are a score and never add a hidden genre, a clashing pair is read as one lane and the screen says so in one quiet line, and when nothing fits the result says which chip it dropped. Movie night rooms obey the same rules. iPhone 390, Now beside Proposed.

1 · The chip picker

Tonight, scrolled to the moods with More filters open (drawn 88 taller than an iPhone 15 so the moods and every genre show in one frame). Feel-good is a mood (the top section), Action and Thriller are genres (inside More filters). Only the note beside GENRE and one line under the chips change.

Now

Two genres on: "all of these first, then any". With one or none, "any of these". Nothing says Feel-good and Thriller pull apart.

Proposed

The note says what now happens, "all of these", with any number on. The clash gets one sans 13 muted line under the genre chips.

Wording

Genre note: all of these, the same two-word shape as today's any of these and Decade's 1 selected. Shown always, so the rule is read before the first tap. Films with all of these also works but repeats what the chips plainly are.

The reading: Feel-good + Thriller · light thrillers and capers. It borrows today's genreBoth shape (Horror + Comedy · 24 films are both): the pair, a dot, what it means. No icon, no box, no colour: muted 13, 12 under the chips, gone when the clash is. Two lanes, not three: … light thrillers, capers, action-comedies wraps to two lines at 390, and one line is the point.

Where: under the Genre chips, because the genre tap makes the clash and the Genre group is where the eye is. It also rides on the result (frame 2), so it is still said when More filters is folded.

Other pairs: Cozy + Thriller · gentle mysteries, capers; Funny + Horror · horror-comedies; Feel-good + Crime · capers and buddy-cop comedies; Funny + Thriller · action-comedies and comic thrillers. One list, written once, used by Tonight, rooms and the web.

3 · When nothing fits: say what was dropped

Feel-good + Action + Thriller + Horror. No film has all four. Today it would quietly fall back to "most of them"; proposed, it drops one chip in the fixed order and says so, in the notice line the result already has.

Proposed · the notice

One line where model.notice already sits, a quiet Drop another on its right. The recap's existing "Not quite" says it again on the ticket.

Proposed · after Drop another

The chips the empty state already uses (× Feel-good …) open under the line. Horror, dropped now, is the dashed one; a tap on another re-runs with Horror back.

Wording

The line: Nothing fits all four, so these skip Horror. Counts in words up to four (all three, all four), the chip by its own name. Skip over dropped: it says the picks lack Horror, not that the person's choice was deleted (Horror stays on in the setup).

The action: Drop another, a quiet text button (meta semibold, 44 tall target) on the same baseline, the slot Try again uses today. It opens Drop one instead: with the chips; tapping × Action re-runs as Feel-good + Thriller + Horror. No undo needed: Horror was never removed.

Rejected: Keep Horror instead (it does not say what goes in its place), a banner or a toast (the owner's no-decoration rule; it would also vanish before the poster is read).

Two dropped: Nothing fits all four, so these skip Horror and Thriller. Moods are never dropped this way: they are a score, so a film can be less Feel-good without a notice.

4 · Movie night: one picked Feel-good, one picked Thriller

The vote, as built (RoomVote voteHead and cards). You asked for Feel-good; Sam asked for Thriller (a genre, so today the room's clash check never sees it).

Now

The room splits the four between the two asks: two cosy comedies for you, two grim thrillers for Sam. Each card fits one of you.

Proposed

The clash is read as one lane, said in one line under the vote's header (the slot picks.notice uses). Every card fits both.

Wording

The note: Feel-good + Thriller · light thrillers and capers, the same words as Tonight. Not You and Sam want …: the faces and What the room asked for already say who.

Placement: 12 under the header's tools row, muted 13, above the first card, so it is read before a vote and stays put as votes come in.

The genre hint in a room (RoomScreen ~652, guest join ~1019): Shows films in any of these. becomes Shows films with all of these.; the guest row's 2 selected · any of these becomes 2 selected · all of these.

Across people: a room's genres are each person's ask, not one person's AND. The room requires what everyone shares and scores the rest; the clash check now looks at genre chips too, which is what turns this pair into one lane.
