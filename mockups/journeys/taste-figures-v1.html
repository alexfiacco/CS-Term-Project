  • What C gives up: "Crowd 3.7" leaves the row when all three figures show. The comparison with the crowd is the third figure's whole job ("3% against the crowd"), so the row still answers "how do I compare". With two figures (no Against the crowd yet, under 30 rated films with a crowd average), the average keeps both parts as today: "from 1,260 ratings · crowd 3.7", split on two lines under 375.
