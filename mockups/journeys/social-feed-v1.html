Lately: tap the person, tap the film

The owner, comparing Beli's feed: "on our Lately feed you can't click the person who did the activity until you click in. Make it where you can click their profile and also see their film and click their film." Today the face rides on the poster's corner, so tapping it opens the film, and the name is plain text; any other tap opens the post's drawer, and only there does the name reach the profile. Proposed, a slight redesign: each post leads with the person's rounded-square photo, the line reads "Emma rated Prisoners" with the name tapping to the profile and the title to the film, the poster moves to the right with their rating as a small stamp on it (4.5★, in our serif, not a score circle), the take sits in full in Newsreader, "with @alex" taps to Alex, and like, comment and share sit on the post itself. Blue dashed boxes mark each tap target at 44pt or more. iPhone and the web, five kinds of post each: rated with a take, logged without stars, watched with a friend, a grouped day, a save. The feed has no pitches or cuts today, so none are drawn. Drawn from ActivityRows.tsx (FriendFeed) and SocialScreen.swift (single, grouped) on main. Names are made up.

Today, for comparison
  • The poster and the face on it are one button: the film.
  • The name is text. Tapping it opens the drawer, and the drawer's name row is what reaches the profile: two taps.
  • "Jo and Alex watched it together": neither name is a link.
  • A grouped day's face: the profile on the iPhone, nothing of its own on the web.
  • Likes and comments are counts only; liking needs the drawer or a long press.

  • The house rating format, a number then the star. The serif title. Spacing, no cards. Spoiler takes stay hidden until tapped.
  • One red per screen: the dash under Lately. Selected states (the People tab, Social in the bar, a liked heart) are the logo blue.

Recommendation: ship Proposed

  • It answers the owner directly: the person and the film are each one tap from the feed, and both are named in one plain sentence, "Emma rated Prisoners".
  • It is a slight change, not a new feed: the same posts, data and drawer. The face moves off the poster to lead the row, the poster moves right and takes the rating stamp, the take and the actions come out of the drawer onto the post.
  • The photo leading the row makes it read as a person's post first, which is what a friends' feed is. The poster and stamp on the right keep the film easy to scan.
  • Both platforms change together: one row recipe for FriendFeed on the web and single()/grouped() on the iPhone, plus a fix the web lacks today (the grouped day's face goes to the profile).

Watch for

  • Posts get taller with the action row (about 150pt against 117pt). Ten a page still works; the take clamps at three lines.
  • Your fit moves from a big blue number on the right to the time line. If the owner wants it louder, it can sit under the stamp instead.
  • "with @alex" uses the handle as asked; the feed otherwise uses first names (rule 522), so "with Alex" may read more like the rest.
  • Takes lose their italics (reading text is never italic). The web's .ff-take and the iPhone's FeedTakeLine both italicise today.
