Poster badges: reading the number at a glance

User feedback: the stars ("3★") and the match % on posters are hard to read while scrolling Browse, Picks, a profile's Films and lane pages. iPhone at 390, real TMDB posters, hard cases on purpose: 2001 (white and yellow at the bottom left), Little Miss Sunshine (yellow), Fantastic Mr. Fox, Se7en and The Prestige (busy and dark), The Lion King, Midsommar, Parasite.

Contrast below is measured from the render, not guessed: each page is drawn twice, once as shown and once with the badge glyphs made transparent. Fig = how tall the digits actually are, in points. Text = WCAG ratio of the badge colour against the lightest 5% of what sits behind the glyphs. Ink = WCAG ratio of every rendered glyph pixel against its own background (median), and the share of glyph pixels that reach 4.5:1: that is where thin hairlines, tilt and small size show up. Edge = the badge's outer colour against the poster just around it (3:1 is the non-text target).
