The Pitch button, in a type of its own

The owner: "I think we can stylize the pitch button, like at least the text, the font in the pitch button." Five ways to set the words "Pitch" and "Pitch it", each in context: Browse's pinned search row (rule 1113) and the Pitch drawer. Drawn from main: .pitch-btn and PitchButton (44 tall pill, --panel-2, the fan mark at 18, 7 apart, 14 in), .recut-go and PrimaryButtonStyle (the cream pill, 44), and the drawer's ask. The drawer is renamed: the caps "Pitch" kicker goes and its one title is Pitch me a film; the button stays Pitch it. Only the words change in options 1 to 3 and 5; the mark, the sizes and the colours stay.

Recommendation: 1, Bodoni roman, sentence case

It gives Pitch the film voice without shouting. Bodoni is the face for film titles and section heads (rule 443). Set at the card size, 18 and 600, "Pitch" reads as an invitation next to a search field that stays in the plain UI face, so the two halves of the row stop looking like one control split in two.

It matches what is already in the drawer. The example pitches under "Or try" are Bodoni 500 at 18, and Tonight's own cream ticket button, "Just pick for me", is Bodoni 600 at 18. "Pitch it" in the same face makes the drawer one voice from title to chips.

It costs nothing in space or rules. The button grows by about 6 points, the field still shows "Search films or people" in full, the mark keeps its blue, and no red is added. On the web it is one line, font: 600 var(--fs-card)/1 var(--serif) on .pitch-btn and .recut-go; on the iPhone Typo.card(.semibold) in PitchButton and on the Pitch it label.

Why not the others

2, Bodoni caps looks like a title card, but tracked capitals in a hairline face get thin at 14 on black, and the drawer would then have caps on the button and roman on the chips.

3, House caps is the label voice: it says "section", not "do this". It also makes the button the quietest thing on the row.

4, the P monogram drops the fan mark the owner chose today in recut-v9, and a letter in a circle reads as an avatar.

5, bigger and bolder is the right instinct if 1 feels too quiet: keep it in mind as a step up. At 700 and 20 the Bodoni contrast gets heavy, and the Large 50 button outweighs the box it submits.
