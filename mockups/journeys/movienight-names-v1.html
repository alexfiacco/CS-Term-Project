Movie Night: a name on the card

Today the share card says who was there only as initials. A night with a name reads as someone's night, which is what makes a stranger tap "Start your own". Saved groups already have a name (the creator sets it in Edit group, and a night started from a group is already tagged with it). This adds the name to the card for those nights, and for a one-off night the host gets a suggested name from the room's archetype, which can become a saved group in one tap. Optional at every step; no member names ever reach the card.

1 · Today

The card as shipped in the share mock: film, the night, initials only, the archetype. Nothing says whose night it was.

2 · A saved group's night

The night was started from Sunday Horror Club, so the card reads "Sunday Horror Club · Movie Night": the name in Bodoni, as a title, under the wordmark. The date moves to the corner; the rest is unchanged. The name is the group's own, so nobody sets anything.

3 · A one-off night: a suggested name

Once the film is picked the host sees a name built from the archetype. Rule: "The <archetype word> <count word>", or the plural form when the archetype is a noun, and a collective word past six: The Nihilist Four · Three Old Souls · The Midnight Cult. Edit and Remove are the host's only; everyone else sees the name, read-only. Remove and the card is today's card.

4 · Keep this group?

Asked once, after the host keeps or edits the name. One tap makes tonight's people a saved group with that name: signed-in members are in now, guests get the group's invite link to join when they like. "Not now" is the same size and the same weight; nobody is told unless it's kept.

5 · The one-off night's card

The same card with the name the host kept. Kept as a group or not, the card is identical: the name belongs to the night first.

The cards, a third of real size

Open for you

Who can rename

Saved group: the creator only, in Edit group as today (rule 162); the card follows the group's current name.

One-off night: the host only, until the night ends. Once kept as a group, the creator (the host) renames it in Edit group.

Guests and the name

Guests see it, never edit it: in the room, on the result screen and on the card they share.

Members other than the host see it read-only too. Suggest: no "suggest a name" for anyone else in v1.

Max length

28 characters on the card (fits two lines of the Post card; tested with "The Friday Night Arthouse Co").

Edit group allows 60 today. A longer group name: cut at a word with "…" on the card only, or cap new names at 28? Suggest: cut on the card, leave Edit group at 60.

On the public card

Yes, it's the point: the name and "Movie Night", nothing else new.

No member names or handles, still initials only. A name the host types runs through the same word filter as group names before it can reach a card.
