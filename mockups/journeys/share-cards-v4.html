Share cards v4: one red per card, mixed across the set

The owner on v3: the spine looks good, but on every card it gets a little annoying and the cards all look the same. v4 keeps one red accent per card and mixes three: the spine, a subtle shadow, the red dash. The set reads as one family (same ink, same Bodoni, same pill, same oxblood) without one mark stamped on everything. Plus a pick ticket rebuilt from scratch (three options), the reel retired for plain rows everywhere, and every reading line in Bodoni roman. v3 word counts kept on every card (counted live under each one).
