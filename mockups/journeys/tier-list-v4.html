Tiers v4: four directions for the colours

v3's ramps were all muted takes on one idea. These four are different ideas, on the same auto frame (Montage's tiers for Christopher Nolan, the full scroll): D only Montage's own colours (Canon in the app's red, Essential in the match blue, Good night cream, then greys); E a warm duotone like the house grade, no blocks, the name's colour carries the tier; F no row or block colour at all, only a Browse-style caps label with a short rule and a thin top edge on each poster; G a richer cinema palette (crimson, burnt gold, forest, petrol, charcoal) in v3's label block. Contrast for each, then the pick on the Story and Post card.
