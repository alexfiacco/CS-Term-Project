Share cards v4 (g): the red dash as the primary accent

An experiment the owner asked for: four cards that carry the spine in v4, redrawn with the red dash in its place, to see the dash as Montage's primary mark. The dash is quieter and sits inside the layout, next to words; the spine frames the whole card and reads first at chat size. Our take: the dash holds up at every size and is the most refined of the three, but it is also the quietest: in a chat full of cards the spine is what says Montage first. Keep the dash as one of three accents (it already leads 7 of the 23 cards) rather than the only one; if the owner wants it primary, these four show it works.
