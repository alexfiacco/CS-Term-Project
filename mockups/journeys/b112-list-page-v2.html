Why these keep a plain count

The progress line means "how far through this set you are", which only reads when the set is finite and someone chose it: a list, a person's films, a lane, a mix made for you.

Browse is the whole catalogue: "seen 3% of 4,210" is a line that never moves, so it says 4,210 unseen ▾. Your Films are what you've seen, so a seen stat would always be full: 1,262 films. The Watchlist is what you mean to see next; the useful number is what's left: 837 to watch ▾.

Fit: each row at its natural width with the longest sort that page offers, never shortened. Room: 343pt at 375, 361pt at 393.
