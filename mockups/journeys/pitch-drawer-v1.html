The top fit as Tonight's ticket, inside the same drawer. Next fit steps down the 13. Back returns to the shortlist as it was. Swipe down closes Pitch and you are on Browse where you started.

Recommendation: make Pitch a drawer

It is a question, not a place. You ask, look at a short list, and either open a film or go back to browsing. A drawer says that: Browse stays under it, and swiping down puts you back where you were. A full page makes Pitch feel like a section you have to leave.

It matches Tonight. The two "find me a film" tools become siblings: same panel, same grab bar, same round close, same ticket at the end. Just pick one for me already uses Tonight's ticket, so inside a drawer the hand-off is seamless.

Half height first, full on results. Empty, it needs only the box, Pitch it and three examples, so half height is honest and quick to dismiss. The shortlist needs room, so the drawer grows. Opening a film from a row pushes the film page over the drawer; back returns to the list.

The web already does this. On the web Pitch is RecutSheet in a PortalModal, a sheet over the page. So this brings the iPhone in line with the web, not the other way round.
