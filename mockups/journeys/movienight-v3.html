Verdicts

  • Lobby: needs uplift. Three headers, faces twice, a raw URL.
  • Invite landing: needs uplift. Sign in leads; the guest door is under the fold.
  • Mood step: fine. Rule 1400 fixed it today (≈30 words). Only change: drop the Movie night bar like every other step.
  • Weighing: fine. Keep the count.
  • Vote: needs uplift. A, the big poster.
  • Tally and wait: needs uplift. Lock in is off screen for the host.
  • Reveal: uplift the top. Keep the ticket, add Watch on.
  • Room chat: fine. A plain thread with system lines; leave it.
  • Across all steps: the "Movie night" caps bar and the labelled rail cost ≈90pt on every screen. The rail becomes four slim bars under the top row.
  • Add to calendar: a room has no start time; it is live tonight. Only worth it with a "set for 8pm" option (new column).
