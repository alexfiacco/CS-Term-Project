The DM film card, seven new looks part 1 of 2: directions 1 to 4

Round 3. The owner on v2: "There needs to be buttons and it needs to look better." So every direction here starts again from the brand, not from v1's Print, and every one ends in a real row of pill buttons: Save (or Rewatch on a film you have rated), then Watch on Hulu when the film is on your service, else Open. Saved turns the first pill blue with a filled bookmark. The line on each card is yours: the blue "91% for you" stamp if you have not seen it, "Rated 5★" if you have. The oxblood offset is on the newest card only. Same thread with Sam in every phone; each card's height in pt is printed beside it. Directions 5 to 7, today's card, every height and the recommendation (1, Backdrop band) are on dm-card-v3b.

Every direction keeps

  • Two pills, 34pt (44pt tap area), never a box cut by lines. Left is the save; right is where to go next.
  • The tap on the rest of the card opens the film; hold reacts, swipe replies, as now.
  • The note is Bodoni roman, never italic; the meta is year · director.

The second pill

  • Watch on Hulu when the film streams on one of your services (the same lookup Movie Night's card already does, sharerService), so the card answers "where?" in one tap.
  • Otherwise Open, which goes to the film page. It repeats the card tap on purpose: people look for a button.

Stand ins in this mock

  • The mock has posters only, so the backdrops and stills (directions 1, 3, 5, 7) are poster crops. The build uses TMDB backdrop_path, which is a real wide still and looks better than drawn here.
  • Direction 4's colours are picked by eye from each poster; the build samples them once and caches.
