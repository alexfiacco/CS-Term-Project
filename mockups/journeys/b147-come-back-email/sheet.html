A · Recommended: a short note, reply to answer
Personal and plain: "Did Montage miss something?" and "hit reply: it comes straight to me". Replies land in your inbox (reply-to set to you), so early users can tell you anything. Recommended: at this size, a real reply is worth more than a tally, and a note from a person gets answered more than a survey.
B · One-tap answers
Five chips ("The picks weren't for me", "Not enough friends on it", "I forgot about it", "Something broke", "Something else"); a tap records the answer on a small thank-you page (needs building). Countable later at scale, but you learn less per answer.
