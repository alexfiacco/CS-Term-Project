Recommendation: A

A answers the note directly with the fewest new parts. Space and surfaces do the separating: the ask is one panel, history is one card, the ideas are their own named block. It reuses what the app already has (panel, field, Bodoni heading 22, the pills) and keeps the order people already know. B is the prettiest at a glance but adds a poster list to maintain and makes the ideas louder than Action!. C is right for a first visit (no history yet) but wrong for the owner's daily use.
  • Same build on web (Recut.tsx + globals.css) and iPhone (Recut.swift ask block): Space.s32/s40 gaps, Typo.heading for the two section names, one panel card for recents.
  • Saved cuts, when someone has any, take the same treatment as Previous takes: a Bodoni 22 heading and one card.
  • The friend idea ("Something for me and @") shows as a fifth pill when friends exist; A's wrapping pills take it with no change.
  • Red count: one, the dash under PITCH. No italics. No em dashes. No "Open" button.
