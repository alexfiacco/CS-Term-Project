Recommendation on the name: "Tune your picks"

It is a verb people act on, it names what it changes (Picks, not Screen DNA), and it reuses the Settings section's own word, Tune for you. It also lets the Picks deck card say "Tune your picks ›" and land on a page with that exact title, which is what makes the door learnable. "What we've learned" survives as the summary's job, not the page's name. Honest cost: it is untested with users, and the menu and Settings rows change words that rules 1780, 1783 and 2031 pin, so it needs the owner's amendment and the check updated.

Not recommended: "Your picks, tuned" (passive, a report again), "Tune what you see" (vague: Tonight and Pitch also obey Less of this, but people think in Picks).
