Recommendation

Name: Pitch. Still the only word that passes all three uses cleanly. None of its fifteen relatives beat it: Tip is the best cousin ("Got a tip?" is lovely, "3 free tips left" counts cleanly) but sits awkwardly next to a paid limit, where "tip" means money. Cue stays the cool alternative with a weaker counter. Shout is great in the UK ("good shout") and reads as yelling in the US. Hook, Toss, Brief and Tease each break in the counter. Prompt and Logline are the ones the owner already ruled out: AI and jargon.

Icon: the throw into a frame (Pitch 1). It is the only mark that says both halves of the name: an arc, the motion of pitching, landing in a film frame. At 20px it stays two shapes, a curve and a square, so it never turns to mush beside the search glass, and it looks like nothing else in the app. Large, it becomes a proper hero mark.

Runner-up 1: Pitch with the film-frame speech bubble (Pitch 2). The most instantly readable (you say something, about film), and the safest. Less ownable: chat apps have bubbles.

Runner-up 2: Cue with the cue mark (Cue 2). The reel-change dot in the corner of the frame is a real projectionist's cue, a lovely detail for people who know. Cleanest icon on the board, but only if the name is Cue.

Dropped: the director's chair (too many parts at 20px), the mic (podcast), the spark (reads as AI), and the megaphone (reads as announcements).
