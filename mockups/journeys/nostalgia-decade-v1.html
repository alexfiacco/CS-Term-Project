When did you grow up watching movies?

The owner, 2026-11-06: "Add a decade you grew up in question to onboarding? Yeah sure. Show me where that would come in." Nostalgic is a streak, never the name: someone who rates the films of their childhood years above the crowd and goes back to them. Today it is read blind: Montage knows no age, only 7 of 21 libraries carry rewatch data, and the fixed 1980 to 2010 rule finds it on 2 of 21 (theorist-dry-run.md). One optional tap, "the 90s", turns "an earlier era" into your years. Below: three places it could be asked (A, B, C), what happens after each, how the answer is used, what the profile shows, and what you need to decide.

Asks everyone, on day one

The deck already stops for four one-tap questions, as cards in the reel. The decade becomes a fifth, single-select: one tap shows the pick for a beat (350ms, as today) and the reel glides on. It adds no step to the progress bar: question cards never count (rule 39), so the bar and "13 of 30" hold still while the eyebrow reads "Quick one · 4 of 5". Skipping is a quiet link (or just swiping on, which today already counts a card as answered with no pick).

A2 · Or a screen of its own

The brief's other spot: after "Keep going", before the first deck card. It reads worse: the bar freezes at "9 of 30" on a screen with no films, and it is one more full screen between "Rate films you love" and the deck for every new person. If A, take the reel card.

Where in the reel

  • Questions sit after every 4 cards (QUESTION_EVERY), so a fifth lands after card 20, past the read at 15. Put it fourth, right after "Older and black-and-white films?" (the two read as a pair), and move "Older" to third.
  • Same on iPhone: TasteDeckModel mirrors lib/taste-test/questions.ts.
  • It is not a taste answer: no axis nudge, no boost, no picks lean. It only feeds the Nostalgic read.

The case against

  • Every new person meets it, including the ones with too few older films for it to ever matter.
  • Asking an age-shaped question before someone has seen any payoff is the moment it feels most like a form.
  • It lengthens the deck that the onboarding audit just made faster (rule 1143, ADVANCE_MS 250).

B
Later, on the Taste tab: asked once it can actually matter
Recommended

No onboarding step. One card on your own Taste tab, under the archetype, shown once: at 30 rated (Film DNA firms up, Sharpen's last step) and only if you have rated 12 or more films from before 2015, so there is something for the answer to read. One tap answers; ✕ or Skip hides it for good (the field stays in Edit profile, C). Visitors never see the card.

B1 · The card, at 30 rated
Red on screen: 0, the card adds none
Under the archetype, the streak line and the mix bar. One tap on a chip saves it. The privacy line is on the card itself.
B2 · After the tap
Red on screen: 0
The card shrinks to one line: "Noted: the 90s. Only you see this." The read runs again; if the streak now holds, the header gains it in place, last, with no share of the mix bar, and a one-time toast says so.
B3 · You tap "Nostalgic"
Red on screen: 1, the dash under the label
Streak names in the header are buttons (rule from bad00a27); Nostalgic opens the streak card, not an archetype lookup. On your own profile it shows why: your years and the films in them you rate above the crowd.
B4 · A friend taps it
Red on screen: 1, the dash under the label
Someone else's view: the streak and its directory description and films. Never the decade, never the years, never "your 90s" and never the films that tripped it (they would give the age away).

If it does not hold yet

✓
Noted: the 90s.
If your 90s favorites stand out, a Nostalgic streak shows up here. Only you see this.
  • No verdict, no "you are not nostalgic". The line goes on the next visit.
  • From then on every nightly read checks it, so the streak can arrive later with more ratings.

Why B

  • Zero added friction at signup. The deck stays as the audit left it.
  • Asked when it can pay off: at 30 rated with enough older films, the answer can change the header the same minute.
  • The headline says why in plain words: "we'll spot your nostalgia picks".
  • Same card can live in Sharpen your profile's Next section for people who open that instead (one card, two doors, answered once).

Costs

  • Fewer people answer than in A (only those who reach 30 and visit their Taste tab).
  • People who import a big Letterboxd library pass 30 at once: they see it on their first profile visit, which is fine (and they are the ones with the data to use it).

C
Edit profile only: a passive field
Needed whichever you pick

A field in Edit profile, under Bio and the two toggles, that nobody is asked to fill. On its own it will be filled by almost no one, so as the only placement Nostalgic stays on today's blind rule. Its real job is to be where you change or clear it later, whether you answered in A or B.

C1 · Not set
Red on screen: 0
"Grew up watching movies in", "Not set", the same five chips, and the privacy line. Save as today.
C2 · Set, and how to change it
Red on screen: 0
Answered in A or B: the 90s is filled. Tap another decade to change it, or "Clear" to take it off. Clearing drops the streak back to today's rule at the next read.

How to change it later

  • Edit profile (web ProfileMenu "edit" view, iPhone EditProfile) is the one place, on every platform.
  • B2's line and B3's foot both say "Change it in Edit profile". There is no change button on the profile itself.
  • Clear = never answered. No "prefer not to say" value to store.

Privacy, in one line on every card

  • "Private. Never shown on your profile." Stored on the account as a private field (a decade, never a birth year), out of every public profile, share card, Blend and API response.
  • Not sent to the AI writer. The header word "Nostalgic" is public; the decade behind it is not.

Same shape as the proposed rule in theorist-dry-run.md, with your window in place of the fixed 1980 to 2010: you rate films from your years at least 0.30★ further above Letterboxd's average than films from after them (weighted as Film DNA weights them), with 12 rated and 5 loved (4.5★+) in your years; or a quarter of your loved films from your years are rewatches (only where a diary exists). Read against the crowd, so "old films that lasted are good" does not count.

Without an answer the fixed 1980 to 2010 rule stays as it is (2 of 21 today), so nobody loses anything by skipping.

What the profile shows

The word, never the decade

  • Header, the dry run's form: "Nihilist, with a streak of Hardboiled, and a Nostalgic streak". Alone: "Nihilist, with a Nostalgic streak".
  • Always last, at most three streak names, no mix share and no defining film (it is not a kind of film).
  • Own view of the streak card: your years and your films above the crowd (B3). Anyone else: the directory text only (B4).
  • Group names can use it ("Throwback Crowd") as drafted; never with a decade.

What you need to decide

  1. Where to ask. A (a fifth Quick one card in the deck), B (a card on the Taste tab once it can matter), or C alone (Edit profile, passive). C ships with either A or B as the way to change it.Recommend B + C.
  2. When B shows. At 30 rated and 12+ films from before 2015, once, until answered or dismissed. Lower to 15 (when the archetype first appears) asks more people but often before the read has enough older films.Recommend 30 and 12.
  3. The headline. Neutral "When did you grow up watching movies?" for everyone, or a guess from their ratings, "Grew up on the 90s?", with that chip outlined. The guess is warmer but guessing someone's age can land wrong.Recommend neutral; keep "Tell us and we'll spot your nostalgia picks." as the line under it.
  4. The chips. The five (70s to 2010s), or add "Earlier" for older members. The 70s window overlaps Old Soul's pre-1980 films; both can hold at once, as streaks already can.Recommend the five; revisit if older members ask.
  5. Without an answer. Keep today's fixed 1980 to 2010 rule as the fallback, or show Nostalgic only to people who answered.Recommend keeping the fallback.
  6. The header form. "…, and a Nostalgic streak" (last, no share), or fold it into the list, "with streaks of Hardboiled and Nostalgic".Recommend "and a Nostalgic streak", as in the dry run.
  7. Privacy. A decade, not a birth year; private; never on the profile, share cards, Blends, the API or the AI writer; visitors see the word only.Recommend exactly that.
Recommendation

B, with C for changes. Not in onboarding.

Agree with your lean. No extra onboarding step: the deck stays as fast as the audit made it, and nobody meets an age-shaped question before seeing anything back. Asked once, when it can matter: at 30 rated with enough older films, one tap can put "and a Nostalgic streak" in the header the same minute, so the question explains itself.

  • B: one card on your own Taste tab (and the same card in Sharpen's Next), shown once, ✕ hides it for good.
  • C: "Grew up watching movies in" in Edit profile, to change or clear.
  • The read: the dry run's Nostalgic rule with the answer's window; the fixed 1980 to 2010 rule when there is no answer.
  • A stays on the shelf: worth trying only if, after launch, few people reach 30.
