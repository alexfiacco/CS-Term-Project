Where the facts come from

  • Films: logs with a watch date in the year (in-app watched dates and the Letterboxd diary feed). Under 10, dated Letterboxd imports for that year are added. Rewatches count, one per day per film.
  • Hours: runtimes summed; left off when under 60% of the films have one.
  • Archetype shift: January's taste snapshot against now. With no January snapshot, the card says only who you are.
  • Boldest take: your rating furthest from the crowd's average, by the hot-take rule (a star or more).
  • Best call / worst miss: only guesses shown before you rated (match_predictions, rating_predictions "shown"), 5 or more this year. Held-out guesses never count.
  • Closest Blend: your joined pair Blend with the highest number this year. Movie nights: rooms you were at that landed on a film, and the group name from everyone who came.

Never empty, never embarrassing

  • No director with 2+ films and no decade with 3+: card 4 is skipped.
  • No hot take, no honest guesses, no Blend, no movie nights: those cards are skipped.
  • Under 10 films after the import fallback: the short version (numbers, top films, archetype). Under 3 films: no year card at all.
  • Top films fall back to "Your films of 2026" when fewer than 3 are rated.
  • No archetype yet (under 15 rated): the archetype card is skipped.
  • The words never say "taste match".
