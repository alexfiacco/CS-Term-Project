Motion, proposed

Eight short recordings of proposed motion, played on the real Montage components (the Pitch shortlist, the rating sheet, PosterWall and RevealBeats, the film page, the Picks feed, the Blend hero, the Social tab with PullToRefresh) in a harness with mocked data. The motion is a layer on top: nothing here is in the app yet. Cinematic and restrained: one ease-out curve, nothing over about 600ms except the shuffle and the reveal, and every clip says what it does under Reduce Motion.

  • Ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)
  • ≤ ~600ms per move
  • No bounce, no overshoot past 6%
  • One red accent, one cream primary
  • Bodoni for display
  • 390×844 @2x
1 · Pitch shortlist / Tonight

Choose one from these

The 7 shortlist posters deal into a fan, riffle once, then a light passes over them, slowing, and stops on one. It lifts forward with its match % and title.

Duration
About 1.8s: deal 0.66s (7 cards, 360ms each, 50ms apart), riffle 0.46s, light 9 steps from 55ms to 230ms, lift 520ms, title 380ms.
Easing
cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1); the gather into one stack uses (.4,0,.2,1) so it reads as a single movement.
Haptic
iPhone: a selection tick on each light step (they space out with it), one success notification on the landing. Web: none.
Reduced
No deal, riffle or lift. The fan fades in (200ms), then the chosen poster, its % and title crossfade in place (250ms). One success haptic.

2 · Your rating sheet

Rating a film

A tap on 4.5★ fills the stars left to right. At 4.5★ and up, one quiet glint crosses them. Then "We said 88%. Called it." slides up underneath.

Duration
Fill 55ms per star (each 140ms colour, 260ms settle to 1.0 from .86); glint 600ms, once; line 420ms, rising 12px, once the save answers.
Easing
cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) throughout; the glint's position is ease-out cubic.
Haptic
A light impact per star as the fill passes it (5 taps, 55ms apart), then a success notification with "Called it."
Reduced
Stars fill at once, no settle and no glint; the line fades in (200ms). One light impact.
Launch
3 · Onboarding

The reveal

"Hardboiled" sets letter by letter like title-card type, over a slow push-in on the real tilted PosterWall (tiles at 37% brightness). Replaces today's blur-and-pulse name beat and the ring.

Duration
About 2.5s to settled: "You're" 420ms, letters 460ms each, 85ms apart (1.3s for ten letters), red tick 380ms, streak line 480ms. The push-in runs 3.6s, scale 1 to 1.075.
Easing
Letters and lines cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1); the push-in a gentle ease (.25,.1,.25,1) so it never visibly stops.
Haptic
One soft impact as the last letter lands. Nothing else.
Reduced
The wall holds still (the drift already stops). The name fades in whole (400ms), then its line (300ms). Same soft impact.
Launch

7 · Picks card

Saving to your watchlist

Tap Watchlist: the poster thumbnail shrinks and arcs down to the Profile tab (the Watchlist lives there), whose icon gives one small pulse. The feed then moves on as it does today.

Duration
450ms flight, scale 1 to .16; the tab pulse 300ms to 1.16 and back.
Easing
The arc is two curves: across with cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1), down with (.5,0,.75,.5), so it lifts a little then drops.
Haptic
A light impact on the tap, a soft one as it lands.
Reduced
No flight: the button fills and the tab icon fades to 60% and back (200ms).
Later
8 · Any tab

Pull to refresh

The real PullToRefresh, with a small film-sprocket reel in place of the strip. It turns with your finger while you pull, then spins evenly while refreshing.

Duration
Follows the finger (2.4° per pixel); while refreshing, 1 turn a second, shown for at least 600ms (today's minimum).
Easing
Linear spin; the indicator's height follows the pull with today's resistance.
Haptic
One light impact when the pull crosses "Release to refresh".
Reduced
The reel holds still; the label carries the state, as the strip does today.
Later
