Blend · the decision, the model, the cost

Build the kept Blend on Groups, not as a new object

The honest question: groups already have members, invites, a thread, a shared watchlist, movie nights, mute switches and blocks. Should a kept Blend be (a) a new object, or (b) a group with its Blend list turned on? The repo already answers half of it: friend chats are groups of kind = 'dm' with exactly two members (lib/dm.ts, rule 567), with one id per pair (dmIdFor). A kept pair Blend can live on that same row.

Every path ends on one page per pair: the Blend page (the old match page), with the list on top once it's kept. No new tab, no new Groups rows, nothing for people who never keep one.

"Something real happened": the triggers

  • Either person rates. No hook on the rating path: the job reads user_library_events (the ledger setUserRating already writes) since the Blend's built_at. That count is the "Jo rated 3 films" line.
  • A title lands on a service one of you has. The release-reminders job (lib/release-reminders.ts) already dates each first sighting per provider. Widen its film set from watchlists to the slugs in kept Blends' shortlists; a new sighting on a member's service marks the Blend dirty.
  • A new release fits you both. Films added to the candidate pool since built_at with a release date in the last 60 days. They're scored for the Blend only on its next rebuild.
  • Not a trigger: the calendar. A Blend with none of the three is not rebuilt, so the job's cost scales with activity, not with the number of Blends.

Data model

blends                       -- one per group (pair = the dm group)
  group_id      text pk → groups(id) on delete cascade
  status        'asked' | 'kept' | 'waiting' | 'lapsed'  -- waiting: someone under 15
  asked_by      uuid, asked_at timestamptz     -- lapses at 14 days
  kept_at       timestamptz
  built_at      timestamptz                    -- last rebuild
  dirty_at      timestamptz null               -- set by the triggers
  dirty_why     jsonb   -- {rated:{jo:3}, service:[slug], release:[slug]}
  picks         jsonb   -- [{slug, scores:{id:n}, why, added_at, on:{svc,who}}] ≤ 12
  last_friday   timestamptz                    -- what "new since Friday" counts from

blend_members              -- each person's own switches
  group_id, profile_id   pk
  muted       boolean default false
  seen_at     timestamptz      -- clears "3 new" for this person

group_members (exists)     activity_feed (exists): blend_invite, blend_kept
push_log (exists)          theaters.group_id (exists)

The cap of 4 is a count over blend_members joined to blends where status ≠ lapsed, checked in POST /api/blends.

The jobs

  • Nightly rebuild (/api/cron/blends, after release-reminders at 16:10 UTC): for each kept Blend with dirty_at set, run buildMovieNight for its members with seedOnly-style AI off (no group-name call, no describePicks), keep 12, write the template why lines (lib/blend-why.ts), stamp added_at on films that weren't there before. Bounded per run like RELEASE_LIMITS; the rest wait a day.
  • Friday push (/api/cron/blends-friday, hourly on Fridays only): for people whose local hour is 17, collect their unmuted kept Blends with picks added since last_friday. One push (blend_new, capped) naming one Blend, or bundling several. Then stamp last_friday. Nothing new means no push and no row.
  • The nightly job makes no model calls: the hasClaude() paths are skipped by a flag on the call.

Risks, honestly

  • The rename touches about 60 strings and every share card. "Blend" for people and "match" for films sit side by side on many screens, and a few lines mix them (see the report's table). The film-match rename must never leak.
  • Building on dm groups means loosening isDm refusals for Blend reads. A mistake there could open watchlist or movie night routes to dm rows. Add an invariant check.
  • Freshness depends on activity. Two people who stop rating get a list that never changes, and so never a push. That's by design, but the feature can look dead. The "Rate a film or two" line is the only nudge.
  • Group rebuilds are the cost. A 7-person Blend scores each candidate 7 times. Rebuilds are capped per run and skipped when nothing's dirty.
  • Templated why lines will repeat. Five templates × 3–4 phrasings is enough for launch, not for a year.
  • The Friday push shares the 3-a-day cap. It can be crowded out.
