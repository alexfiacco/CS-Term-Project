When a kind repeats, and where to undo it

The nudge is the only follow-up: no full drawer after the first time. Undo lives in three places: the toast, the film's why drawer (tap the %), and Settings, which gets a Not my thing row on iPhone for the first time.

The rule: ask once, then only when it repeats

So the drawer never becomes a tax on Not for me. Counts live on the account (as montage_hide_asked does today), so web and iPhone share them.

When it shows

  1. First Not for me ever: the drawer. It replaces today's "Stop suggesting…?" ask, so first-time users see one sheet, not two.
  2. After that, no drawer. Only the nudge, when a 2nd film of the same kind is passed within 60 days.
  3. At most one nudge a session, and only for a kind that is not already off.
  4. Not now on a kind: never asked about that kind again. Three Not nows: no more nudges at all.
  5. Never in a movie night, and never for a kind this search asked for (Tonight set to Western).
  6. A film with no chips (a 2023 drama under 2.5 hours) skips the drawer: just today's toast.

What the chips can and can't be

Kinds (NOT_MINE): Animation, Horror, Musicals, Documentaries, Westerns, Black-and-white, Silent films, Very long films. Up to three per film, in that order.

Era is not a NOT_MINE kind. "Before 1970" sets the existing Oldest films to recommend (noOlder + since), the first decade after the film's year. It is a cut-off, so it is offered last.

Language is left out. Settings' Languages is a list of what you want, not what you don't: "No Italian" can't be said without a new field. Same for director and runtime bands under 2.5 hours.

Genres beyond the eight (Western is in; Crime, War, Sci-fi are not) would need new NOT_MINE entries. Something else… opens the full list.
