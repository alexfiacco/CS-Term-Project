Drawers v1: a little character in every drawer

The owner: "These drawers could have a red accent maybe, and look through all the drawers where it looks a little plain and see where we can add some character." Montage has about 60 bottom drawers on the iPhone (.drawer(, Drawer.swift's edge-to-edge panel; web BottomDrawer), and most of them open on the same plain head: DrawerHeader, a Bodoni title, then a grey line. This sets one small kit for all of them, settles where the red dash goes, and redraws the eight plainest at 390pt, Today beside With character. Part a: the kit, the rule and drawers 1 to 4. Part b: drawers 5 to 8 and the full inventory, with the ones to leave alone.
Sources (on every frame): owner's phone = the owner's own screenshot (real); drawn = HTML from the Swift on origin/main (6fac58d3), Theme.swift values, posters from the mocks' set (approximate).

The rule: every content drawer has one kicker line under its title (where you are, then a count or a place: "Film DNA · 1 of 9 strands", "Weekly streak · your best is 9", "Your watches · 3, newest first") and the red dash starts it. Nowhere else in the drawer. A drawer about one film leads with the film: the film's title is the drawer's title and the drawer's job is the kicker (Discussion already does this: "Incendies / Discussion"; Our read does it with its own dash). Confirms, reports and the system share sheet stay plain: no kicker, no dash.

How it lands

  • One change does most of it. DrawerHeader (Controls.swift) is used by 28 drawers. Its sub becomes a kicker drawn as HStack(spacing: 8) { RedDash(); Text(kicker) }, one line. Web: BottomDrawer's sub gets the .rd class it already has.
  • Long subs move down. Six DrawerHeaders use the sub as a sentence ("Everyone sees it, guests too. It goes on the share card."). That sentence becomes the first body line; the kicker becomes two to four words.
  • Hand-made heads join in. Streak, Where to watch, Rating and the Discussion header build their own; each takes the same kicker line.
  • Bodoni roman, not italic: the strand's definition (Typo.cardItalic today) and any italic line left in a drawer.
  • Lines out: Filters' row rules and footer rule, Where to watch's rule above JustWatch.
  • Left alone: Tonight, Pitch, Our read, the archetype explainer, the share drawers, the rating drawer's ticket. They already carry the brand (part b, inventory).
