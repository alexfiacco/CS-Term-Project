Who gets Founder
Only his account. A server-set flag nobody can earn or edit. For him it replaces Founding member, it never stacks: one badge beside the name, ever.

1. The Founder badge

Same slot as Founding member: beside the name, top of the header. Each option shows his own profile and the page as a visitor sees it (handle in the top bar, Follow). The archetype's oxblood bar is the screen's one red, so none of these may add another.

2. A 150-character bio, with a counter near the end

150 is Instagram's bio limit (X allows 160): two or three lines on a phone, enough for a sentence with some personality, short enough that every header stays the same shape. No counter while there is room; it appears at 120 as "128/150" in muted, turns ink at 140, and at 150 the field stops taking letters. Pasting something longer keeps the first 150 and says so in a small note. On the profile the bio shows at most 4 lines, then "More" (only line breaks can get it there at 150).

3. Your header with three bios

His own profile with the recommended badge (a), each bio at its real length on a 390pt screen. All three are well under 150.

What would change when built (not in this mock)
  • Server: lib/public-profile.ts slice(0, 280) becomes 150, counted by characters as people see them (an emoji counts as 1; Swift's count already does, the web should count with Intl.Segmenter rather than .length), so web, iPhone and server agree.
  • Existing bios over 150: none expected, but run a read-only count first (select count(*) from profiles where char_length(bio) > 150). Any found are left as they are and only shortened if their owner edits them; the 4-line clamp covers them on the profile.
  • Web: ProfileMenu.tsx bio textarea maxLength 280 to 150, plus the counter and the paste note. iPhone: AccountScreens.swift prefix(280) to 150, same counter under the field. Profile bio: lineLimit(4) + More on iPhone (ProfileScreen.swift), a 4-line clamp on .phe .ph-bio on the web.
  • Founder: a server-set flag on his profile only (a column set by hand, or his profile id in config), returned beside founding; the badge component shows Founder when it is set and Founding member otherwise. Same treatment wherever Founding member appears (header, Films together row, invite sheet).

What you need to decide

  1. Which Founder badge: (a) the outlined "FOUNDER" pill, recommended; (b) the cream "Founder" pill; or (c) the fan mark + "Founder".
  2. The bio limit: 150 (Instagram), recommended, or 160 (X).
  3. When the counter shows: from 120 (muted), ink from 140, as mocked; or always visible.
  4. The profile clamp: 4 lines then "More", or no clamp (at 150 characters a bio only reaches 4 lines with line breaks).
  5. Your bio: one of the three drafts in section 3, or your own.
  6. OK to run the read-only count of bios over 150 before the cap changes.
