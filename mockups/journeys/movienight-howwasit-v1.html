Movie Night: How was it?

After the room picks a film, everyone who was there rates it afterwards: the host, members and guests with no account. Once two people have rated, the night gets a result: "The room gave it 4.1★", who loved it most and least (first names, as the room shows them), how split the room was, and whether our prediction for the room held: "We predicted 86% for the room · Called it ✓", the same verdict as How we did. It ends on the shipped share card with the room's rating on it. Web and iPhone the same.

Where each line comes from

  • The room gave it 4.1★: the mean of every seat's stars, one decimal ("4★" when whole), from 2+ ratings. Before that: "You gave it 4.5★ · 1 of 5 rated".
  • Loved it most / least: the top and bottom stars, by first name as the room shows them ("Jo", "Gina"); shown when they differ. Ties name two, then "and 2 more".
  • Split on it: 2★ or more between the top and the bottom. Within 1★: "Everyone agreed". Between: no line.
  • We predicted 86% for the room: the film's own room % from the night (the ticket's stamp). The verdict is How we did's (verdictFor at the mean rounded to a half star, the standard star table, CALLED_WITHIN): "Called it ✓", or "The room liked it more / less than we thought".

Reaching people

  • Guests: after the pick the room is remembered on the device (localStorage on the web, the iPhone's own storage) for 14 days; back on the link, the room asks "How was <Film>?".
  • Members: the next-day Activity item and its push (existing, no new permission) open the room's stars; the rating also goes through the normal rating path to their films, dated the night.
  • Host: "Ask the room how it was" shares the room's own link (the invite everyone already has) with "How was The Wicker Man? Rate it for the room". Never a new link, never the card's /mn.

Rules, storage and the switch

  • One rating per seat per room, changeable for 14 days after the night. Guests' stars live on the room only. Writes are rate limited per caller, and only a seat that was there can rate.
  • Card: initials only, as before. The room's link is never on the card: it still says montagefilm.app/mn.
  • Tracked: mn_rate_prompt {where}, mn_night_rated {role, rating, changed}, mn_rate_ask {how}, mn_result_seen {n}.
  • Ships ON with a kill switch (MN_HOW_WAS_IT=off hides it all, web and iPhone). Never "taste match".
