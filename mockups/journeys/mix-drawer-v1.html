How you got Nihilist, without the read

Today the drawer opens with the whole read, five lines, before the line that says what the drawer is. Proposed: the drawer starts at its explanation, and the whole read lives where its first sentence already is, on the Taste tab, opened in place by Read more.

Now · iPhone
The whole read first (ArchetypeWhySheet read), then the explanation line, the bar and the three rows. Read more › and Your mix › both open it.
Proposed · the drawer
The read goes. Title, sub and tick, then the explanation line 16 under, the bar, the rows, 16 apart as today. Your mix › opens it; Read more › no longer does.
Proposed · the read's home
Read more › opens the read in place on the Taste tab, ending in Less; the figures move down under it. A second tap, or Less, folds it back to the first sentence.

What Read more opens today

  • Web: the Taste tab's "Read more ›" (.tt-sum-more in Result.tsx ProfileShortTop) bumps mixKey, which opens the How you got <archetype> bottom drawer (SectionInfo openKey), the same one "Your mix ›" opens. Its first child is the whole read, <p class="tt-mix-read">, then the explanation line (.info-lede), then ArchetypeWhy.
  • iPhone: a tap on the read, when it has more than its first sentence, sets whyOpen (ProfileScreen.swift), the same drawer as "Your mix ›": ArchetypeWhySheet with read: the whole read, drawn first.
  • So the drawer is the read's only full home. Take the read out of the drawer and Read more must open something else, or the rest of the read is gone.

Recommendation

  • Drawer: drop the read (web: the tt-mix-read line in SectionInfo's children; iPhone: stop passing read:). The drawer opens on "We sorted your favorites …", which is what it is for. On someone else's profile the same.
  • Read more expands in place on the Taste tab: the whole read, Newsreader 15 as the first sentence is now, " Less" in the UI meta face at its end, the figures 16 under it as today. Nothing new to design: the iPhone already has this More / Less fold for reads without a mix (ProfileScreen.swift open), and the web its ClampedBlurb.
  • The read stays one tap from the profile, and the drawer stays one job: how the archetype was read. Your mix › stays its only door.
  • Stand-ins: the streak line on the tab, the mix shares, and the film in Nihilist's row ("Se7en is one of yours." in the shape the app writes it); the read, the explanation, the tags and "↑ Much more than most people" are the owner's.
