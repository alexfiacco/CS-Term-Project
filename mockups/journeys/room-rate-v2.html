1 · The room, after the night

Sealed until you rate, with the film page's stars

Same layout as v1's C: the ticket carries the title once, "How was it?" sits under it, your row has the stars, the others stay sealed until yours is in. Only the stars change: grey and filled, the same row the film page shows under "Your rating", so rating here and rating on the film page feel like one control (and the rating is the same one: a member's stars go to their films too).

2 · The next morning

One push, then the room's stars

One notification the morning after a locked night, to the seats that have not rated. The seal is the hook: when others have rated, the push says who, and that theirs open when yours is in. A tap lands on the room with "How was it?" at the top.

Copy 1 · recommended
How was A Bronx Tale? / Emma and Sam rated it. Rate yours to see theirs.
The seal in one line. No one rated yet: "Be the first in the room to rate it." Three or more: "Emma, Sam and 2 more rated it."
Copy 2
Emma and Sam rated A Bronx Tale / Theirs stay sealed until you rate yours.
Leads with people, so it reads like a friend's news; longer, and it needs a different title when no one has rated.
Copy 3
How was last night? / A Bronx Tale with Emma, Sam and Theo. Rate it for the room.
Warmest, about the night, not the score; drops the seal, so it pulls least.
Where a tap lands
The room (/t/<room>), scrolled so "How was it?" and your row are at the top and the ticket's foot shows above. The stars are live at once; nothing else to tap first.
Push off, or no app: the Activity bell
The same moment as an Activity row (it is written there first in any case; the push is only the buzz). The poster, "How was A Bronx Tale?", "Rate it to see the room's · Emma and Sam rated", and a Rate it button that lands where the push does. Once you rate, the row turns to "You gave it 4.5★" and the button goes.

The send rule

  1. When: 10:00 in the person's own time zone, the morning after the host locked the night in (the first 10:00 at least 8 hours after the lock-in, so a 1am lock-in waits until the next day's 10:00).
  2. Who: each seat that was there (seatArrived) and has not rated this room, and has not logged the film elsewhere since. Never someone who already rated. The host too.
  3. How often: one per night, ever. The room is marked followed up before anything is sent, so a retry never doubles it. No second reminder.
  4. Settings: only with the Movie nights switch on; it counts toward the daily 3 for capped pushes and keeps quiet hours (10pm to 8am), as rule 581 has it for "How was it?" today.
  5. Without push: the Activity row (signed-in members). A link guest with no account gets nothing pushed; the room is kept on their device and asks when they come back to the link.
  6. Copy picks itself: with 0 others rated, the "be the first" body; with 1 or 2, their names; with 3 or more, two names and "N more". First names as the room shows them.
What the code does today: the server sends one "How was A Bronx Tale? / Your movie night picked it. Rate it for the room." from a cron at 16:30 UTC (11:30 in New York, 8:30 in Los Angeles) for rooms locked 12 to 36 hours ago, and the iPhone also sets its own 10:00 local reminder for the room (RateLater.remind). A signed-in member with the app can get both. Moving to one send at 10:00 local means the server picks the hour per person, and the phone's own reminder stays only for someone not signed in.
3 · What you need to decide

Five calls, with my recommendation

1 · Push copy

Which words go out?

Copy 1: "Emma and Sam rated it. Rate yours to see theirs."Copy 2: names first, "Theirs stay sealed…"Copy 3: "How was last night?"

Copy 1 asks the question and gives the reason in one line, and falls back cleanly when no one has rated.

2 · Send time

When in the morning?

10:00 local9:00 localEvening, around 18:00

10:00 is after the commute and the first messages, while the night is still fresh. 9:00 lands in the busiest hour; an evening send competes with that night's plans and is a day colder.

3 · Guests

Does the seal apply to guests with no account?

Yes, the same seal for every seatNo, guests see everyone's at once

One rule is simpler to explain and to build, and a guest is the one most likely to skip rating if they can just look. Their stars stay on the room only, as now.

4 · The prediction line

Show "We predicted 86% · Called it ✓"?

Yes, once everyone is in, after the sealYes, from two ratings (as built)No

It is the one line that says the app got the room right, but before everyone is in it can flip. One quiet line under "The room gave it 4.4★", only when it is final.

5 · A second reminder

Remind again after 2 days if still unrated?

No, one push per nightYes, once more on day 2

A second nudge about a film you have already half forgotten reads as nagging, and the row stays in Activity and the room keeps asking on the link for 14 days.
