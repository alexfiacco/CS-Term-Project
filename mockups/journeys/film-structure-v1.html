Film page structure: where everything lives

The owner asked: "Where do themes, cast and crew come in? And are we just putting Watch together there?" Left, today's iPhone film page in its real order (read from FilmScreen.swift on main). Then three full-page orders, each as the whole 390pt scroll (shown at 60%) with its outline beside it. The six things asked about are blue in every outline: Themes, Cast, Crew, Details, More like this, Watch together. The rating block is v7 throughout.

My recommendation: C, "One question per section"

C keeps today's top half exactly as the owner already approved it (rating and Prediction card, then Friends, then Where to watch), so the app changes are all below the fold, and the page has one order for every film, seen or not, on iPhone and web alike. It answers both questions directly. Watch together is not a section: it is a person ("Watch with @hugo, wants to see it, 78% Blend") and a Movie night button, so it belongs inside Friends, next to the faces it is about, instead of a header button that has to guess who to ask (A) or a lone card near the bottom (today). Takes are friends' words, so the best one is quoted in the same card with "All 8 takes"; that removes two sections without losing anything. Themes, Cast, Crew and a new Details tab are reference you dip into after deciding, so they share one tab block under Where to watch, with the theme chips (lanes) staying in the header as the quick "what kind of film is this". B is clever but the page reorders itself the moment you rate a film, which is disorienting right after the tap, and it doubles the layouts to keep in step. A is close, but a header Watch together button would sit on every film, even ones nobody you know wants to see.

What changes in the app for C: Watch together and Discussion stop being sections; the Friends card gains a "Watch with" row (the same person and Movie night button as today) and the top take (the same quote and "All N takes" as today's Discussion card; not seen yet, the count with "hidden until you've seen it"). Themes · Cast · Crew gains a Details tab (release date, runtime, language, country, studio). The series row sits just above More like this. The Friends section shows whenever any of its three parts has something.
