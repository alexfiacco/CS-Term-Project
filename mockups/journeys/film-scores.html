Recommendation: build the Details tab with the Parental guide (S + M); hold the critic scores. The guide reuses labels we already pay for and answers a real "can the kids watch this?" question, as long as it says plainly it is our estimate. Critic scores need a new data source and a licence call (IMDb's free file is non-commercial; RT has no API), and they pull the eye away from our own number. If the owner wants one, IMDb alone via OMDb is the cheap, low-risk version.
