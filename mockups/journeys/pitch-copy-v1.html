Pitch copy v1: Set the scene, Action!, Your cut

The owner set Pitch's words: the feature stays Pitch (the Browse button), the page is Set the scene, the button Action!, the wait Rolling… with a rotating film line, the results Your cut, and a shared pitch reads "Sam sent you a pitch" or "Sam sent you their cut". Here is the whole flow in those words, on iPhone and the web, with a few Easter eggs where they are free (Previous takes, the writers' room, Take two, the reel, the can). Every control still says what it does: the eggs sit on headings, captions and empty or error lines, never on a button a first-timer has to guess (Undo, Cancel, Details, Share stay plain). One red per screen, as today.

      Recommended set

      • Ask: no kicker, Set the scene (red dash under it), the rotating placeholder, Action!, "2 free pitches left this month", Previous takes, From the writers' room.
      • Wait: Rolling… over a rotating film line; the read chips still land under it (they are what makes the wait feel quick). Button reads Rolling… while busy.
      • Results: caps "Your pitch", Take two (was Start over), Your cut (was Sorted for you; "Cut for you and Sam" for two), Final cut: pick one, a tiny TAKE 2 slate on the pinned bar after an edit, New scene at the end.
      • Lines: empty "Nothing in the can. Remove a chip to widen it."; error "We lost the reel. Try again."; toast "Cut Scary" with a plain Undo.

      A pitch or their cut: the rule

      • "Sam sent you a pitch" when Sam shares it as Montage first read it: the sentence is the thing being sent.
      • "Sam sent you their cut" when Sam changed it before sharing: removed or added a chip, steered it, or did a Take two. The list really is their cut.
      • Decided once at send time and stored on the card, so the card, the push, the group line ("Sam sent their cut · The Witch (2015)") and the walkthrough all agree. Random alternation was the other option; it would read as a bug when two cards differ for no reason.

      Small choices

      • Action! over "Roll" (reads as scroll) and "Roll camera" (two words, wraps on the web's narrow pill). Busy state "Rolling…" keeps the joke going.
      • Final cut: pick one over "Just pick one for me": the film term first, the plain verb after it, so it still says what it does.
      • Kept plain on purpose: Understood as, Steer it, Undo, Cancel, Details, Share, Start a movie night with these, "Pitch it for me" on the card (the feature's name, and the card's one action).
      • "Share your cut" heads the share drawer. The walkthrough card "Pitch a film" is new: Set the scene, call Action!, get your cut.
