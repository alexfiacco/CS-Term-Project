Who rated it: the people line opens the people

A list row says "3 people you follow · 4.5★", and on the iPhone and the web that line is already its own button, but a tap just opens the film. Proposed: the line becomes a 44 target that opens one drawer, People you follow on the film, each person with their stars, their take if they wrote one, and when they watched it. The film page's People you follow card opens the same drawer instead of unfolding in place, so there is one list of your people for a film, reached from anywhere their rating shows.

The card stays as approved; its top line and "+2 all" open the drawer above instead of unfolding. Seen without stars sits last with an eye; a spoiler take is never quoted.

API changes needed

  • One set of people. The row says "people you follow" but counts only mutual friends; the film page lists everyone you follow. Pick one so the drawer's count matches the line that opened it. I'd move ratingLines to everyone you follow (as rule 1097 already did for the film page) and keep the row's words; otherwise the row should say "friends".
  • film_follow_view: add watched_on date (coalesce(ur.watched_on, ur.updated_at::date) for in-app, library_json rated.w for imports, null otherwise) and the person's newest visible take: take_id, take_body, take_spoiler, only when the take's audience lets this viewer see it (friends-only takes need mutual). One new migration, the same exclusions.
  • /api/film-people: pass those through on saw[], and add ?order=rating (stars desc, then watched_on desc, seen-without-stars last) for the drawer; the card keeps its Blend order for its five faces. Both clients already load this endpoint for the film page (ScreenLoads.swift:53), so the drawer from a row is one more call of it, not a new route.
  • Privacy holds as today: ratings_hidden people and anyone blocked either way are never counted or listed, on the line or in the drawer; nothing says "2 hidden". A take quoted in the drawer follows its own audience, and a spoiler take shows "Take with spoilers" only.

Recommendation

  • Yes, ship it, as one drawer. The line already looks and presses like a button and then does the same thing as the row, which reads as a miss. Opening the people answers the question the line raises: who, and why that many stars.
  • Use the same drawer from the film page and drop the in-place unfold: one list, one sort, one place takes and dates live. The card's faces, average and "Most split" stay as approved.
  • Picks' "Your people 4.2★ · 3" opens it too, as a 44 target, for the same reason.
  • Sort by stars, then latest watch (the owner's words), not Blend %: the drawer is "who rated it how", and the Blend % reads as a second number to decode. The faces on the card can keep their Blend order.
  • The row's tap still opens the film everywhere else; only the people line moves. VoiceOver label: "3 people you follow rated it, average 4.5 stars. Show them."
