Save a pitch: Saved cuts

A cut worth keeping can now be saved from Your cut and found again on Set the scene. Two ways to offer Save on the results page: A, a bookmark circle beside share, or B, a "Save" text button in the header; both turn to the chosen style (the logo's blue at 22%, a 1.5px outline, cream) once saved, with the toast "Saved to your cuts" · Undo. On the ask, Saved cuts sits above Previous takes: up to five rows (the sentence, the top film's tiny poster, the date), "See all" when there are more, a swipe or × to unsave; then Previous takes (the last three) and From the writers' room. iPhone and the web. One red per screen: the sentence's bar on the results, the dash under "Pitch" on the ask. Drawn from Recut.tsx and Recut.swift on main, in Pitch copy v1's words (not landed on main yet).

On the web

Same control, same list

  • Option A's circle sits between Take two and share, as on the iPhone; the pinned bar has it too.
  • Saved cuts rows show their × always, like Previous takes; Undo in the toast puts a row back where it was.
  • "See all" opens the full list as its own view inside Pitch, back to the ask.

Recommended: A, the bookmark circle

  • It pairs with share. Both are actions on the whole cut, both 36pt circles at the header's right, and the same pair fits the pinned bar (32pt) without squeezing the sentence.
  • The saved state reads at a glance: the circle fills in the chosen blue with a cream bookmark, the app's one "on" look.
  • The toast at the foot on this page (Pitch copy v1's "Cut Scary" place), not the top, so it never covers the control just tapped; one exception to Toast.swift's full-screen rule.
  • The words say where it went: the toast reads "Saved to your cuts", and the circle's label is "Save this cut" (VoiceOver "Saved. Remove from your cuts" once on), so the bookmark is not read as Watchlist.
  • The bookmark does mean Watchlist elsewhere (the film sheet, chat cards, the toast's own glyph). On this page no film row carries one, so there is one bookmark on screen and it is the cut's.

Why not B, the "Save" text button

  • Two text buttons side by side, "Take two" and "Save", read as one phrase at a glance; B needs a pill to keep them apart.
  • On the pinned bar "Saved" takes 70pt, so the sentence cuts to "Like Arrival but sc…" (B3). A would keep the bar as main draws it plus one 32pt circle.
  • B is still the plainer word: if the owner wants no new glyph on this page, it works, with the pinned bar dropping Save and keeping share.

How Saved cuts behaves

  • What is saved: the cut as it stands (the sentence, its chips, any steer or Take two), the top film's poster and the day. Reopening is free and re-ranks it for you now, as a previous take does.
  • Order: newest saved first; five on the ask, "See all" from the sixth. No saved cuts: the section is not drawn, the ask is as today.
  • No sentence twice: a saved cut leaves Previous takes; unsaved, it is a take again if it is one of the last three.
  • Unsave: × or a swipe on the row, or the filled circle on the results; the toast "Removed from your cuts" · Undo. A cut Sam shared can be saved too; its date line says "from Sam".
