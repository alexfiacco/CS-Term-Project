Brand pass v1: the top three fixes

Before is the iPhone as shipped this week, rebuilt from the owner's screenshots. After is the proposal, built only from the tokens already in globals.css: cream, ink, oxblood, the red and blue tick, Bodoni, Newsreader. Nothing here adds a colour or a font.

1One chosen state, and it is a ticket, not a blue capsule

The filled blue segmented capsule (Unseen · Seen · All, Notifications · You, Just me · Date night, Both · Jacob · You, Post · Story, To Watch · Rewatches) is the most generic control in the app. The same one comes with every dark-mode template. After: the same three options as a stub strip. The chosen stub is cream paper with the ticket's punched corners. That reuses the cream button and the Admit One ticket, so the brand shows up wherever you pick something.

2Settings reads like Montage, not like Popcorn's settings

Montage's Settings (ae9528b8, eaf771be) and Popcorn's (e9f0a5bc, 0256826c) are nearly the same screen: bordered grouped cards, a stock outline icon in front of every row, a chevron, Apple's switch. It is the most template-looking page in the app, and it is the page people open when something is wrong. After: a printed programme. Hairline rows on the black, no boxes and no row icons, the current value on the right, your account at the top, and the Montage switch in place of Apple's.

Rule: a settings page is caps labels and hairline rows on the ground: no panel boxes, no icon in front of a row, the value on the right in muted, danger as text. One switch everywhere: the card's corner and a cream square knob, the track in --select-deep when on. 3Faces: a contact sheet, not pastel letter tiles

Social opens on a row of pastel squares with a dark letter (FaceTint: 8 swatches) and a % stamped across the bottom. Below that, every row repeats a blue "Following" pill. It looks like a default contacts screen. After: each face is a frame on the contact sheet, the initial in Bodoni cream on the panel. The person's tint shrinks to a 3px edge, so one person keeps one colour. The match number moves under the name in Bodoni blue, which is data. The redundant "Following" pill goes, and the row says who the person is (their archetype) in the serif italic.
