One quiet line when something opens, and one place that lists it

The unlock moment copies rule 850's "Your match % is on": a line in a slot the screen already has, shown once per account, then gone. No confetti, no badge. The thing itself does the showing (the % fades in, the dashed radar draws solid). For the "what's next" place, the honest finding is that it already exists: "Sharpen your profile" (rule 84) is a slim row on Profile with a checklist drawer and rating goals at 15, 30, 50, 100 and 150. So the proposal reshapes that drawer rather than adding a hub.
