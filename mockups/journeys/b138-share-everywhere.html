b138 · One share screen for every card
The owner: "if that's what sharing the archetype looks like, then you have to make everything else look like that too." Today Montage has one share drawer (rule 147, ShareSheetFrame: the card in a sheet, then a row) but each surface fills it differently: some have Instagram and TikTok, some only a system "Share" button, some no card at all. Proposed: every share that has a card opens the same full-screen screen: the card edge to edge, × top left, a short pill naming what is shared, and the same bar (Instagram Story, Instagram post, Messages, Copy link, TikTok, More). A private link (a movie night room, a group) keeps only Messages, Copy link and More, and gets a card of its own.

2 · The same screen, six cards
1 · Archetype
As in part 1. Profile cover on page 2.
2 · Blend (new 9:16)
The Blend has only a 4:5 today; a Story from the same parts (both faces, the %, the band, three shared films) lets Instagram Story and TikTok fill the frame. Instagram post sends today's 4:5.
3 · Pitch shortlist
The Pitch card's Story as it is. Send to friends and groups (the chat card) stays: it's a small "Send to a friend" pill on this screen's top right that opens today's Send to drawer.
4 · Movie night result
Everyone who had a seat. Copy link is montagefilm.app/mn, never the room. Replaces today's single Share button, so the result gets Instagram and TikTok in one tap like the rest.
5 · List
The list's existing Story card, which today only Instagram and TikTok use; now it's what you see.
6 · Movie night invite (link only today)
A private link gets a card of its own, so the screen isn't empty, and only the private bar: Messages (with the card), Copy link, More. No Instagram or TikTok on a room link.
The bar's order is fixed everywhere (Instagram Story, Instagram post, Messages, Copy link, TikTok, More); a target leaves only when it can't work (no card: Instagram and TikTok; a private link: both; a card without a link: Copy link). The pill names the thing in sentence case. One component per platform: web ShareScreen replacing ShareSheetFrame's drawer body, iPhone ShareScreen as a fullScreenCover replacing ShareCardSheet and ElsewhereRow's row. Cost: every surface in the table moves at once or the app has two share looks for a while; Send to (friends, groups) stays a drawer on top, one more layer for film and pitch shares.
