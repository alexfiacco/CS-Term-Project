Recommendation: A

  • A, Montage's compact menu. 200 wide (fits "Copy link" with room for "Unblock" and "Link copied"), rows 44 (the 44 rule) with 16 sides, a 16 Lucide icon 12 before sans 15 regular. Order: Copy link, Report, a 1px --line hairline, then Block in --danger last, the way destructive actions sit apart at the end. --panel-2 with the --line-2 keyline, radius 12, no tail, no dim. The only lift is the drawer shadow token's values (black .8, 40 blur, -16 spread; its y offset points down here because the menu drops), never the oxblood offset. Its top sits 8 under the ••• 44 target, its right edge on the 16 gutter. 141 tall against today's ~190.
  • Why not B. The system menu is compact and familiar, but it brings SF and the system red instead of Montage's face and danger, a fixed ~250 width and a blur that varies with what is behind it, and the web can't match it. The app moved off the system Menu for this exact menu on 2026-11-07 (rule 1273); B would undo that and split it from the sort menus.
  • One change, every place. A is a restyle of the shared MenuPopover: on the iPhone the .menuPopover modifier drops the system popover (its tail and big corner) for an overlay anchored to the button; the 268 frame becomes content width with a 200 floor; rows gain an optional icon. That carries to the Films and Library "Sort by" menus too (checked rows and the segment unchanged). The web's MenuPopover.tsx (14 corner, pointer, 268) takes the same, and the profile's ••• on the web, today the older .hero-avatar-menu (a bottom sheet under 700 wide), moves onto it so both platforms match.
