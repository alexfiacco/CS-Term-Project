Brand audit: the eight calls for you

Shipped already (no decision needed): about 80 hand-typed colours on the web and 25 on the iPhone now use the tokens, four undefined web tokens fixed, every iPhone button style presses the same, Delete forever is a pill. These eight are design decisions: two patterns compete, or a screen drifts off the brand. Left: what ships today. Right: my proposal.

1 · Every screen
Caps labels are two greys
The web's eyebrow is --muted (#A9A69E, rule in :root "--eyebrow"). The iPhone's CapsLabel defaults to faint (#8A877F); 44 of 85 labels take the default. Same label, darker on the phone.
Recommend: muted on both (the CSS is the source of truth); faint only for a stat caption.

2 · Every screen
Three side gutters
The phone web's .wrap pads 20px; the iPhone's .page() pads 14 (Theme.gutter); STYLE.md says 16. Content sits 6px further in on the web than in the app.
Recommend: 16 on both (STYLE.md's number, between the two, keeps "no wasted space").

3 · Tickets, main buttons
Two creams
--ink #EFE8DA is type and every main button; --cream #ECE6DA is the web's tickets (Tonight, movie night, Picks stub, poster badges, ~40 rules). The iPhone draws its tickets in --ink. Side by side they're a hair apart, which reads as a mistake.
Recommend: one cream. --cream becomes an alias of --ink (#EFE8DA); the iPhone already matches.

4 · Chat, group thread, movie night room
A second blue in chat
The web's own bubbles use --chat-me #3D6FB6, a darker blue than the app's one blue; the iPhone uses Theme.accent #5B8FC4. "One blue" is broken on the web, and the two apps differ.
Recommend: --accent on both (web --chat-me = var(--accent)); keep their bubble #26262C.

5 · Errors, deletes, caveats
Six pinks for "wrong"
Danger #E8737F (now a token), --err #D0606E, the web's .err #F0A0A0, the toast's #E07A84, the film's "Where it might lose you" #D98A8A, the theater link #E0A9A9. "Red only as an accent": a caveat isn't an error.
Recommend: --danger for deletes and errors (one pink); the caveat label goes muted with the oxblood rule it already has.

6 · Sign in, save your profile
The only square buttons left
Every button is a pill (rule 598), except Continue with Apple / Google: 8px corners on the web, 12 on the iPhone. They're the first buttons a new person sees.
Recommend: pills on both (Apple's guidelines allow a capsule).

7 · Settings and account
Rows that don't push in
The iPhone's account rows (AcctRowPress) only dim to 55% when held; every other row, chip and button pushes in with a soft tap (rules 628, 675, 1007). Settings is the one place that feels flat.
Recommend: the standard push-in on account rows too.

8 · Picks, Made for you, Social, long-press
A brown poster stand-in
Four iPhone places draw a missing poster as a warm brown gradient (#2A211C → #16120F). Everywhere else, and on the web, a poster waits on --panel-2 with the skeleton sheen. Brown is a sixth colour on the black.
Recommend: --panel-2 everywhere (the skeleton while loading, the title on --panel-2 if it never comes).
