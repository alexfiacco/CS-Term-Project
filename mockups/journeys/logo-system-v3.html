Montage brand / logo system v3

The fan, ticketed

The marketing animation ends on a fan of posters. v3 keeps that ending and lets the posters become tickets: films, fanned, picked and ticketed for you. Three directions follow, each with icon sizes, avatar, lockup and a six-frame storyboard of the end of the animation.

Construction rules (all directions)
  • Card 30 × 44 on a 100 grid; fan pivot 24 below the card base, so the three tears sit on one arc.
  • Corner radius = notch radius = 3.5. Tear 15 up from the base (two-thirds of the height down).
  • Fan angles ±16°; cards separated by a 3-unit keyline in the background colour, never overlapped flat. On cream the front stub turns ink.
  • Perforation: five holes, r 0.9, on a 4.5 pitch. Dropped at 32 and below.
  • Tick 16 × 2 (8:1) at −6°, printed only on a cream stub; 4:1 at small sizes.
  • Colours only red #c24c56, cream #efe7d6, blue #5a86bd on #0b0b0d. Flat, no effects.
The monogram, honestly

The M over the tick should stop being a logo. Without the fan or the ticket it says nothing about films or choosing what to watch; it is a letter and a stripe. Keep the tick as the in-product signature (UI accents, rating marks, the rule under headings) and use the wordmark with the tick under it as the text logo for email and press.
