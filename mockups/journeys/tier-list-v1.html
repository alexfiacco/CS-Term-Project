Tiers: Montage tiers it, you argue

Pick a director, an actor, a collection, a lane, a decade, your Canon or any list, and Montage sorts it into five tiers before you touch it: your stars for what you've seen, your match for what you haven't. Drag a film and it keeps a faint note of where Montage had it. That note is the share: "Montage says I have Tenet too low." It lives in two places, a "Tier" action on any page of films and a third view on a list (Grid / Ranked / Tiers), and saves as a list.
