Seen it vs Watchlist: two checks

The viewer on This week, for you and the taste mixes. Top row of each: the film is on your watchlist but not logged. Bottom row: logged. Real app styles at phone width.

Now
A saved Watchlist and an untouched Seen it both show a check. Only the blue box tells them apart.
A · Seen it is an eye (recommended)
Letterboxd's own icon for "watched", so it reads right away. The check stays with Watchlist ("saved"). Once logged, the eye turns blue and says Seen.
B · Seen it is a star
Says "rate it". Once rated, your stars replace it (as now). But "Seen it, no rating" then has a star with no rating.
C · Watchlist is a bookmark
Watchlist becomes an outline bookmark that fills when saved, and Seen it keeps the check. It works too, but this changes the Watchlist icon everywhere else it appears.
