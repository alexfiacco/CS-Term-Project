1 · Films and Watchlist filters on a phone

On a phone every filter has to be 16px (smaller and iOS zooms the page when you tap it), which is why the current row wraps into three uneven lines. Each option below uses the real app styles at phone width.

Now
Wraps into three uneven lines: two filters, one on its own, then sort.
A · Three across, sort below (recommended)
Genre, Rating and Decade share one row, equal widths. Sort gets the full row with the ↓ flip. Short labels: the filter's name until you pick one.
A · with filters set
A set filter shows its value in the blue "on" state, so you can see at a glance what's narrowing the list.
A · Watchlist
Same layout with its two filters.
B · One scrolling row
Everything in one line of pills that scrolls sideways. Compact, but Decade can sit off-screen, and pills inside content break the flat look.
C · Filter button and chips
One Filter button opens a sheet (like Browse). Tidiest, but every filter change is two taps and a sheet.

2 · "Don't remember" in Rate what you've seen

At phone width each tile is about 75px across. "Don't remember" doesn't fit on one line, so its tile is taller than the others. A shorter label fixes it. It does the same thing: that film won't be asked about again.
