A real pager: the content follows the finger, and the blue 2pt underline slides and stretches between Taste and Films in step with it, then settles on the tab. Tapping a tab animates the same way.

Exactly what differs from the app today

  • Tab set: unchanged (Taste · Films · Watchlist · History). No rename was needed: there's no "Lists" tab. A "People" tab doesn't exist, and I haven't added one. If you want it, it would replace the followers/following sheet.
  • Swipe: today a drag past 70pt switches tab on release (DragGesture) and the underline moves with a 0.18s matchedGeometry hop. Proposed: the content pages with the finger and the underline interpolates position and width live.
  • Sharpen row: 66pt → 52pt. One line instead of title + dashes, "2 of 4" text instead of the dash row, ring 40 → 32. Same steps, Rate button, ✕ and drawer.
  • Taste tab content: unchanged. Archetype card, figures, lanes, defining films, Canon, DNA and folds are as today. I found nothing there worth changing for this pass.
