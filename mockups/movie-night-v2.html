Exactly what differs from the app today

  • New: progress rail (option A) above the back row on every step. Today there is none.
  • Film list: the marquee + 84pt stubs become one card per film (64×96 poster). The leader still sorts first. The grid/shelf toggle isn't shown here; "one at a time" (shelf) would get the same chips and tally.
  • Mood chips replace the blue tagLine text. Same data (moods the room asked for that the film delivers), now with counts, and yours in blue. The genre moves to the sub-line.
  • After you vote: the faces and counts already exist today. New: a tally bar per film, a "You voted X · N of M in" line and a "Change vote" link. Before you vote it looks as today.
  • New: Suggest a film. Nothing like this exists in the room code. It needs a server endpoint to add a pick to the room's set.
  • Chat: the docked bar stays as it is (round face now). New: a chat icon with an unread badge beside the title. In the sheet, faces go from radius 8 to round, bubbles get the tighter joined corner, and vote/suggest events become system lines (today only "locked" events show).
  • Unchanged: "Lock in" (host), "What the room asked for" (moved under the list, not shown here for space), the ticket after lock-in, ↻ New picks (host).
