Montage learns your taste in film and finds what you'll love next, for you or your whole group.

PICKS MADE FOR YOU
Open Montage to a deck of films picked for your taste: not what's trending, but what you in particular are likely to love. Swipe through, save what catches your eye, and mark what you've seen. Switch tabs for new releases or this week's pick. Every rating makes the next deck sharper.

A MATCH ON EVERY FILM
Every film carries your match: how well it fits your taste, before you press play. Tap it and Montage tells you why, in plain words, using films you've already rated. When something isn't for you, it says why, too, and what might still win you over.

FIND OUT WHAT KIND OF VIEWER YOU ARE
Rate films you've seen, and in a few minutes Montage tells you which of 15 kinds of viewer you are, with your Screen DNA: the specific corners of film you keep coming back to, and why you like what you like. Share your card when it lands.

DESCRIBE IT, GET IT
Pitch is for the film you can picture but can't name. Type something like "a slow-burn heist in the rain" or "space travel to Mars" and get a shortlist ranked by your match, with the best fit up top. Add a note like "shorter" or "older" and it reshuffles.

SEE WHOSE TASTE MATCHES YOURS
Compare tastes with anyone and see your Blend: how alike you are, the films you both love, and what's on both your watchlists. Start a Blend with a friend and get a fresh list of films you'd both love, updated as you both rate. Groups get a shared taste of their own, with films the whole group would love, a group watchlist and a chat.

MOVIE NIGHT, SETTLED
Start a movie night and drop one link in the group chat. Everyone picks a mood, Montage finds films that fit the whole room's taste, and you vote live until it locks in. Guests can join from the link without an account. While the vote is open, it shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Afterwards, everyone rates it and you see how the room felt.

TONIGHT
When you just need an answer: say who's watching, pick a mood, choose your services and how long you have, and get one film with your match on it. Not feeling it? Re-roll.

WHERE TO WATCH
Every film shows where it's streaming, to rent or to buy in your region, with the trailer one tap away. Playing in theaters? Get tickets in a tap at your theater chain, or through Fandango.
